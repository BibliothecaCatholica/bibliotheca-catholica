Athanasius of Alexandria (296–373)

Champion of Nicaea against Arianism; wrote On the Incarnation and the Life of Antony. Athanasius I of Alexandria, also called Athanasius the Great, Athanasius the Confessor, or, among Coptic Christians, Athanasius the Apostolic, was a Christian theologian and the 20th Patriarch of Alexandria. His intermittent episcopacy spanned 45 years.
