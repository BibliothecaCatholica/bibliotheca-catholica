Ignatius of Loyola (1491–1556)

Founder of the Jesuits, author of the Spiritual Exercises.

Ignatius of Loyola was a Spanish Basque Catholic priest and theologian, who, with six companions, founded the religious order of the Society of Jesus (Jesuits), and became its first Superior General, in Paris in 1541.
