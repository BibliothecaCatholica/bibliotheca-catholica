# Related Church teaching (data)

Job M1, November 23, 2026. This data maps works, authors, saints, and topics to Catechism paragraphs and magisterial documents on vatican.va. Each reference has a short neutral label: the Catechism's own heading for the paragraph, or a document's title, author, and year. A label is never a summary of doctrine.

- `catechism-index.json`: every paragraph (1–2865) of the official English Catechism on vatican.va, mapped to the page that holds it. Generated by `tools/teaching/teaching.py --index` from the IntraText pages under https://www.vatican.va/archive/ENG0015/. They were fetched on November 23, 2026: 375 pages, 367 with paragraphs.
- `subjects.json` (edited by hand): what to look for.
  - For a work, author, or saint: the citation form exactly as the Catechism prints it ("St. Augustine, Conf.", "STh ", "John Henry Cardinal Newman").
  - For a topic: a paragraph range and the heading that opens it.
  - Documents: vatican.va URLs, each with a phrase the page must contain.
- `entries.json`: generated by `tools/teaching/teaching.py --build`.
  - A Catechism reference is `verified` when its citation form is in that paragraph's footnotes or text on the official page.
  - A document is `verified` when its URL answered 200 and the page contains the expected title.
  - Anything else is `unverified` and is never published. On November 23 there were none.
  - Subjects with many matches keep at most 8 paragraphs, the first under each distinct heading. `catechismMatches` records the full count.

`python3 tools/teaching/teaching.py --check` validates the data offline, and `tests/test_teaching.py` runs it. It checks that:
- Catechism numbers are in range and on the linked page;
- every URL is on www.vatican.va;
- every work, author, saint, and topic slug exists;
- no work flagged in `data/editorial-flags.json` has an entry.

Requests go only to www.vatican.va. Magisterium AI is never queried, and no model answer is stored.
