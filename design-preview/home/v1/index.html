About the library

Bibliotheca Catholica (bibliothecacatholica.com) is an independent, free reading room for the Catholic tradition, with 129 public-domain editions to read here and 476 authors to explore. Every hosted text keeps its source, original notices, and rights evidence.

Bibliotheca Catholica (bibliothecacatholica.com) is an independent project and is not affiliated with other projects of a similar name.
