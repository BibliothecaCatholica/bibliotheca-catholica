Take up and read: two thousand years of Catholic books, free to read here.

Bibliotheca Catholica (bibliothecacatholica.com) is an independent, free reading room for the Catholic tradition, with 130 public-domain editions to read here and 476 authors to explore. Every hosted text keeps its source, original notices, and rights evidence.

  • Apologia pro vita suaJohn Henry NewmanNewman explains the development of his religious convictions and his passage from Anglicanism to the Catholic Church.
  • OrthodoxyG. K. ChestertonChesterton presents an imaginative defense of Christian belief through paradox, wonder, moral limits, and his own intellectual journey.

About this independent project

Bibliotheca Catholica (bibliothecacatholica.com) is an independent project and is not affiliated with other projects of a similar name.

Follow the project on X (@BiblioCatholica) and Bluesky (@bibliothecacatholica.com), or get the Newsletter on Substack. These are the accounts of this independent project, not official accounts of the Catholic Church.
