Design refresh, November 2026

Design previews

Four variations for each of five areas, built from the site’s own content and tokens. These pages are unlisted and not indexed; nothing here changes the live pages. The reasoning for each variation and a recommendation per area are in design-directions-2026-11.md.

Home page hero and Start here

  1. v1: Title page

    A centered book title page: label, Tolle Lege, ornament, one button, then the engraving as a frontispiece and Start here as a numbered contents list.

  2. v2: Facing pages

    An open-book spread: the engraving on the left, the title and a sentence about the library on the right, then Start here as rows with covers and descriptions.

  3. v3: Editor’s shelf

    Type only above the fold, with a facts line of counts; Start here becomes an annotated reading list; the engraving moves beside About as a small vignette.

  4. v4: Saints first

    A quiet banner with the engraving, then the eight portraits as the main gallery, each with the book to begin with.

Library and author index

  1. v1: Faceted catalogue

    A sticky facet column (subject or era, with counts) beside rows with small covers or portraits; the result count updates as you filter.

  2. v2: Printed shelf list

    Dense two-column lists grouped by subject or era, with a jump line and one plain filter bar; no descriptions, no images.

  3. v3: Cover shelf

    The design C covers as a wall of books, with subject tabs as underlined text; authors as a portrait index.

  4. v4: Index and chronology

    Works A to Z with a sticky letter bar; authors in a chronology with an era rail and dates in a hanging column.

Reading pages

  1. v1: Running head

    A slim sticky running head with the book, the chapter, a Contents menu, and arrows; a three-part pager; a centered colophon after “The End”.

  2. v2: Margin contents

    The contents in a sticky left margin with the current chapter marked; it folds into a disclosure on phones; the colophon is a ruled list.

  3. v3: Chapter strip

    A strip of chapter numerals under the opener; at the end, a Continue reading block with the next chapter’s first sentence; a book-style colophon.

  4. v4: Scholarly apparatus

    Paragraph numbers in the margin for citing, arrow-key chapter paging, an edition record table, and a Cite this edition line.

Sources and credits

  1. v1: Copyright-page ledger

    One column at reading measure; the summary table becomes a ledger of material, terms, and where; sections follow as plain prose.

  2. v2: Contents rail

    A sticky On this page list beside the text, including Rights and Takedown links; the summary stays a table, without the panel border.

  3. v3: Credits by source

    The terms ledger, then where the hosted texts come from (counts by source), then images, with the full note in an open disclosure.

  4. v4: Printed note

    A centered document with section numbers hung in the margin; the table stacks into labelled rows on phones.

Navigation and footer

  1. v1: Masthead

    A centered wordmark with the sections in serif small capitals on a ruled line below; a three-column footer.

  2. v2: Quiet bar

    One compact row: brand, sections, search, Support; the current section is underlined, not filled; a centered colophon footer.

  3. v3: Two tiers

    The seven sections plus a tinted row of links within the current section; the footer groups its seventeen links into Read, Explore, and Elsewhere.

  4. v4: Library directory

    A quiet bar in serif type; on phones the menu lists each section with its one-line description; the footer becomes a directory of every section.
