# Detailed project rules

Mandatory for relevant changes. Migrated without removing the existing rules on November 9, 2026. Paths in code spans are repository-root-relative. Read [AGENTS](../AGENTS.md) and [CENTRAL](../CENTRAL.md) first. Dated implementation descriptions are historical context; current code and explicit owner decisions govern current state.

## Content rules (non-negotiable)

1. **Public domain cutoff.** Host full texts only if they were published before 1931 (US public domain) and the edition's rights have been checked. Anything later is a link out.
2. **Gutenberg notice.** Every reader section of a Project Gutenberg text shows the Gutenberg license notice; downloaded texts keep the full original license.
3. **Reading room first.** "Read here" is the primary way to read. Isidore EPUB and PDF downloads are an alternate format ("Other formats"), not the headline, and appear only where the rights badge allows (rule 10).
4. **Editorial flags.** Fringe, conspiracy, and sedevacantist titles in the Isidore Index stay listed (removing them feeds the conspiracy), but each gets a calm, factual context note, the Dignitatis Humanae 1 and 3 disclaimer quoted verbatim from vatican.va, and a link to the Fidelity & Editorial Policy (`#/policy`, `/policy/`). Listing is not endorsement. Sedevacantist works are labeled as sedevacantist. Do not add Nostra Aetate quotes without the owner's approval.
5. **Flagged titles are never featured.** They must not appear on the home page, in reading lists, edition guides, reading options, reading-room matches, recommendations, the sitemap, or static pages. Flagged book pages carry `noindex`.
6. **Search defaults to "Everything"** (selected works rank first; the scopes are Everything, Selected library only, and Isidore Index only).
7. **Money.** Bookshop.org affiliate ID `130222` and the TAN Books referral link `https://lddy.no/1qbrw` only (`data/affiliates.js`; see "Affiliate links" above: book pages, edition guides, and TAN on classic Catholic works' authors, always below the free reading options and beside the disclosure). `bookshopShop` in `data/affiliates.js` is `https://bookshop.org/shop/bibliotheca-catholica` (confirmed by the owner on 2026-11-03; the URL was blank before because it returned 404 on 2026-10-25). It is used only for the "Our Bookshop.org shop" button on the author Buy tab (next to the affiliate disclosure) and the shop line on `#/support`; if it is ever blanked, both stay hidden. Never hard-code the URL elsewhere. `tests/test_affiliates.py` checks this. New Bookshop links beyond these and the book-page and edition-guide links built by `tools/build_print_editions.py` and `build_static.py` still need the owner's approval: the author-page hooks `RESEARCH_BOOKSHOP_BUTTONS` and `AUTHOR_GUIDE_BOOKSHOP_BUTTONS` in `tools/author_pages.py` stay `False`. No Amazon links. Donations are never described as tax-deductible (the library is a sole proprietorship, not a 501(c)(3)).
8. **Saints (v15).** CatholicSaints.Info is **link-only**: its robots.txt bars crawling and its terms forbid copying. Never copy its text, never fetch it at build or run time, and link it only where `data/saints.json` has a `catholicsaints` object, which `tools/import_saints_pilot.py` takes from the hand-checked tier 1 matches in `catholicsaints-mapping.json` (139 links). The link always comes after New Advent and Vatican News. No CatholicSaints.Info links for tier 2 or 3 saints (the 30 name-match-only candidates stay unpublished), and no outreach to its author. **Owner's decision (2026-11-09, 4:00 PM CST, for the full-year calendar):** no per-entry CatholicSaints.Info links anywhere; the calendar facts are credited by one Resources entry (References, "Saints & hagiography": "CatholicSaints.Info"). This rule is otherwise unchanged, and the 139 hand-checked tier 1 links stay as they were. Vatican News “Saint of the day” dates are always labelled as such, never as the General Roman Calendar (GRC dates stay `null` until checked against the Roman Missal). The tradition and legend saints (`historicity` flag) always show their flag. No biographies are written yet; profiles show only sourced fields. `tools/build_saints.py` and `tests/test_saints.py` enforce these rules.
9. **Style.** Use the Oxford comma. Keep notes neutral and sourced. Do not contact outside sites (isidore.co, CCEL, and others) on the owner's behalf; draft only.
10. **Rights (v16).** Every Isidore Index record and every reading-room edition shows one of five badges: **Public domain (US)** (`public_domain_us`), **Open license** (`open_license`), **Permission granted** (`permission_granted`), **Link only** (`link_only`), or **Unknown** (`unknown`), with its evidence and checked date (tooltip and record-page line), linking to `#/rights`.
   - **Download, Files, Calibre-viewer, and reader buttons appear ONLY for the first three.** Link-only and unknown records link only to the record's isidore.co **book page** (`legacy/book/<id>/CalibreLibrary`), never to raw files (`get/…`, `CalibreLibrary/<folder>/`, or the viewer). If `data/rights.js` fails to load, every record counts as unknown. The open data follows the same rule (`download_urls` is `[]` and `isidore_files_url` is `null` unless hostable).
   - **Be conservative.** The research classification (`hosting-classification.csv`, 2026-10-25) is the starting point: L* codes are link only; H*/U* codes, including every "likely hostable" lead, are **unknown**. A lead is never public domain on its own. The only automatic `public_domain_us` records (code `P1`) are the same Project Gutenberg ebook as a hosted, rights-checked reading-room edition, without a modern publisher's ISBN. Anything else becomes hostable only through a `MANUAL` entry in `tools/build_rights.py` with written evidence (URL, quotation, or CCE volume and page), a checked date, and who checked it. When in doubt, unknown. The 1931 rule and the editorial-flag rules still apply on top of the badge.
   - **No raw folder listings (v18).** `isidore.co/CalibreLibrary/<folder>/` is an "Index of" page that links every file whatever its badge, so nothing links to it: not author pages, not the hub resources, not the Index's Authors A–Z tab (`#/isidore/folders`, which now opens `#/isidore/<name>` Index searches where badges gate the links), not search results (type `Isidore Index author`), and not the open data (`isidore-folders` has `folder` as text and `index_url`; `authors` has `isidore_index_url`). The 211 author-page folder links were removed from `links` in v18; since v18.1 each of those authors gets an internal "All their books in the Isidore Index" link instead (`#/isidore/<catalog name>`, from `isidoreFolder` matched to `hub.isidore`; app Works tab "Search & data" and the static author page). Hub authors keep the folder name as data in `isidoreFolder` (used by `app.js`, `tools/build_static.py`, `tools/build_books.py`, and `tools/build_open_data.py`), and `hub.isidore` is `[{n, f}]` (name and folder text, no URL). Book pages (`legacy/book/<id>/CalibreLibrary`) are the only isidore.co record links for link-only and unknown records. The Index page, book pages, and `/rights` say that Bibliotheca Catholica does not host, and does not vouch for, any file at isidore.co. The nav label is "Isidore Index" (it was "Ebooks & PDFs"); the `/isidore/` path and `#/isidore` and `#/books` routes are unchanged. `tests/rights.test.js` and `tests/test_licensing.py` check this.
   - **Isidore author links find books (v18.2).** Folder names drop letters a file system dislikes ("Wojtyła" became "Wojtyla", "Søren" became "Soren", "1248?-1309" became "1248_-1309"), so a search for the folder name could find nothing. `python3 tools/build_isidore_names.py` (run it after `tools/build_books.py` refreshes `data/books.json`) stores `a`, the author name exactly as that folder's books carry it, on each `hub.isidore` entry where it differs from `n` (262 of 3,352 on 2026-10-26). Every author link (Authors A–Z, search results, author pages, static pages, and the open data) is `#/isidore/author=<a or n>`, which `books()` reads as that search with the "Catholic & related subjects only" filter off, since 446 of the A–Z names have only books that filter hides. The Index search itself also folds letters that do not decompose (ł, ø, đ, ð, þ, æ, œ, ß, ı, ħ) in `norm()` (and `scripts/search.js`), treats ? and _ like . (`isoNorm()`), and folds each book field on its own so initials never merge across fields. Plain `#/isidore/<text>` links still work as a normal search. `tests/isidore_links.test.js` runs the app's own search code and checks that all 211 author-page links and all 3,352 A–Z links find at least one book.
   - **Catalog name corrections (v18.3).** Catalog author names are shown as isidore.co supplies them, except for hand-checked factual errors listed in `tools/catalog_fixes.py` (each with its source). `tools/build_books.py` applies them to the book author strings in `data/books.json`, and `tools/build_isidore_names.py` applies them to `hub.isidore` `n`; the folder name (`isidoreFolder`, `f`, the open data `folder` column) stays as it is on isidore.co. The first entry corrects Gregory the Great's catalog dates from St Benedict's "(480-547)" to "(c. 540-604)". To rebuild `data/books.json`, run `python3 ../site/tools/build_books.py` from a sibling folder that holds isidore.co's Calibre `metadata.db`, then `python3 tools/build_isidore_names.py`, `python3 tools/build_open_data.py`, and `python3 tools/build_static.py`.
   - **No isidore.co catalog searches (v22, 2026-11-03).** isidore.co's mobile catalog (`isidore.co/calibre/mobile`, with or without `?search=`) lists raw `get/EPUB` and `get/PDF` files whatever a record's badge, so nothing links to it. The "Search the isidore.co catalog" link was removed from all 476 author records (`links` in `data/catholic-hub.json`, and `tools/build_data.py` no longer adds it), the "St. Isidore — simple (no-JS) catalog" resource was removed from Digital Libraries, and the three app links (the Isidore Index "same search on isidore.co", the book page "Search isidore.co for this title", and the search results "Search isidore.co’s full catalog") are gone; the results page links the internal Isidore Index search (`#/isidore/q=<term>`) instead. Authors with an Isidore folder keep "All their books in the Isidore Index". `tests/test_isidore_raw_links.py` fails if any served file links `calibre/mobile`, if any HTML page links raw files, folder listings, or the viewer, or if `get/` appears outside the two badge-gated download helpers (`dlUrl` in `app.js`, `isidoreDl` in `scripts/library.js`).
   - **Host nothing new** because of a badge. The badge permits links; hosting a file is a separate, owner-approved step (rule 1 and the Gutenberg pattern above). Never bulk-download isidore.co files.
   - `/rights` and `/takedown` both say "This is not legal advice." The takedown agent contact is a visible **PLACEHOLDER** until the owner decides on DMCA agent registration (US Copyright Office directory, $6 per 3 years); never fill in a guessed name or email. `tests/test_rights.py` and `tests/rights.test.js` enforce these rules.

Population layers (phase 3) and regional outlines or a public-domain reader (phase 4) are **not approved**: do not build them. `tests/test_rites.py` and `tests/rites.test.js` enforce these rules.


## Google Analytics (GA4)

- Measurement ID **`G-HFG445Y1LT`**. Every HTML content page must carry **exactly one** copy of the standard Google tag (gtag.js) snippet, placed immediately after `<head>`. The owner-supplied Search Console verification file `google3b007babfa8923a4.html` is a verification response, not a content page: retain its exact original bytes, add no analytics or HTML wrapper, and keep it published at the site root.
- The single source is `index.html`. `tools/build_static.py` copies it into every static page and into `404.html` (it fails if `index.html` has zero or two tags). Never paste the tag into generated pages by hand, never remove it, and never add a second tag, Google Tag Manager container, or another analytics script without the owner's approval.
- **Host gate (pages.dev mirror, 2026-11-09).** `BCAnalytics.allowedHost()` / `track()` are a no-op unless `location.hostname` is `bibliothecacatholica.com` or `www.bibliothecacatholica.com`. The inline `gtag('config', …)` in `index.html` is wrapped in the same check so the Cloudflare Pages preview (`*.pages.dev`) never configures the tag or sends events (it still loads the one gtag.js script; do not add a second tag). Keep exactly one GA4 measurement ID. Bump the `scripts/analytics.js?v=` cache token in `index.html` (and let `build_static.py` copy it) whenever the helper changes.
- **Page views (v14).** The config line is `gtag('config', 'G-HFG445Y1LT', {'send_page_view': false});`, so the tag sends no automatic page view. `app.js` `gaPageView()` sends exactly one `page_view` (`page_title`, `page_location`, `page_path`) for the first load (after the first `route()`, which also covers static entry pages through `window.BC_BOOT`) and one per later `hashchange`, debounced 400 ms and deduped by the raw URL. **v18:** `page_path` is the normalized route from `BCAnalytics.pagePath(location.hash, window.BC_BOOT)`, so one view logs one path whether it was served from `/` or a static page (`/work/x/#/author/y` and `/#/author/y` both give `/author/y/`). **Real paths (QA hit-level test `qa/pageview-path-test-2026-11-09.md`, owner-approved fix 2026-11-09):** the path is the site's real path form with a trailing slash (`/author/karl-adam/`, `/work/confessions/`, `/library/`, `/work/orthodoxy/ch/<NN-slug>/`, `/summa/1-2/q12/a3/`), never `/#/...`: GA4 sets Page path from the URL path of `page_location` and ignores the fragment, so the old v18 form `/#/author/y` counted every view as `/` (1,062 of 1,073 views over 90 days). On every static page it equals the path of its `<link rel=canonical>` (`tests/analytics.test.js` checks all of them). App-only routes use the same form even though no static file exists (`#/read/<work>/<edition>/<section>` gives `/read/<work>/<edition>/<section>/`, `#/saints/calendar/11-09` gives `/saints/calendar/11-09/`). Search and Isidore Index filter routes collapse to `/search/` and `/isidore/` (book pages keep `/isidore/book/N/`, and `/isidore/folders/`), so no search term or filter ever reaches a page view; a segment that is not a plain slug ends the path (at most six segments). `page_location` is `https://bibliothecacatholica.com` plus that path (never `location.href`), and the title of those two collapsed routes is fixed too. Expected hit on `/author/karl-adam/`: `dl=https://bibliothecacatholica.com/author/karl-adam/`, `dp=/author/karl-adam/`. Never go back to a `#` in `page_path` or `page_location`. **v18.1:** `track()` calls `gtag('set', {page_title, page_location})` with `BCAnalytics.context()` (the term-free `pageTitle()` and `pageLocation()` for the current route) before every event, so `search`, `outbound_click`, and GA's own events never fall back to a title or URL with free text, and `results()` sets `document.title` to the fixed `Search · Bibliotheca Catholica`. Never put a search term or Index filter in `document.title`. `404.html` carries the same config and no `app.js`, so it sends nothing; the `/#/...` page it redirects to counts the visit once. Do not remove `send_page_view: false`, and do not add a second page-view listener. The owner turns off GA4 enhanced measurement "page changes based on browser history events" in GA4 admin so hash changes are not counted twice. **Campaign tags (spec 2026-11-03):** the landing `page_view` only (the first one `gaPageView()` sends in a page load) appends to `page_location` the `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, and `utm_content` values from `location.search`, each trimmed and cut to 100 characters (`BCAnalytics.utmQuery()`). `utm_term`, `gclid`, `lookup`, `q`, and every other parameter are dropped; later page views and the `gtag('set')` context carry the bare route. **Tags before the `#` (QA 2026-11-03, owner-approved fix 2026-11-09):** GA4 reads campaign tags only from the query string of `page_location`, so `pageLocation()` always writes them in the query string after the path (`https://bibliothecacatholica.com/work/y/?utm_source=x`; a path that still held a `#` would get them before it, never `/#/work/y?utm_source=x`). Links that put the tags after the `#` still count: on the landing view `BCAnalytics.landingSearch(location.search, location.hash)` adds the hash's utm_ tags (a tag in `location.search` wins; the same four, each cut to 100 characters), the same `page_view` carries them (no new event), and `BCAnalytics.stripHashUtm()` then removes every utm_ pair from the hash with `history.replaceState` (other hash parameters stay; `gaLast` follows so the view is not sent twice). Tested in `tests/analytics.test.js`. **Built-in measurements:** the owner switched off GA4 enhanced measurement "Outbound clicks" and "Form interactions" on 2026-11-03, so GA's own `click` (with `link_url`), `form_start`, and `form_submit` events are no longer sent; the Privacy section says they are off. Keep them off: "Outbound clicks" would send full outside URLs, which for isidore.co and vatican.va search links include search terms.
- **Tagged links (for every bot, post, newsletter, and doc):** put the UTM query before the `#`: `https://bibliothecacatholica.com/?utm_source=x&utm_medium=social#/library`, or on a static page `https://bibliothecacatholica.com/work/orthodoxy/?utm_source=x&utm_medium=social`. Never `https://bibliothecacatholica.com/#/library?utm_source=x`. Use only `utm_source`, `utm_medium`, `utm_campaign`, and `utm_content` (`utm_term` is dropped). Build them with `BCAnalytics.campaignUrl(route, tags)`, for example `node -e "console.log(require('./scripts/analytics.js').campaignUrl('#/library',{utm_source:'x',utm_medium:'social'}))"`.
- **Events (v14).** Every event goes through the helper `window.BCAnalytics.track(name, params)` in `scripts/analytics.js` (loaded once, after `data/affiliates.js` and before `scripts/library.js` and `app.js`). It checks `allowedHost()` first (production hostname only), then `typeof gtag==='function'`, wraps everything in `try/catch`, accepts only the event names below, drops empty values, and caps string params at 100 characters. `app.js` and `scripts/library.js` call it through a local `track()` wrapper; nothing else may call `gtag(` or touch `dataLayer`. GA4's own enhanced-measurement outbound `click`, `form_start`, and `form_submit` events are switched off (2026-11-03); if they are ever switched on again, they come from the tag itself, not our code, and the Privacy section must say so.

## Files that must never be deleted or moved

**Document archive:** `BibliothecaCatholica/bibliotheca-texts` serves the 64 verified TXT copies (pg-70175, The Secret of Father Brown, and the four Summa volumes pg-17611, pg-17897, pg-18755, pg-19950, added 2026-11-09; the Supplement transcription ia-summa-supplement, added 2026-11-09; On Christian Doctrine ccel-npnf102-doctrine, Shaw / NPNF1-02, added 2026-11-09; and The Incredulity of Father Brown se-incredulity-father-brown, added 2026-11-09; Orthodoxy moved from pg-16769 to pg-130 the same day) at `https://texts.bibliothecacatholica.com/texts/`. The main reader prefers that host and falls back to local `data/texts/`. Keep local originals and provenance. `textArchive` in `data/library.json` controls this behavior. Export offline with `python3 tools/build_text_archive.py --output /absolute/path/to/bibliotheca-texts`; review the manifest and checksums before publishing the separate repository. Do not bulk mirror unreviewed imported books. See `docs/PROJECT-REVIEW-2026-10-22.md`.

**Link-only editions (v14 pattern):** a public-domain scan or text that is not on the validated Gutenberg path (Internet Archive, Fordham, and similar) is an edition with `access: "full-text"` and a `source` URL but no `text`, like the New Advent and Vatican editions. Its card says "Full text at source" and it is never downloaded or mirrored. Leave out `added` on link-only works so **Recently added** ("New in the reading room") lists only texts readable here.

**Adding a hosted Gutenberg text (v10 pattern):**
1. Check the specific edition or translation year in the Gutenberg front matter (pre-1931 only; record the evidence in the edition `note`).
2. `python3 tools/fetch_gutenberg_edition.py <PG number> ...` downloads the original `pgN.txt` bytes to `data/texts/pg-N.txt`, snapshots the catalog record to `data/sources/pg-N.json`, and prints the SHA-256. It never edits `data/library.json` and skips releases with no plain-text file.
3. Add the work and edition to `data/library.json` by hand (`label`, `rights`, `checked`, `note`, `sourceTitle`, `text`, `sha256`), with `"added": "YYYY-MM-DD"`. The `added` date drives the **Recently added** section on Discover & Read and a "New" card badge for 90 days.
4. `python3 tools/fetch_librivox.py` rebuilds `data/librivox.json/.js` and the per-recording chapter lists in `data/librivox/<id>.json` that power the reader's persistent **Listen** dock. Use the work's `audioTitles` array for LibriVox title aliases; leave out a recording that is clearly a different work or an abridgment by listing its LibriVox id in the work's `audioExclude` array (for example, Butler's one-volume *Lives of the Saints with Reflections*, #5324, is excluded from the full Kenedy volumes).
5. `npm run build`, update test counts, then re-export and push `bibliotheca-texts` (step above) so the text is served from the texts subdomain.
