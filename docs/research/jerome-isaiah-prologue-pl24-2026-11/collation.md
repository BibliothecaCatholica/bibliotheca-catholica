# Jerome, Commentary on Isaiah, prologue: PL 24 collation (21 November 2026)

Latin only. The English word gate is not used.

## Sources

- Clean text: Corpus Corporum, University of Zurich, idno 7170, section 7170:1 (Prologus). Display: https://mlat.uzh.ch/php_modules/display_text.php?ajax=true&path=7170:1 . Work page: https://mlat.uzh.ch/?text=7170
- Cross-check: Latin Wikisource raw page, Commentaria in Isaiam (Hieronymus)/pro, which itself names Migne 1845 and Corpus Corporum as its source.
- Witness: Internet Archive item PatrologiaLatina, file Patrologia Latina Vol. 24.pdf (the Harvard scan of Migne, Patrologiae tomus XXIV, Paris: Petit-Montrouge, 1845). Title page dated 1845. Prologue at printed columns 17-22, PDF pages 13-15.

## Method

The Corpus Corporum / Wikisource wording of the prologue was read word for word against the 1845 page images. Ligatures were expanded to ae, and punctuation spacing was normalized, following Corpus Corporum. In-text scripture citations were kept as Migne prints them. No wording differences were found (agreement 1.0). The editor's preface that precedes the title, Liber I, and the Maurist/Martianay footnotes were not hosted.
