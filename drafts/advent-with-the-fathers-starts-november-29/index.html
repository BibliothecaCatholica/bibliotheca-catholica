Advent with the Fathers starts November 29

Twenty-six free daily readings, and a book to begin with

Dear friends,

Advent begins on Sunday, November 29. For the twenty-six days that follow, through Christmas Eve, Bibliotheca Catholica will send one short reading each morning from the Fathers and other public-domain voices in our library: Clement of Rome, Augustine, Catherine of Siena, Thomas à Kempis, Bede, Newman, Thérèse, and more. Each note is brief. Each one links back to the full text on the site, so you can read as little or as much as the day allows.

The plan is already live on the library:

The Advent 2026 reading plan: https://bibliothecacatholica.com/reading/advent-2026/

If you are not yet subscribed here, this is the place to be for those daily notes. They are free, and they always will be. The first weeks look toward Christ's coming at the end of time; from December 17 the readings follow the O Antiphons toward Christmas.

Featured book: Confessions

Among the Advent books, Augustine's Confessions opens the season with a longing many of us know too well in December: a heart that will not settle. Day 3 of the plan (Tuesday, December 1) begins at the famous opening of Book I. You need not wait for that morning. The whole work is free to read on the site now, in the Pusey translation we host.

Great art Thou, O Lord, and greatly to be praised; great is Thy power, and Thy wisdom infinite. … Thou awakest us to delight in Thy praise; for Thou madest us for Thyself, and our heart is restless, until it repose in Thee. (St. Augustine, Confessions, Book I, ch. 1; trans. E. B. Pusey (Project Gutenberg #3296; hosted on the site))

That restlessness is not a December problem to fix with more activity. It is the ache of a heart made for God. The Child we wait for is the answer Augustine names: faith given "through the Incarnation of Thy Son."

What to expect

  • Daily, Nov 29 – Dec 24: one short excerpt, a plain reflection, and links to the day's passage and the work page.
  • After Christmas: a weekly From the Library digest on Saturday mornings, with Book of the Week on Wednesdays (web only), then a January Read-Along in Orthodoxy, and the Confessions again for Lent (Ash Wednesday is February 10, 2027).

Save the Advent plan, subscribe if you have not, and, if you like, open Book I of the Confessions before the first candle is lit. We will walk the rest of the way together.
