From the Library: The week of December 14

How do we know what happened?

Dear friends,

The faith rests on events that really happened. This week we looked at how Christians have weighed the evidence for them, and for the many stories that grew up around them.

Essay · How Do We Know What Happened? Eusebius walking "a lonely and untrodden path," Bede naming his witnesses, and Hippolyte Delehaye on the difference between hagiography and history. [LINK: essay]

Book of the Week · Bede's Ecclesiastical History of the English People How the faith came to England, told by a monk who listed his sources. [LINK]

Exploration · Is a Saint's Legend the Same as History? "Legend" first meant the reading for a saint's feast. How to read saints' lives without being either credulous or cynical. [LINK]

Exploration · How Did Bede Know? Reading Bede's preface, and one chapter, for the way he tells us where each story came from. [LINK]
