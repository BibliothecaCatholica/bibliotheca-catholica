From the Library: The week of November 30

Augustine and the pagans, plus eight Catholic classics to give

Dear friends,

Welcome to the first weekly digest from Bibliotheca Catholica. Each Saturday we'll gather the week's essays and short pieces in one place, so you can read them at your own pace.

This week's question is an old one: should a Christian read books by people who don't share the faith?

Exploration · Does Reading an Author Mean Endorsing Him? St. Paul quoted a pagan poet, and St. Thomas explains why that was no compliment to paganism. [LINK]
