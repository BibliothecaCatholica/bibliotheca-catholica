Notes · historical evidence · Substack Note

"[S]ince I am the first to enter upon the subject, I am attempting to traverse as it were a lonely and untrodden path."

— Eusebius, Church History I.1

The first historian of the Church knew he had no map. He gathered fragments, which he said rise "from afar... like torches." Much of what we know about the first Christian centuries survives because he copied it.
