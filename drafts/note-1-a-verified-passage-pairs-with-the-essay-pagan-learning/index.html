Notes · pagan learning · Substack Note

"[L]et every good and true Christian understand that wherever truth may be found, it belongs to his Master."

— St. Augustine, On Christian Doctrine II.18

Augustine wrote this to future teachers of the Church. It is still the best one-line answer I know to "should a Christian read books by non-Christians?" This week's essay looks at why.
