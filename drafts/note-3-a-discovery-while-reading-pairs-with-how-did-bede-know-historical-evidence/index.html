Note 3: a discovery while reading (pairs with "How Did Bede Know?")

Notes · historical evidence · Substack Note

In one short chapter of Bede (III.2) you can find three kinds of evidence. There's "we are told" for an old report. There's "the place is shown to this day" for something readers could visit. And there's a healed man named Bothelm, "who is still living." Bede wanted to be checked.
