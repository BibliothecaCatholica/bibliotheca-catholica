Note 3: an observation (pairs with "Does Reading an Author Mean Endorsing Him?")

Notes · pagan learning · Substack Note

St. Thomas ranks his sources plainly: philosophers as "extrinsic and probable arguments," the doctors of the Church "merely as probable," and the canonical Scriptures as "an incontrovertible proof." Even a saint's opinion is weighed, not simply obeyed. That is a freeing thing to know when you open an old book.

Summa I, q. 1, a. 8, in the library: https://bibliothecacatholica.com/summa/1/q1/a8/
