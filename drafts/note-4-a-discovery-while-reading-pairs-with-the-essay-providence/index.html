Notes · providence · Substack Note

Why does God work through creatures at all, when He could do everything Himself? St. Thomas's answer: "not on account of any defect in His power, but by reason of the abundance of His goodness; so that the dignity of causality is imparted even to creatures." Our choices are part of His generosity.

Summa I, q. 22, a. 3: https://bibliothecacatholica.com/summa/1/q22/a3/
