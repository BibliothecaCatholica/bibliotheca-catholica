Note 4: an observation (pairs with "Is a Saint's Legend the Same as History?")

Notes · historical evidence · Substack Note

"Legend" didn't originally mean "made up." The Jesuit scholar Hippolyte Delehaye explains that the legend "is the history that has to be read, legenda, on the feast of a saint." It was the day's reading, without any claim about how historical it was.
