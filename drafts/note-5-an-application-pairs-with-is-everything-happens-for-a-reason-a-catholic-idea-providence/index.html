Note 5: an application (pairs with "Is 'Everything Happens for a Reason' a Catholic Idea?")

Notes · providence · Substack Note

Instead of "everything happens for a reason," try: nothing is beyond God's care, and He can bring good even out of this. That's St. Augustine's point in the Enchiridion: God would never permit evil "if He were not so omnipotent and good that He can bring good even out of evil."
