The Lord of the Rings

J. R. R. Tolkien. “A fundamentally religious and Catholic work” (Tolkien, Letter 142).

  1. One volume: 50th Anniversary one-volume edition (Houghton Mifflin Harcourt)
  2. Tolkien on his faith: The Letters of J. R. R. Tolkien, Revised and Expanded (HarperCollins)
  3. The Silmarillion: The Silmarillion (Houghton Mifflin Harcourt)

Also good: Holly Ordway’s Tolkien’s Faith (Word on Fire) is the best study of his Catholicism.
