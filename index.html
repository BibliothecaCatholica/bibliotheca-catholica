About the library

A Catholic reading room and resource index

Bibliotheca Catholica (bibliothecacatholica.com) gathers Catholic books and the libraries that preserve them. The reading room offers 64 editions to read here for free. Each keeps its source, original notices, and rights evidence. Other works open at their original libraries or publishers.

Explore 476 authors, compare translations, or browse the Isidore Index of 6,633 e-books. Listing is not endorsement; our editorial policy explains the context notes. Bibliotheca Catholica is independent, free for everyone, and has no ads.
