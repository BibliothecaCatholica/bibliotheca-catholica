Podcasts & Media

The major Catholic podcasts, YouTube channels, radio and TV networks, and news outlets.

  • The Bible in a Year (Fr. Mike Schmitz) — Read the whole Bible in 365 daily episodes with Fr. Mike Schmitz and Jeff Cavins.
  • The Catechism in a Year (Fr. Mike Schmitz) — The entire Catechism in 365 daily episodes.
  • Catholic Answers Live — Daily call-in apologetics radio show.
  • Pints with Aquinas (Matt Fradd) — Interviews and debates on philosophy and the Faith.
  • Word on Fire Show (Bishop Barron) — Bishop Barron and Brandon Vogt on faith and culture.
  • The Thomistic Institute podcast — Lectures from leading Catholic scholars.
  • Godsplaining (Dominican friars) — Dominican friars of the Province of St. Joseph discuss the Faith.
  • Catholic Stuff You Should Know — Young priests on doctrine, history, and culture.
  • The Catholic Talk Show — Three priests and a layman on culture and the Faith.
  • Abiding Together — Women's spirituality podcast.
  • Jimmy Akin's Mysterious World — Jimmy Akin and Dom Bettinelli explore the mysterious with a Catholic lens.
  • The Jimmy Akin Podcast — Apologist Jimmy Akin on doctrine and Scripture.
  • Counsel of Trent (Trent Horn) — Catholic Answers apologist Trent Horn on apologetics and ethics.
  • Catholic Culture Podcast — Interviews on Catholic art, literature, and intellectual life.
  • The Pillar Podcast — Church news analysis from The Pillar.
  • Institute of Catholic Culture podcast — Free lectures on theology, history, and Scripture.
  • The Journey Home (EWTN) — Marcus Grodi interviews converts and reverts.
  • Dr. Brant Pitre — Catholic Productions — Bible studies and talks by Dr. Brant Pitre.
  • Reason & Theology (Michael Lofton) — News and theology discussion show.
  • EWTN — Global Catholic Network founded by Mother Angelica: TV, radio, and news.
  • Relevant Radio — Catholic talk radio network.
  • Radio Maria — Worldwide Catholic radio family.
  • Ave Maria Radio — Catholic radio, home of Kresta in the Afternoon.
  • Bishop Robert Barron — Sermons, commentaries, and Word on Fire media.
  • Ascension Presents — Fr. Mike Schmitz and friends; short catechetical videos.
  • Pints With Aquinas — Matt Fradd's interviews and debates.
  • Catholic Answers — Apologetics clips and Catholic Answers Live.
  • The Thomistic Institute — Lectures in philosophy and theology.
  • Dr. Taylor Marshall — Church history, news, and Thomism from Taylor Marshall.
  • EWTN — EWTN programming and shows.
  • Breaking In The Habit (Fr. Casey Cole, OFM) — A Franciscan friar on faith and religious life.
  • Franciscan Friars of the Renewal — Talks and life of the CFR friars.
  • The Counsel of Trent — Trent Horn's apologetics channel.
  • Catholic Productions (Brant Pitre) — Bible studies from Dr. Brant Pitre.
  • Sensus Fidelium — Traditional sermons and talks.
  • Institute of Catholic Culture — Free lectures in theology and history.
  • The Catholic Talk Show — Priests and laymen on culture.
  • Reason & Theology — Michael Lofton's news and theology show.
  • Vatican News — Official video from the Holy See.
  • Marian Fathers — Divine Mercy (Fr. Chris Alar) — Marian Fathers of the Immaculate Conception: Divine Mercy teaching.
  • Scott Hahn — St. Paul Center — Bible teaching from Scott Hahn and the St. Paul Center.
  • Godsplaining — Dominican friars of the Province of St. Joseph.
  • Enlightenment, Ltd. — Short, beautifully made films on Catholic history: saints, medieval monks, manuscripts, and popes, from a team of filmmakers and amateur historians.
  • Catholic News Agency — EWTN-owned Catholic news wire.
  • National Catholic Register — EWTN's Catholic newspaper.
  • The Pillar — Investigative Catholic journalism.
  • Catholic World Report — Ignatius Press news magazine.
  • First Things — Ecumenical journal of religion and public life.
  • Crisis Magazine — Catholic commentary magazine.
  • The Catholic Thing — Daily Catholic commentary.
  • Aleteia — Catholic news and lifestyle in many languages.
  • America Magazine — Jesuit weekly.
  • National Catholic Reporter — Independent Catholic news.
  • Catholic Herald — UK Catholic magazine.
  • OSV News — Catholic news service from Our Sunday Visitor.
  • Crux — Independent news on the Vatican and the Church worldwide.
  • Catholic World News — Daily headline digest from CatholicCulture.org.
  • ZENIT — International news agency covering the Pope and the Holy See.
  • Agenzia Fides — News agency of the Pontifical Mission Societies, focused on the missions.
  • The Tablet — London Catholic weekly, founded in 1840.
