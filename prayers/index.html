Prayers & Devotions

The common prayers of the Church, the Rosary, daily readings, the saint of the day, prayer apps, and parish finders.

The common prayers and devotions of the Church, with links to daily readings and prayer resources.

  • USCCB Daily Readings — Today’s U.S. Mass readings from the approved lectionary, with audio and reflections.
  • Universalis — Today's Mass readings and the complete Liturgy of the Hours.
  • Daily Gospel (Evangelizo) — The day's Gospel with a commentary from the Fathers and saints, by email or online.
  • Vatican News: Word of the Day — The day's readings with words of the Pope and the saints.
  • Vatican News: Saint of the Day — Saints of the Roman calendar, day by day.
  • Franciscan Media: Saint of the Day — A short daily saint biography with a reflection.
  • GCatholic liturgical calendar — Feasts, colors, and celebrations for the universal and local calendars.
  • Catholic Culture: Liturgical Year today — Today's feast with prayers, activities, and recipes for the home.
  • USCCB Catholic Prayers — The bishops' collection of basic prayers, devotions, and novenas.
  • EWTN Prayers & Devotions — Rosary, novenas, litanies, and chaplets.
  • Catholic Culture: Prayers for the liturgical year — Thousands of prayers searchable by season, feast, and theme.
  • Thesaurus Precum Latinarum — The great Latin prayers, litanies, and hymns, with English translations.
  • The Mysteries of the Rosary (vatican.va) — The twenty mysteries, with Scripture for each.
  • Rosarium Virginis Mariae — St. John Paul II's letter on the Rosary, which added the Luminous Mysteries.
  • How to Pray the Rosary (USCCB) — Step-by-step guide from the U.S. bishops.
  • The Divine Mercy Chaplet — How to pray the chaplet given to St. Faustina, from the Marian Fathers.
  • Hallow — Catholic prayer and meditation app: Rosary, Lectio, sleep stories, and Bible in a Year.
  • Laudate (iOS) — Free all-in-one app: readings, Liturgy of the Hours, Rosary, prayers, and the Catechism.
  • Laudate (Android) — The same free Laudate app for Android.
  • iBreviary — Breviary, missal, and prayers in many languages, free on every platform.
  • Universalis apps — The Liturgy of the Hours and Mass readings offline on phone and desktop.
  • Divine Office — Liturgy of the Hours with audio, on the web and in its app.
  • Magnificat — Monthly prayer missal in print and app: readings, prayers, and meditations.
  • FORMED — Augustine Institute streaming library of films, studies, and audiobooks; many parishes give free access.
  • MassTimes.org — Find Mass and confession times worldwide.
  • GCatholic churches — Dioceses, cathedrals, shrines, and churches worldwide.
  • Catholic-Hierarchy.org — Find any diocese and its bishop.
  • VISION Vocation Network — Search hundreds of religious communities and discern a vocation.
