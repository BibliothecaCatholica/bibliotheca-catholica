Saints of 1 January

63 saints, blessed, and feasts kept on 1 January, with short facts.

Feasts (2)

  • Feast of the Circumcision of the Lord Also called In Circumcisione Domini et Octav Nativitatis.
  • Mary, Mother of God

Saints (42)

  • St. Fulgentius of Ruspe Born: c.465 at Carthage, North Africa (modern Tunis, Tunisia). Died: 1 January 533 in Ruspe. Also called Fabius Claudius Gordianus Fulgentius.
  • St. Baglan of Wales Also called Faglan and Breton Missionaries to Britain.
  • St. Basil of Aix Died: 521.
  • St. Basilius of Ancyra Died: torn to pieces by lions in the arena in Caesarea in 362.
  • St. Brogan
  • St. Buonfiglio Monaldi Died: 1 January 1261. Beatified: 1 December 1717 by Pope Clement XI (cultus confirmed). Canonized: 15 January 1888 by Pope Leo XIII. Also called Bonfilio Monaldo.
  • St. Clarus of Vallis Regia Died: 1043. Also called Chiaro.
  • St. Clarus of Vienne Born: c.590 in Vienne, Dauphine, France. Died: c.660. Beatified: 9 December 1903 by Pope Saint Pius X (cultus confirmed). Patronage: tailors. Also called Clair of Vienne and Clair du Dauphiné.
  • St. Colman mac Rónán Also called Colmanus.
  • St. Colman Muillin of Derrykeighan Also called Colman of Doire Caocháin, Colman Moldendarius, Colman Miller, and Colmanus....
  • St. Concordius of Arles Died: c.343. Also called Concorde.
  • St. Concordius of Spoleto Died: beheaded c.175 in a prison cell in Spoleto, Italy. Also called Concord, Concorde, Memorial.
  • St. Concordius of Tivoli Born: Rome, Italy. Died: beheaded in 175 at Tivoli, Italy. Also called Concordio.
  • St. Connat Died: c.590. Also called Comnatan.
  • St. Cuan Died: 6th century. Also called Claunus, Mochua, Moncan, Moncain.
  • St. Demet of Plozévet Also called Demet of Plozeved, Demat, Dervel, Devet, Tevet, Zevet, and Breton Missionaries to Britain.
  • St. Elvan Born: 2nd century Britanny (part of modern France). Also called Eluan, Elvanus.
  • St. Eugendus of Condat Born: c.449 at Izernore, Ain, France. Died: 1 January 510 at Condat, Switzerland. Patronage: Saint-Oyen, Tarentaise, France, Saint-Oyen, Valley of Aoste, Italy, and Saint-Oyens, Vaud, Switzerland.
  • St. Euphrosyne of Alexandria Died: 470. Also called Euphrosyna, Smaragdus.
  • St. Fanchea of Rossory Born: at Clogher, Ireland. Died: c.585. Also called Faenche, Fainche, Faine, Garbh, Garbhp.
  • St. Felix of Bourges Died: c.580.
  • St. Fintan of Myshall Born: Ireland. Also called Fintan of Midhíseal and Fintanus, Fiontan.
  • St. Frodobert of Troyes Born: c.600 in Troyes, France. Died: c.670 in Troyes, Neustria (in modern France). Also called Frodobert of Moutier-la-Celle, Frodobert of Luxeuil, and Frobert, Frodoberto.
  • St. Gregory Nazianzen the Elder Born: c.276 at Nazianzos, Cappadocia, Asia Minor. Died: 374.
  • St. Joseph Mary Tomasi Born: 12 September 1649 at Licata. Died: 1 January 1713 at home next to the church of San Lorenzo in Panisperna, Rome, Italy. Canonized: 12 October 1986 by Pope John Paul II.
  • St. Justin of Chieti Died: c.540. Patronage: Chieti, Italy.
  • St. Maelrhys Born: 6th century Brittany (part of modern France).
  • St. Magnus the Martyr
  • St. Mydwyn Born: 2nd century Britanny (part of modern France). Also called Meduin, Medwin, Medwy.
  • St. Odilo of Cluny Born: 962 at Auvergne, France. Died: 1 January 1049 at Souvigny, France. Patronage: against jaundice and souls in Purgatory. Also called Archangel of Monks and Olon, Odilon.
  • St. Peter of Atroa Born: 773 near Ephesus, Asia Minor (modern Turkey) as Theophylact. Died: 1 January 837 at Atroa. Also called Pierre d'Atroa and Theophylact.
  • St. Peter of Temissis Born: Pelopon, Greece. Died: hanged in 1776 in Temissis, Asia Minor (in modern Turkey).
  • St. Sciath of Ardskeagh Died: 6th-century Ireland. Also called Scéithe, Scethe, Scetthe, Skay.
  • St. Severino Gallo Born: France. Died: tortured then nailed to a pole and left to die.
  • St. Telemachus Also called Almachio, Almachius, Almachus.
  • St. Thaumastus of Mainz Died: 5th century Poitiers, France. Patronage: against colic in children, against fever, and against toothache. Also called Thaumastus of Poitiers and Theomastus of....
  • St. Theodotus Died: beheaded.
  • St. Tyfrydog Born: Welsh. Also called Tyvrydog.
  • St. Vincent Strambi Born: 1 January 1745 at Civitavecchia, Italy. Died: 1 January 1824 in Rome, Italy. Canonized: 11 June 1950 by Pope Pius XII. Also called Vincenzo Maria Strambi.
  • St. William of Dijon Born: 962 in the family castle on San Giuglio Island, Lake Orta, Novara, Piedmont. Died: 1 January 1031 at Fecamp monastery, Normandy, France. Also called William of Cluny, William of Saint Benignus, William of Volpiano, and Guglielmo, Guillaume.
  • St. Zedislava Berka Born: c.1220 in the diocese in Križanov, Ždár nad Sázavou, Vysocina kraj (modern Czech Republic). Died: 1 January 1252 at Jablonné, Ceská Lípa, Liberecký kraj (modern Czech Republic). Beatified: 28 August 1907 by Pope Pius X (cultus confirmed). Canonized: 21 May 1995 by Pope John Paul II.
  • St. Zygmunt Gorazdowski Born: 1 November 1845 at Sanok, Podkarpackie, Poland. Died: 1 January 1920 at Lviv, L'vivs'ka oblast', Ukraine. Canonized: 23 October 2005 by Pope Benedict XVI at Rome, Italy. Also called Zigmund Horazdowski.

Blessed (14)

  • Blessed Adalbero of Liège Born: 1070. Died: 1 January 1128. Also called Adalbero of Louvain and Alberon....
  • Blessed Andrés Gómez Sáez Born: 7 May 1894 in Bicorp, Valencia, Spain. Died: shot on 1 January 1937 in Santander, Cantabria, Spain. Beatified: 28 October 2007 by Pope Benedict XVI.
  • Blessed Beatrice of Amptenhausen Died: 1 January 1111.
  • Blessed Bonannus of Roio Died: c.1320.
  • Blessed Catherine de Solaguti
  • Blessed Gisela of Rosstreppe Died: late 9th century in Harz, Germany.
  • Blessed Hugolinus of Gualdo Cattaneo Born: c.1200 in Gualdo Cattaneo, Italy. Died: 1 January 1260 in Gualdo Cattaneo, Italy. Beatified: 12 March 1919 by Pope Benedict XV (cultus confirmed). Patronage: Gualdo Cattaneo, Italy. Also called Hugo Linus a Gualdo Captaneorum, Hugo Linus of Gualdo, and Ugolino de Gualdo.
  • Blessed Jean-Baptiste Lego Born: 13 May 1766 in La Flèche, Sarthe, France. Died: 1 January 1794 at Angers, Maine-et-Loire, France. Beatified: 19 February 1984 by Pope John Paul II at Rome, Italy.
  • Blessed Jean of Saint-Just-en-Chaussée Born: c.1100 in France. Died: 1160.
  • Blessed Lojze Grozde Born: 27 May 1923 in Trzisce, Zgornje Vodale, Slovenia. Died: tortured to death on 1 January 1943 in Mirna, Trebnje, Slovenia. Beatified: 13 June 2010 by Pope Benedict XVI. Also called Alojzij Grozde.
  • Blessed Marian Konopinski Poznan Born: 10 September 1907 in Kluczewo, Wielkopolskie, Poland. Died: 1 January 1943 in the concentration camp at Dachau, Oberbayern, Germany. Beatified: 13 June 1999 by Pope John Paul II.
  • Blessed Odilo of Stavelot Died: 954.
  • Blessed René Lego Born: 5 October 1764 in La Flèche, Sarthe, France. Died: 1 January 1794 at Angers, Maine-et-Loire, France. Beatified: 19 February 1984 by Pope John Paul II at Rome, Italy. Also called Renatus Lego.
  • Blessed Valentin Paquay Born: 17 November 1828 in Tongres, Limburg, Belgium as Louis Paquay. Died: 1 January 1905 in Hasselt, Limburg, Belgium. Beatified: 9 November 2003 by Pope John Paul II. Also called Jan Louis Paquay, Joannes Ludovicus Paquay, Valentijn Paquay, Valentine Paquay, and Valentinus Paquay.

Martyr groups (2)

  • Martyrs of Africa
