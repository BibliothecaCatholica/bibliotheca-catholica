Saints of 9 February

34 saints, blessed, and feasts kept on 9 February, with short facts.

Marian titles (1)

  • Our Lady of the Bells

Saints (22)

  • St. Apollonia Died: burned to death c.249 at Alexandria, Egypt. Patronage: against tooth disease, against toothache, dental technicians, dentists, and 3 cities. Also called Apolline of Alexandria.
  • St. Alexander of Rome Died: in Rome, Italy.
  • St. Alexander of Soli
  • St. Alto of Altomünster Born: in Ireland. Died: c.760 in Bavaria, Germany. Also called Alto of Ireland and Alton....
  • St. Ammonius of Soli
  • St. Ansbert of Rouen Born: at Chaussy-sur-Epte, France. Died: 695 at Hainaut (part of modern Belgium). Also called Ansbert of Fontenelle and Ansberto, Aubert.
  • St. Auedebertus of Senlis Born: early 7th century in Senlis, France. Died: late 7th century. Also called Auedeberto, Ausberto, Autberto, Autbertus.
  • St. Brachio of Auvergne Born: Touraine, France. Died: 576 in Auvergne, France. Also called Braque.
  • St. Cuaran the Wise Died: c.700. Also called Curvinus, Cronan.
  • St. Donatus the Deacon Died: 362 in Lavallum, North Africa.
  • St. Eingan of Llanengan Also called Anianus, Einion, Eneon, Eingan, Einganor.
  • St. Maro Born: 350 in Syria. Died: 433. Patronage: Maronite Christians, Saint Maron of Brooklyn for the Maronites, eparchy of, Saint-Maron de Montréal, Québec, diocese of, and Volperino, Italy. Also called Maro of Beit-Marun and Maron, Marone, Maroun.
  • St. Miguel Febres Cordero Muñoz Born: 7 November 1854 at Cuenca, Ecuador. Died: 9 February 1910. Canonized: 21 October 1984 by Pope John Paul II.
  • St. Nebridius of Ègara Born: Girona, Spain. Died: c.545. Also called Nebridi, Nebridio.
  • St. Nicephorus of Antioch Died: c.260 at Antioch, Syria. Also called Niceforus.
  • St. Primus the Deacon Died: 362 in Lavallum, North Africa.
  • St. Raynald of Nocera Born: c.1150. Died: 9 February 1217 in Nocera, Umbria, Italy. Beatified: popular devotion and reports of miracles begin soon after the death of Raynald. Patronage: Assisi-Nocera Umbra-Gualdo Tadino, Italy, diocese of and Nocera, Italy. Also called Rinaldo.
  • St. Romanus the Wonder Worker
  • St. Ronan of Lismore
  • St. Sabino of Abellinum Died: early 6th century. Patronage: Atripalda, Italy. Also called Sabino of Atripalda and Sabino of Avellino.
  • St. Sabinus of Canosa Died: 556. Patronage: Andria, Italy, diocese of, Bari, Italy, Bari-Bitonto, Italy, archdiocese of, and Canosa di Puglia, Italy.
  • St. Teilo of Llandaff Born: Penally, Wales. Died: 6th century. Patronage: against fever and horses.

Blessed (9)

  • Blessed Anne Catherine Emmerich Born: 8 September 1774 at Flamsche. Died: 9 February 1824 at Dülmen, Germany. Beatified: 3 October 2004 by Pope John Paul II. Also called Anne Catherine Emmerick.
  • Blessed Bernardino Caimi Born: early 15th century in Italy. Died: 9 February 1500.
  • Blessed Erizzo Born: in Florence, Italy. Died: 1094. Beatified: 1600 by Pope Clement VIII (cultus confirmation).
  • Blessed Francisco Sánchez Márquez Born: 24 June 1866 at Alpandeire, Málaga, Spain as Francisco Tomás de San Juan Bautista Márquez y Sánchez. Died: 9 February 1956 in Granada, Spain. Beatified: 12 September 2010 by Pope Benedict XVI. Also called Francisco Tomás Márquez Sánchez and Leopoldo de Alpandeire.
  • Blessed Giacomo Abbondo Born: 27 August 1720 in Salomino, Duchy of Milan (in modern Italy). Died: 9 Febuary 1788 in Tronzano, Ducky of Milan, Italy. Beatified: 11 June 2016 by Pope Francis. Patronage: Tronzano, Italy.
  • Blessed Godeschalk of Zeliv Born: c.1126 in Cologne, Germany. Died: 9 February 1184 in Zeliv, Czech Republic. Also called Godeschalcus, Godeskalk, Gottschalk.
  • Blessed Lambert of Neuwerk Died: 1123.
  • Blessed Luis Magaña Servín Born: 24 August 1902 in Arandas, Jalisco, Mexico. Died: shot by firing squad at 3pm on 9 February 1928 in Arandas, Jalisco, Mexico. Beatified: 20 November 2005 by Pope Benedict XVI.
  • Blessed Marianus Scotus Born: early 11th century in County Donegal, Ireland. Died: 10 February 1088 at Ratisbon (Regensburg), Germany. Also called Marianus of Ireland, Muirdach MacRobartaigh, Murdoch MacGroarty, and Muiredach MacGroarty.

Martyr groups (2)

  • Martyrs of Alexandria
  • Martyrs of Membressa Died: in Membressa in Africa Also celebrated but no entry yet.
