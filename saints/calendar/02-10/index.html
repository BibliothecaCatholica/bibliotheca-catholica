Saints of 10 February

39 saints, blessed, and feasts kept on 10 February, with short facts.

Marian titles (1)

  • Our Lady of the Dove

Saints (18)

  • St. Scholastica Born: 480 in Italy. Died: 543. Patronage: against lightning, against rain, against storms, Benedictines, convulsive children, nuns, Le Mans, France, and Monte Cassino Abbey.
  • St. Andrew of Bethlehem Died: 1st century Bethlehem.
  • St. Aponius of Bethlehem Died: 1st century Bethlehem.
  • St. Austrebertha of Pavilly Born: 630 at Therouanne, Artois, France. Died: 704 at Pavilly, Normandy, France. Patronage: Barentin, France. Also called Austreberta, Eustreberta, Eustreverte.
  • St. Baldegundis Died: c.580.
  • St. Baptus of Magnesia Died: 203 in Magnesia, Asia Minor. Also called Bapto, Baptos, Dauktos, Dauto.
  • St. Charalampias Died: 203 in Magnesia, Asia Minor (in modern Turkey). Patronage: against plague, against cattle diseases, and against cholera.
  • St. Cronan of Clashmore Died: 631 near Dublin, Ireland. Patronage: Clashmore, Ireland.
  • St. Derlugha of Lemmagh Also called Derlugha of Lawyn and Darluga....
  • St. Erluph of Werden Born: Scotland. Died: 830. Also called Erlulph.
  • St. Paul Shipwrecked
  • St. Porfirio Died: 203 in Magnesia, Asia Minor. Also called Porfyrius, Porphyrius, Porphyry.
  • St. Prothadius of Besançon Died: 624. Also called Protadius, Protagius.
  • St. Salvius of Albelda Died: 962.
  • St. Silvanus of Terracina
  • St. Soteris the Martyr Died: beaten and beheaded on 10 February 304 in Rome, Italy. Also called Soteris of Rome.
  • St. Troiano of Saintes Died: c.500. Also called Trojan.
  • St. Trumwin of Whitby Died: c.704. Also called Trumwin of Abercorn.

Blessed (18)

  • Blessed Alexander of Lugo Born: 1595 in Lugo, Italy. Died: hacked to pieces and burned at the stake in 1645 on Chios Island, Greece. Also called Alexander Baldrati and Alexander Baldrati a Lugo.
  • Blessed Alojzije Stepinac Born: 8 May 1898 at Brezaric, Krasic, Croatia as Alojzije Viktor Stepinac. Died: 10 February 1960 at Krasic, Croatia. Beatified: 3 October 1998 by Pope John Paul II at Marija Bistrica, Croatia. Also called Aloysius Stepinac.
  • Blessed Bruno of Minden Died: 10 February 1055. Also called Bruno of Waldeck and Brun....
  • Blessed Catherine du Verdier de la Sorinière Born: 29 June 1758 in Saint-Pierre de Chemillé, Maine-et-Loire, France. Died: 10 February 1794 in Avrillé, Maine-et-Loire, France. Beatified: 19 February 1984 by Pope John Paul II at Rome, Italy.
  • Blessed Clare Agolanti of Rimini Born: 1282 at Rimini, Italy. Died: 10 February 1344 at Rimini, Italy. Beatified: 22 December 1784 by Pope Pius VI (cultus confirmed). Also called Chiara Agolanti and Clara, Klara.
  • Blessed Eusebia Palomino Yenes Born: 15 December 1899 in Cantalpino, Salamanca, Spain. Died: 10 February 1935 at Valverde del Camino, Huelva, Spain. Beatified: 25 April 2004 by Pope John Paul II.
  • Blessed Eusebius of Murano Born: 15th century Spain. Died: 1501.
  • Blessed Hugh of Fosse Born: at Fosse, Belgium. Died: 1164. Beatified: 13 July 1927 by Pope Pius XI (cultus confirmed). Also called Hugues.
  • St. José Sánchez del Río Born: 28 March 1913 in Sahuayo, Michoacán de Ocampo, Mexico. Died: hacked. Canonized: 21 January 2016.
  • Blessed Louise Bessay de la Voûte Born: 22 August 1721 in Saint-Mars-des-Prés, Vendée France. Died: 10 February 1794 in Avrillé, Maine-et-Loire, France. Beatified: 19 February 1984 by Pope John Paul II at Rome, Italy.
  • Blessed Louise Poirier épouse Barré Born: 22 February 1754 in Le Longeron, Maine-et-Loire, France. Died: 10 February 1794 in Avrillé, Maine-et-Loire, France. Beatified: 19 February 1984 by Pope John Paul II at Rome, Italy.
  • Blessed Marie-Anne Hacher du Bois Born: 3 April 1765 in Jallais, Maine-et-Loire, France. Died: 10 February 1794 in Avrillé, Maine-et-Loire, France. Beatified: 19 February 1984 by Pope John Paul II at Rome, Italy.
  • Blessed Marie-Louise du Verdier de la Sorinière Born: 27 June 1765 in Saint-Pierre de Chemillé, Maine-et-Loire, France. Died: 10 February 1794 in Avrillé, Maine-et-Loire, France. Beatified: 19 February 1984 by Pope John Paul II at Rome, Italy.
  • Blessed Mikel Beltoja Born: 9 May 1935 in Beltoj, Shkodrë, Albania. Died: shot by firing squad on 10 February 1974 in Shkodrë, Albania. Beatified: 5 November 2016 by Pope Francis.
  • Blessed Paganus Born: Italian. Died: 1423.
  • Blessed Paul of Wallachia Born: Hungary. Died: c.1240 in Wallachia (in modern Romania).
  • Blessed Pierre Frémond Born: 16 September 1754 in Chaudefonds, Maine-et-Loire, France. Died: 10 February 1794 in Avrillé, Maine-et-Loire, France. Beatified: 19 February 1984 by Pope John Paul II at Rome, Italy.
  • Blessed William of Maleval Born: French. Died: 10 February 1157. Beatified: 1202 (cultus confirmed) by Pope Innocent III. Patronage: Arms manufacturers, armourers, blacksmiths, tinsmiths, Castiglione della Pescaia, Italy, Laoag, Philippines, diocese of, and San Fernando La Union, Philippines, diocese of.

  • Martyred Soldiers of Rome Died: 120 at Rome, Italy. Also kept on 2 July in some calendars.

Other (1)

  • William X of Aquitaine
