Saints of 11 February

38 saints, blessed, and feasts kept on 11 February, with short facts.

Marian titles (2)

  • Our Lady of Lourdes Patronage: sick people, France, Tennessee, Lancaster, England, diocese of, and 6 cities.
  • Our Lady of the Divine Commission

Saints (22)

  • St. Ampelius of Africa Died: c.303 in Carthage in north Africa.
  • St. Ardanus of Tournus Died: c.1057. Also called Ardagne, Ardagno, Ardagnus, Ardain, Ardaing, Ardan.
  • St. Caedmon Born: in the British Isles. Died: c.670 at Whitby, Yorkshire North Riding, England. Also called Cædmon, Cadfan, Cedmon.
  • St. Calocerus of Ravenna Born: Greek. Died: c.130. Also called Calogero, Caio, Calocero.
  • St. Castrensis of Capua Patronage: Castel Volturno, Italy, Marano di Napoli, Italy, Monreale, Italy, city of, Monreale, archdiocese of, and San Castrese di Sessa Aurunca. Also called Castrensis of Sessa, Castrensis of Campania, Castrensis of Africa, and Castrense, Castrese, Castrenze.
  • St. Davitus the Senator Died: 304.
  • St. Desideratus of Clermont Also called Desiderato, Désirat, Desiratus, Désiré.
  • St. Duban Born: mid-5th century in the British Isles. Died: early 6th century Ireland. Also called Dubhán.
  • St. Etchen of Clonfad Born: 490 in Ireland. Died: 11 February 577. Patronage: farmers and plow-men. Also called Echen, Ecian, Eciano, Éidchéan, Etchenius.
  • St. Eutropius of Adrianopolis
  • St. Felix the Senator Died: 304.
  • St. Gobnata Also called Abigail, Albina, Deborah, Gobnat, Gobnet, Gobnait.
  • St. Jonas of Muchon Also called Jonas of Demeskenyanos and Jonas the Gardener.
  • St. Lucius of Adrianople Died: c.348 in the diocese of Adrianople (modern Edirne, Turkey). Also called Lucius of Edirne, Lucius of Odrin, Lucius of Edrêne, Lucius of Jedrene, and Lucius of Hadrianopolis.
  • St. Pedro de Jesús Maldonado-Lucero Born: 15 June 1892 in Chihuáhua City, Chihuáhua, Mexico. Died: 11 February 1937 in Chihuáhua City, Chihuáhua, Mexico. Canonized: 21 May 2000 by Pope John Paul II.
  • St. Saturninus of Africa Died: c.303 in Carthage in north Africa.
  • St. Secundus of Puglia Also called Secundus of Apulia and Secundino.
  • St. Severinus of Agaunum Born: Burgundy, France. Died: c.507.
  • St. Simplicius I of Vienne Died: 417.
  • St. Soter of Rome Born: Rome, Italy. Died: beheaded on 11 February 305 on the Via Appia, Rome, Italy. Also called Sotere, Soteris, Sotra.
  • St. Theodora the Empress Died: 867.
  • St. Victoria of Carthage Died: c.304 in Carthage, North Africa (modern Tunis, Tunisia).

Popes (2)

  • Pope St. Gregory II Born: 669 at Rome, Italy. Died: 11 February 731 at Rome, Italy. Also called Gregory the Younger and Gregory Junior.
  • Pope St. Paschal I Born: at Rome, Italy. Died: 824.

Blessed (9)

  • Blessed Anselm of Rot an der Rot Born: 12th century Germany. Died: 11 February 1162 in Steingaden, Bavaria, Germany. Also called Anselm of Steingaden.
  • Blessed Bartholomew of Olmedo Died: November 1524 in Mexico.
  • Blessed Bertrada of Saint Gallen Died: c.983.
  • Blessed Elizabeth Salviati Born: Italy. Died: 1519.
  • Blessed Gaudencia Benavides Herrero Born: 12 February 1878 in Valdemorillo, León, Spain. Died: 11 February 1937 in Vistillas, Madrid, Spain. Beatified: 27 October 2013 by Pope Benedict XVI.
  • Blessed Helwisa Died: c.1060. Also called Elisa, Eloisa, Heloise, Helvisa.
  • Blessed Henry of Vitskøl Born: 12th century France.
  • Blessed Pietro of Cuneo Born: Cuneo, Italy. Died: 11 February 1322 in Valencia, Spain. Also called Pietro de 'Pasquali.
  • Blessed Tobias Francisco Borrás Román Born: 14 April 1861 in San Jorge, Castellón, Spain. Died: shot on 11 February 1937 at the Hospitaller community just outside the city of Valencia, Spain. Beatified: 25 October 1992 by Pope John Paul II. Also called Francisco Borrás Romeu, Tobias Borrás Román, and Tobias Borrás Romeu.

Martyr groups (1)

  • Martyrs of Africa

Other (2)

  • Guardians of the Holy Scriptures Died: c.303 in Numidia. Also called Anonymous Martyrs in Africa, Martyrs of Africa, Martyrs of Numidia, and Martyrs of the Holy Books.
  • World Day of the Sick
