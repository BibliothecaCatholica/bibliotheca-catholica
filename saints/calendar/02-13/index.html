Saints of 13 February

33 saints, blessed, and feasts kept on 13 February, with short facts.

Saints (27)

  • Sts. Aimo and Vermondo of Meda Died: c.790. Also called Aimo and Vermondo Corio and Aimonius, Antimond, Aimone.
  • St. Archelaus of Oristano Born: Forum Traiani (modern Fordongianus), Italy. Died: stoned to death in Forum Traiani (modern Fordongianus), Italy. Patronage: Oristano, Italy, archdiocese of and Oristano, Italy, city of. Also called Archelao.
  • St. Benignus of Todi Died: c.303.
  • St. Carterius of Bourges Also called Carterio, Chartier.
  • St. Castor of Karden Born: 4th century. Died: c.400 in Karden, Moselle (in modern Germany). Patronage: Koblenz. Also called Castore, Kastor.
  • Blessed Christina of Spoleto Born: 1435 at Lake Lugano, Italy as Agostina Camozzi. Died: 13 February 1458 in Spoleto, Italy. Beatified: 1834 by Pope Gregory XVI (cultus confirmed). Patronage: Calvisano, Italy. Also called Agostina Camozzi, Christina Camozzi, and Christine....
  • St. Dyfnog Born: Wales. Died: 7th century.
  • St. Ermen Born: Ireland.
  • St. Ermenilda of Ely Died: 13 February 703.
  • St. Fulcran of Lodève Died: 13 February 1006. Patronage: diocese of Lodève, France. Also called Fulcrano, Fulcranno.
  • St. Fusca of Ravenna Died: stabbed to death. Patronage: 10 Italian and 1 Croatian city. Also called Fosca.
  • St. Gilbert of Meaux Born: Vermandois, France. Died: 1009 at Meaux, France.
  • St. Giuliana of Turin Also called Giuliana of Ivrea and Juliana....
  • St. Gosbert of Osnabrück Died: 2 February 874.
  • St. Guimérra of Carcassone Died: c.931 in Carcassone, Narbonne, Gaul (in modern France). Also called Guimera.
  • St. Huno Died: c.690 near Chatteris, England. Also called Huna.
  • St. Julian of Lyon Died: Lyon, France.
  • St. Marice
  • St. Martinian the Hermit Born: c.350 at Caesarea, Palestine. Died: c.398 at Athens, Greece. Also called Martinian of Athens.
  • St. Maura of Ravenna Died: c.250 in Ravenna, Italy. Patronage: Frisanco, Italy.
  • St. Modomnoc Born: 6th century Ireland. Died: c.550. Patronage: bees. Also called Dominic, Dominick, Domnoc, Domnock, Modomnock.
  • St. Passivus of Séez Also called Passivo, Passif.
  • St. Paulus Liu Hanzuo Born: c.1778 in Lezhi, Sichuan, China. Died: strangled to death on 13 February 1818 in Chengdu, Sichuan, China. Canonized: 1 October 2000 by Pope John Paul II. Also called Baolu.
  • St. Peter I of Vercelli Died: 997. Also called Petrus.
  • St. Phaolô Lê Van Loc Born: c.1830 in An Nhon, Gia Ðinh, Vietnam. Died: beheaded on 13 February 1859 at the city gates of Gia Ðinh, Vietnam. Canonized: 19 June 1988 by Pope John Paul II. Also called Paul Le-Van-Loc.
  • St. Stephen of Lyon Died: 512. Also called Stephanus, Stefano.
  • St. Stephen of Rieti Died: c.590.

Blessed (6)

  • Blessed Angelo Tancredi Born: late 12th century in Rieti, Italy. Died: 1258. Also called Angelo of Rieti and Angelus....
  • Blessed Berengar of Assisi Died: Santa Maria Guardia Pratorum. Also called Berengario de Asís.
  • Blessed Eustochium of Padua Born: 1444 at San Prosdocimo convent, Padua, Italy as Lucrezia Bellini. Died: 13 February 1469 at San Prosdocimo convent, Padua, Italy. Patronage: against insanity, against mental illness, against temptations, children whose parents are not married, illegitimacy, and mentally ill people.
  • Blessed James Alfred Miller Born: 21 September 1944 in Stevens Point, Wisconsin. Died: 13 February 1982 in Huehuetenango, Guatemala. Beatified: 7 December 2019 by Pope Francis. Also called Leo William, Santiago.
  • Blessed Jordan of Saxony Born: c.1190 at Padberg Castle. Died: drowned in 1237 in a shipwreck off the coast of Syria. Beatified: 1826 (cultus confirmed) by Pope Leo XII. Patronage: against drowning, Dominicans, and University of Santo Tomas Faculty of Engineering. Also called Jordan de Alamaia.
  • Blessed Maria Alvarez Marco Died: 1501 at Albacete, Spain.
