Saints of 26 February

29 saints, blessed, and feasts kept on 26 February, with short facts.

Marian titles (1)

  • Our Lady of the Fields

Saints (14)

  • St. Alexander of Alexandria Born: 3rd century in northern Egypt. Died: February 326 at Alexandria, Egypt. Also called Alessandro di Alessandria.
  • St. Agricola of Nevers Died: c.594.
  • St. Dionysius of Augsburg Died: c.303. Also called Dionigi.
  • St. Ethnea Also called Athna, Ethene, Ethni, Hethna.
  • St. Eutropia of Lentini Died: 260 in Lentini, Italy.
  • St. Faustinian of Bologna Also called Faustinianus, Faustinus.
  • St. Felix
  • St. Flavianus of Como Died: 26 February 565. Also called Flaviano.
  • St. Fortunatus
  • St. Irene Born: c.470. Died: 490.
  • St. Paula of Saint Joseph of Calasanz Born: 11 October 1799 at Arenys de Mar. Died: 26 February 1889 at Olesa de Montserrat, Barcelona, Spain. Canonized: 25 November 2001 by Pope John Paul II. Also called Paula Montal Fornes and Paola....
  • St. Porphyrius of Gaza Born: Thessalonica, Greece. Died: 420. Also called Porphyry, Porfirio.
  • St. Servulus of Verona Died: c.407. Also called Servolo, Memorial Note, and Andrea di Firenze.
  • St. Victor the Hermit Born: 6th century at Troyes, France. Died: 6th-century at Saturniac (modern Saint-Vittre). Patronage: Arcis-sur-Aube, France. Also called Victor of Arcis, Victor the Hermit, and Vittre, Vitre.

Blessed (11)

  • Blessed Adalbert of Tegernsee Died: 8th century. Also called Adalbert of Warngau and Adalbertus....
  • Blessed Arnold of Stromberg Also called Arnoldus.
  • Blessed Francisco Bejarano Fernández Born: 1 June 1877 in Añora, Córdoba, Spain. Died: 26 February 1938. Beatified: 16 October 2021 by Pope Francis.
  • Blessed Leo of Saint-Bertin Died: 1163.
  • Blessed Martino Martini Born: late 12th century. Died: 1249 in Lisbon, Portugal.
  • Blessed Mechthild of Sponheim Born: in the area of modern Germany. Died: 26 February 1154 in Sponheim, Germany.
  • Blessed Michela Ranzi of Vercelli Died: 1493.
  • Blessed Ottokar of Tegernsee Died: 8th century. Also called Otkar, Oatkar, Octarius.
  • Blessed Piedad de la Cruz Ortiz Real Born: 12 November 1842 in Bocairente, Valencia, Spain as Tomasa Ortiz Real. Died: 26 February 1916 in Alcantrarilla, Murcia, Spain. Beatified: 21 March 2004 by Pope John Paul II. Patronage: Salesian Sisters of the Sacred Heart of Jesus. Also called Tomasa Ortiz Real and Pietà della Croce Ortiz Real.
  • Blessed Robert Drury Born: c.1567 at Buckinghamshire, England. Died: hanged. Beatified: 22 November 1987 by Pope John Paul II. Also called Robert Drewrie.
  • Blessed Ulrik of Obermarchtal Born: early 12th century Germany. Died: 26 February 1187 in Swabia, Germany. Also called Ulric, Ulrich.

  • Martyrs of Alexandria

  • Inger
