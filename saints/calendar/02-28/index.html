Saints of 28 February

37 saints, blessed, and feasts kept on 28 February, with short facts.

Saints (20)

  • St. Abercius Also called Abircius.
  • St. Auguste Chapdelaine Born: 6 January 1814 at La Rochelle-Normande, France. Died: beheaded on 29 February 1856 in Su-Lik-Hien, Kwang-Si province, China. Canonized: 1 October 2000 by Pope John Paul II. Also called Augustus Chapdelaine, Father Ma, Papa Chapdelaine, Addtional Memorial, and Martyrs of China. Also kept on 29 February in some calendars.
  • St. Caerealis Born: Egyptian. Died: 251 in Rome, Italy. Also called Cerulus, Celerius.
  • St. Caerealis of Alexandria Born: Egyptian. Died: in Alexandria, Egypt.
  • St. Cyra of Beroea Died: c.455.
  • St. Ermine Also called Ermina, Febaria.
  • St. Gaius of Alexandria Born: Egyptian. Died: in Alexandria, Egypt. Also called Caius.
  • St. Justus the Potter Died: martyred c.250.
  • St. Llibio Died: 6th century. Patronage: Llanlibio, Wales. Also called Libio.
  • St. Macarius the Potter Died: martyred c.250.
  • St. Maidoc Also called Madoc.
  • St. Marana of Beroea Died: c.455 in Beroea, Syria.
  • St. Oswald of Worcester Died: 29 February 992. Also kept on 29 February in some calendars.
  • St. Pupulus of Alexandria Born: Egyptian. Died: in Alexandria, Egypt.
  • St. Romanus of Condat Born: c.390 at Upper Bugey, France. Died: c.465. Patronage: against insanity or mental illness, drowning victims, and mentally ill people.
  • St. Ruellinus of Treguier Died: 6th century. Also called Ruellin, Ruelin.
  • St. Rufinus the Potter Died: martyred c.250.
  • St. Serapion of Alexandria Born: Egyptian. Died: martyred in Alexandria, Egypt.
  • St. Sillan of Bangor Died: c.610. Also called Sillian, Silvanus.
  • St. Theophilus the Potter Died: martyred c.250.

Popes (1)

  • Pope St. Hilary Died: 28 February 468 at Rome, Italy. Also called Hilarus, Hilarius. Also kept on 29 February in some calendars.

Blessed (9)

  • Blessed Alexius Sugi Shohachi Born: c.1602 in Amakusa, Nagasaki, Japan. Died: 28 February 1627 in Unzen, Japan. Beatified: 24 November 2008 by Pope Benedict XVI.
  • Blessed Antonia of Florence Born: 1400 at Florence, Italy. Died: 29 February 1472 in her convent at Aquila, Italy. Beatified: 1847 by Pope Blessed Pius IX (cultus confirmed). Also kept on 29 February in some calendars.
  • Blessed Bosone of Chiaravalle Born: early 12th century Italy. Died: late 12th century. Also called Boson.
  • Blessed Carlo Gnocchi Born: 25 October 1902 at San Colombano al Lambro, Italy. Died: 28 February 1956 at Milan, Italy. Beatified: 25 October 2009 by Pope Benedict XVI.
  • Blessed Damianus Ichiyata Born: Antoku Koba, Japan. Died: 28 February 1627 in Unzen, Japan. Beatified: 24 November 2008 by Pope Benedict XVI.
  • Blessed Daniel Brottier Born: 7 September 1876 at La Ferté-Saint-Cyr, Diocese of Blois, France. Died: 28 February 1936. Beatified: 25 November 1984 by Pope John Paul II in Rome, Italy.
  • Blessed Dionisius Saeki Zenka Born: Fukae, Nagasaki, Japan. Died: 28 February 1627 in Unzen, Japan. Beatified: 24 November 2008 by Pope Benedict XVI.
  • Blessed Stanislaw Antoni Trojanowski Born: 29 July 1908 in Sadlowo, Mazowieckie. Beatified: 13 June 1999 by Pope John Paul II. Also called Tymoteusz, Timoteo Trojanowski, and Stanislaw Tymoteusz Trojanowski.
  • Blessed Villana Debotti Born: 1332 in Florence, Italy. Died: 29 January 1361.

Martyr groups (4)

  • Anonymous Martyrs of Alexandria
  • Martyrs of Antwerp Died: in Rome, Italy.
  • Martyrs of Corinth
  • Martyrs of Unzen Died: 28 February 1627 in Unzen, Japan. Beatified: 24 November 2008 by Pope Benedict XVI.

Other (3)

  • Abba the Great of Selevkia-Ktesiphon
  • Elisabeth of Pomerania
  • Jirays Hana Saleh
