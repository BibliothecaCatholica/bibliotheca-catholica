Saints of 7 March

33 saints, blessed, and feasts kept on 7 March, with short facts.

Saints (18)

  • St. Ardo of Aniane Born: Languedoc (part of modern France) as Smaragdus. Died: 843. Also called Ardone, Smaragdus.
  • St. Bernard-Louis Beaulieu Born: 8 October 1840 in Langon, Gironde, France. Died: tortured. Canonized: 6 May 1984 by Pope John Paul II.
  • St. Deifer of Bodfari
  • St. Drausinus of Soissons Died: c.674. Patronage: against enemy plots, champions, and invincible people. Also called Drausius, Drausin.
  • St. Enodoch Died: c.520. Also called Wenedoc.
  • St. Esterwine of Wearmouth Born: in 650 in Northumbria, England. Died: 6 March 686. Also called Easterwine, Eosterwine, Esterwinus.
  • St. Eubulus of Caesarea Died: 7 March 308.
  • St. Felicity of Carthage Died: mauled by wild beasts and beheaded 7 March 203 at Carthage, North Africa. Patronage: cattle and martyrs. Also called Felicitas.
  • St. Gaudiosus of Brescia Died: c.445. Also called Gaudioso.
  • St. Ioannes Baptista Nam Chong-Sam Born: 1817 in Chungju, Chungcheong-do, South Korea. Died: 7 March 1866 at the Small West Gate, Seoul, South Korea. Canonized: 6 May 1984 by Pope John Paul II. Also called Nam Jong-Sam, Yohan, and John the Baptist Nam Chong-sam.
  • St. Mocelloc of Magh-Scethe
  • St. Paul of Prusa Died: 840 in Egypt. Also called Paul of Pelusium and Paul of Plousias.
  • St. Paul the Simple Died: c.339. Also called Paolo il Semplice.
  • St. Perpetua Died: mauled by wild beasts and beheaded 7 March 203 at Carthage, North Africa. Patronage: cattle, married women, martyrs, Carthage, Tunisia, and Santa Perpètua de Mogoda, Catalonia, Spain. Also called Vibia Perpetua and Vivia Perpetua.
  • St. Pierre-Henri Dorie Born: 23 September 1839 in St-Hilaire-de-Talmont, Vendée, France. Died: tortured. Canonized: 6 May 1984 by Pope John Paul II. Also called Peter Henricus Dorie.
  • St. Siméon-François Berneux Born: 14 May 1814 in Château-du-Loir, Sarthe, France. Died: tortured. Canonized: 6 May 1984 by Pope John Paul II.
  • St. Simon-Marie-Just Ranfer de Bretenières Died: tortured. Canonized: 6 May 1984 by Pope John Paul II. Also called Pere Païk Chen Fou (Father White as Snow).
  • St. Teresa Margaret Redi Born: 15 July 1747 at Arezzo, Tuscany, Italy as Anna Maria Redi. Died: 7 March 1770 at Florence, Italy. Canonized: 19 March 1934 by Pope Pius XI. Also called Ann Maria Redi, Anna Maria Redi, Anne Mary Redi, Teresa Margaret of the Sacred Heart, and Teresa Margherita Redi of the Sacred Heart.

Blessed (14)

  • Blessed Daniel of Wichterich Died: 7 March 1364 at the Cistercian monastery in Altenberg, Germany. Also called Daniel of Verden.
  • Blessed German Gardiner Died: martyred on 7 March 1544 at Tyburn, London, England. Beatified: 29 December 1886 by Pope Leo XIII (cultus confirmation). Also called Jermyn Gardiner.
  • Blessed Henry of Austria
  • Blessed John Ireland Died: 7 March 1544 at Tyburn, London, England. Beatified: 15 December 1929 by Pope Pius XI.
  • Blessed John Larke Died: 7 March 1544 at Tyburn, London, England. Beatified: 29 December 1886 by Pope Leo XIII (cultus confirmed).
  • Blessed José Olallo Valdés Born: 12 February 1820 in Havana, Cuba. Died: 7 March 1889 in Camagüey, Cuba. Beatified: 29 November 2008 by Pope Benedict XVI.
  • Blessed Leonid Feodorov Born: 4 November 1879 at Saint Petersburg, Russia. Died: 7 March 1935. Beatified: 27 June 2001 by Pope John Paul II in Ukraine. Also called Father Leontios and Leonid F'odorov.
  • Blessed Luc Sy Born: 1938 in Ban Pha Hôk, Xieng Khouang, Laos. Died: 7 March 1970 in Den Din, Vientiane, Laos. Beatified: 11 December 2016 by Pope Francis.
  • Blessed Maisam Pho Inpèng Born: 1934 in Sam Neua, Houaphan, Laos. Died: 7 March 1970 in Den Din, Vientiane, Laos. Beatified: 11 December 2016 by Pope Francis.
  • Blessed Manuel Vílchez Montalvo Born: 5 June 1889 in Moreda, Granada, Spain. Died: 7 March 1937 in Sierra Nevada, Granada, Spain. Beatified: 26 February 2022 by Pope Francis.
  • Blessed María Antonia de Paz y Figueroa Born: 1730 in Silípica, Santiago del Estero, Argentina. Died: 6 to 7 March 1799 in the retreat house in Buenos Aires, Argentina. Beatified: 27 August 2016 by Pope Francis. Also called María Antonia of Saint Joseph and Mama Antula.
  • Blessed Reinhard of Reinhausen Died: c.1168. Also called Reginhard.
  • Blessed Volker of Segeberg Died: c.1135 near Segeberg, Germany.
  • Blessed William of Assisi Born: England. Died: 1232 in Assisi, Italy. Also called William the Englishman.

Martyr groups (1)

  • Martyrs of Carthage Died: mauled by wild beasts and beheaded 7 March 203 at Carthage, North Africa.
