Saints of 11 March

40 saints, blessed, and feasts kept on 11 March, with short facts.

Saints (31)

  • St. Sophronius of Jerusalem Born: Damascus, Syria. Died: c.638; sources disagree on cause and location. Also called Sophronius of Damascus, Sophronius the Sophist, and Sofronio....
  • St. Aengus the Culdee Born: near Clonenagh, Ireland. Died: 11 March 824. Also called Aengus of Clonenagh, Aengus of Tallaght, Aengus Cúldeo, and Angus, Dengus, Oengo, Oengus, Ceile De.
  • St. Alberta of Agen Died: c.286 in Agen, France.
  • St. Alexius U Se-Yong Born: 1845 in Seoheung, Hwanghae-do, North Korea. Died: 11 March 1866 at the Small West Gate, Seoul, South Korea. Canonized: 6 May 1984 by Pope John Paul II. Also called Alejo U Se-yong, Alessio U Se-yong, Alleksio, and U Se-Yeong.
  • St. Ðaminh Cam Born: Cam Chuong, Bac Ninh, Vietnam. Died: beheaded on 11 March 1859 in Hung Yên, Vietnam. Canonized: 19 June 1988 by Pope John Paul II. Also called Domenic Cam.
  • St. Amunia of San Millán Died: 1069.
  • St. Áurea of San Millán Born: c.1042 at Villavelayo, Spain. Died: 1069 at San Millán de la Cogolla, La Rioja, Spanish Navarre. Patronage: Villavelayo, Spain. Also called Oria.
  • St. Benedict Crispus of Milan Died: 725. Also called Benedetto.
  • St. Candidus the Martyr Died: c.257 in North Africa.
  • St. Constantine II Died: bled to death in 874 in a cave near Crail. Also called Constantine of Strathclyde, Constantine of Britannia, Constantine of Cornwall, and Constantine III.
  • St. Constantine of Carthage
  • St. Eulogius of Cordoba Died: scourged and beheaded 11 March 859 at Cordoba, Spain. Patronage: carpenters, coppersmiths, Writings, Apologia, Exhortation to Martyrdom, and Memorial of the Saints.
  • St. Euthymius of Sardis Born: mid-8th century. Died: scourged to death c.840. Also called Euthymios, Eutimio.
  • St. Firmian the Abbot Died: c.1020. Also called Fermanus, Firminus.
  • St. Firmin of Amiens Also called Firmino, Firminus.
  • St. Firmus the Martyr Also called Firminus.
  • St. Gorgonius the Martyr
  • St. Heraclius of Carthage Born: African. Died: c.263 in Carthage, North Africa.
  • St. Marcus Chong Ui-Bae Born: 1795 in Yongin, Gyeonggi-do, South Korea. Died: 11 March 1866 at the Small West Gate, Seoul, South Korea. Canonized: 6 May 1984 by Pope John Paul II. Also called Jeong Eui-Bae and Mareuko.
  • St. Peter the Spaniard Patronage: Boville Ernica, Italy.
  • St. Pionius of Smyrna Born: at Smyrna, Turkey. Died: nailed to a stake and burned alive on 12 March 250. Also called Pione, Pionio, Pionios.
  • St. Piperion the Martyr Died: c.257 in North Africa.
  • St. Quiricus of Murisengo Died: c.286 in Murisengo, Alessandria, Italy.
  • St. Rosina of Wenglingen Died: 4th century. Patronage: Wenlingen, Germany. Also called Rofina, Rufina.
  • St. Serano of Oviedo Also called Serrano, Serani, Sernano.
  • St. Thalus the Martyr Died: crucified c.300 at Laodicea, Syria. Also called Tallo.
  • St. Trophimus the Martyr Died: crucified c.300 at Laodicea, Syria. Also called Trofimo.
  • St. Vigilius of Auxerre Died: murdered in 685 in a forest near Compiègne, France.
  • St. Vincent of Léon Born: Spanish. Died: beheaded on 11 March 630. Patronage: Cercemaggiore, Italy.
  • St. Vindician of Cambrai Born: 632 at Bullecourt, France. Died: 712 at Brussels, Belgium. Also called Vidicianus, Vindiciano, Vindicien.
  • St. Zosimus of Carthage Born: African. Died: c.263. Also called Zozimus.

Blessed (8)

  • Blessed Alramo of Niederaltaich Died: 11 March 1123.
  • Blessed Balbina the Younger Born: 1215 in Assisi, Italy. Died: 11 March 1254 in Assisi, Italy. Also called Balbina of Assisi.
  • Blessed Dedë Nikaj Born: 19 July 1900 in Shkodër, Albania. Died: shot on 11 March 1948 in Shkodër, Albania. Beatified: 5 November 2016 by Pope Francis. Also called Brother Ciprian, Ciprian Nika, and Dedë Nikacj.
  • Blessed Frano Gjini Born: 20 February 1886 in Shkodër, Albania. Died: shot on 11 March 1948 in Shkodër, Albania. Beatified: 5 November 2016 by Pope Francis.
  • Blessed John Kearney Born: 1619 in Cashel, Tipperary, Ireland. Died: hanged on 11 March 1653 in Clonmel, Ireland. Beatified: 27 September 1992 by Pope John Paul II in Rome, Italy. Also called Seán Ó Cearnaigh.
  • Blessed John Righi of Fabriano Born: 1469 at Fabriano, Ancona, Italy. Died: 1539. Beatified: 1903 by Pope Leo XIII (cultus confirmed). Also called Giovanni Battista da Fabriano, Giovanni da Fabriano, Giovanni Righi, Joannes de Fabriano, and Johannes Baptista Righi.
  • Blessed Pal Prennushi Born: 2 October 1881 in Shkodrë, Albania. Died: shot on 11 March 1948 in Shkodrë, Albania. Beatified: 5 November 2016 by Pope Francis. Also called Mati, Mattia.
  • Blessed Thomas Atkinson Born: Menthorpe, North Yorkshire, England. Died: hanged. Beatified: 22 November 1987 by Pope John Paul II.

Martyr groups (1)

  • Martyrs of Antioch Died: c.300 in Antioch, Syria.
