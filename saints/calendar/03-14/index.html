Saints of 14 March

24 saints, blessed, and feasts kept on 14 March, with short facts.

Saints (14)

  • St. Alexander of Pydna Died: beheaded c.305 in Macedonia. Also called Alessandro, Alexandros.
  • St. Aphrodisius of Africa
  • St. Boniface Curitan Died: c.660. Also called Boniface Curitan of Ross, Boniface Kyrin, Boniface Kyrstin, Boniface of Ross, and Kyrin, Kyrstin.
  • St. Diaconus
  • St. Eutychius of Mesopotamia Died: 741. Also called Eustathius.
  • St. Innocent of Verona Died: 14 March.
  • St. Lazarus of Milan Died: 14 March 450. Also called Lazzaro.
  • St. Leo of the Agro Verano Died: Agro Verano, Rome, Italy.
  • St. Leobinus of Chartres Born: at Poitiers, France. Died: 14 March 558. Patronage: against dropsy, against edema, against rheumatism, innkeepers, and wine merchants. Also called Lubinus, Lubin, Lubinius, Lumine, Leubinus, Loubin.
  • St. Matilda of Saxony Born: c.895 at Engern, Westphalia, Germany. Died: 14 March 968 at Quedlinburg, Germany. Patronage: death of children, disappointing children, falsely accused people, large families, people ridiculed for their piety, queens, second marriages, and widows.
  • St. Maximilian
  • St. Peter of Africa
  • St. Talmach
  • St. Urio of Padua Died: latter 8th century Genoa, Italy.

Blessed (8)

  • Blessed Agno of Zaragoza Born: Zaragoza, Spain. Died: 1260 Zaragoza, Spain.
  • Blessed Arnold of Padua Born: 1185. Died: 10 February 1255 in the prison in Limena, Padua, Italy. Also called Arnold de'Cattanei, Arnold Cattaneo, and Arnald, Arnaldo, Arnaud, Arnoldus.
  • Blessed Eve of Liège Born: c.1205 in Liège, Belgium. Died: 1265 in Liège, Belgium. Beatified: 1 May 1902 by Pope Leo XIII. Also called Eve of Saint-Martin, Eve of Mount Cornelius, and Eva, Evelyne, Heva.
  • Blessed Giacomo Cusmano Born: 15 March 1834 in Palermo, Italy. Died: at 04:30am on 14 March 1888 in Palermo, Italy. Beatified: 30 October 1983 by Pope John Paul II. Also called Jacob Cusmano, Jakob Cusmano, and Jacques Cusmano.
  • Blessed Giovanni of Genoa Died: 14 March 1166 at the San Stefano monastery in Genoa, Italy.
  • Blessed Pauline of Thuringia Died: 14 March 1107 in Fulda, Germany. Also called Pauline of Hirsau, Pauline of Münsterschwarzach, Pauline of Fulda, Pauline of Zell, and Paolina, Paulina.
  • Blessed Philip of Turin Born: Turin, Italy. Died: 14 March 1246 in Perugia, Italy. Also called Philip Longo and Filippo....
  • Blessed Thomas Vives Died: stoned to death. Also called Tommaso.

Martyr groups (2)

  • 47 Martyrs of Rome Died: martyred c.67 in Rome, Italy.
  • Martyrs of Valeria Died: hanged on a tree in Valeria, Italy in the 5th century.

This list combines the Vatican News “Saint of the day” pages with a long-form calendar of saints, blessed, and feasts (credited on our Resources page). Calendars differ, so a note marks each place the sources disagree. This is not the General Roman Calendar. Resources · Sources & method
