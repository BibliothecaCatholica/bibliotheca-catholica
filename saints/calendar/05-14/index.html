Saints of 14 May

31 saints, blessed, and feasts kept on 14 May, with short facts.

Saints (27)

  • St. Ampelio Born: 4th century in Upper Egypt. Died: 5 October c.410 in Bordighera, Italy. Patronage: blacksmiths and Bordighera, Italy.
  • St. Augia of Apt
  • St. Boniface of Ferentino Patronage: alcoholics.
  • St. Boniface of Tarsus Died: beheaded c.307 in Tarsus, Cilicia (in modern Turkey).
  • St. Carthage the Younger Born: 555 in County Kerry, Ireland. Died: c.637 at Lismore, Ireland. Patronage: diocese of Lismore, Ireland and diocese of Waterford and Lismore, Ireland. Also called Carthage of Lismore, Carthage of Mochuda, Mo Chutu mac Fínaill, and Cartaco, Carthach, Carthagus, Mochuda.
  • St. Claudius of Antwerp Died: in or near Rome, Italy. Also called Claudio.
  • St. Corona the Martyr Died: c.165 in Syria. Patronage: treasure hunters, diocese of Belluno-Feltre, Italy, Castelfidardo, Italy, Feltre, Italy, and Monte Romano, Italy. Also called Corona of Damascus.
  • St. Costanzo of Capri Born: in Italy as Antonii de Ripolis. Died: 8th century near Marina Grande, Capri, Italy. Also called Costantio, Constantius, and Antonii de Ripolis.
  • St. Costanzo of Vercelli Died: c.541 at Vercelli, Italy.
  • St. Dyfan Also called Deruvianus, Damian.
  • St. Engelmer Died: 1096.
  • St. Erembert of Toulouse Born: at Wocourt near Passy, France. Died: c.672 at Fontenelle Abbey. Also called Erembert of Fontenelle and Erembert of Wocourt.
  • St. Felice of Aquileia Born: in Vicenza, Italy. Died: beheaded in 296 in the San Felice district outside Aquileia, Italy. Patronage: Chioggia, Italy and Vincenza, Italy. Also called Felice of Vincenza.
  • St. Fortunatus of Aquileia Born: in Vicenza, Italy. Died: beheaded in 296 in the San Felice district outside Aquileia, Fruili, Italy. Patronage: Chioggia, Italy and Vincenza, Italy. Also called Fortunatus of Vincenza.
  • St. Gal of Clermont-Ferrand Died: 551. Also called Gallo.
  • St. Henedina of Sardinia Died: c.130 at Sardinia, Italy. Patronage: diocese of Ales-Terralba, Italy. Also called Enedina.
  • St. Isidore of Chios Born: in Alexandria, Egypt. Died: drowned in a well c.251 at Chios, Greece. Patronage: sailors.
  • St. Justa of Sardinia Died: c.130 at Sardinia, Italy. Patronage: diocese of Ales-Terralba, Italy and Gesico, Italy. Also called Giusta.
  • St. Justina of Sardinia Died: c.130. Patronage: diocese of Ales-Terralba, Italy. Also called Giustina.
  • St. Maria Mazzarello Born: 9 May 1837 at Mornese, Acqui, Italy. Died: 14 May 1881 in Nizza Monferrato, Asti Italy. Canonized: 24 June 1951 by Pope Pius XII. Patronage: against bodily ills, sickness; sick people. Also called Mary Dominic Mazzarello.
  • St. Matthias the Apostle Died: stoned to death at Colchis in 80. Patronage: against alcoholism; reformed alcoholics, against smallpox, carpenters, tailors, diocese of Gary, Indiana, and diocese of Great Falls-Billings, Montana.
  • St. Maximus Died: stoned to death in Asia Minor (modern Turkey).
  • St. Michael Garicoïts Born: 15 April 1797 in Ibarre, Pyrénées-Atlantiques, France. Died: 14 May 1863 in Bétharram, Pyrénées-Atlantiques, France. Canonized: 6 July 1947 by Pope Pius XII.
  • St. Pons of Pradleves Patronage: Pradleves, Italy. Also called Pontius, Ponzio.
  • St. Pontius of Cimiez Died: c.258 in Cimella, France. Patronage: Saint-Pons, France.
  • St. Tuto of Regensburg Died: 930. Also called Totto of Regensburg.
  • St. Victor the Martyr Died: beheaded c.165 in Syria. Patronage: diocese of Belluno-Feltre, Italy, Castelfidardo, Italy, and Feltre, Italy.

Blessed (3)

  • Blessed Barbaro of Assisi Died: 1229 in Assisi, Italy.
  • Blessed Diego of Narbonne Born: in Spain of French immigrant parents.
  • Blessed Giles of Santarém Born: 1185 at Vaozela, Portugal. Died: 14 May 1265 in Santarem, Portugal. Beatified: 9 May 1748 by Pope Benedict XIV (cultus confirmed). Also called Giles of Portugal, Giles of Vauzela, and Egigio, Egidius, Gil.

Martyr groups (1)

  • Martyrs of Seoul Died: 14 May 1801 at the Small West Gate, Seoul, South Korea. Beatified: 15 August 2014 by Pope Francis.
