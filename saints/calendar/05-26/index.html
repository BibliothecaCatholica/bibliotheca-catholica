Saints of 26 May

38 saints, blessed, and feasts kept on 26 May, with short facts.

Marian titles (1)

  • Our Lady of Caravaggio Patronage: diocese of Cremona, Italy. Also called Nostra Signora di Caravaggio.

Saints (23)

  • St. Philip Neri Born: 22 July 1515 at Florence, Italy. Died: 27 May 1595 at the church of San Maria in Vallicella, Italy. Canonized: 12 March 1622 by Pope Gregory XV. Patronage: Gravina, Italy, Rome, Italy, and United States Army Special Forces. Also called Amabile Santo, Apostle of Rome, and Philip Romolo Neri.
  • St. Alphaeus Also called Cleophas.
  • St. Anderea Kaggwa Born: at Bunyoro, Uganda. Died: beheaded in 26 May 1886 at Munyonyo, Uganda. Canonized: 18 October 1964 by Pope Paul VI at Rome, Italy. Also called Andreas, Andrew.
  • St. Becan of Cork Also called Becan of Cluain-Aird-Mobecog. Also kept on 5 April in some calendars.
  • St. Damian the Missionary Also called Derivianus, Diruvianus, Deruvian, Dyfan.
  • St. Desiderius of Vienne Born: Autun, France. Died: 608 at Saint-Didier-sur-Chalaronne, France. Also called Didier.
  • St. Felicissimus of Todi
  • St. Fugatius the Missionary Also called Phaganu, Fagan, Ffager, Phaganus.
  • St. Gioan Doàn Trinh Hoan Born: c.1798 at Kim-Long, Thùa Thiên, Vietnam. Died: beheaded on 26 May 1861 near Dong Hoi, Quang Bình, Vietnam. Canonized: 19 June 1988 by Pope John Paul II. Also called John Hoan.
  • St. Guinizo Born: in Spain. Died: c.1050. Also called Guinizzone.
  • St. Heraclius of Todi
  • St. Iosephus Chang Song-Jib Born: 1786 in Seoul, South Korea. Died: 27 May 1839 in Seoul Prison, South Korea. Canonized: 6 May 1984 by Pope John Paul II. Also called Joseph Chang Song-jib, Giuseppe Chang Song-jib, and Yosep Jang Seong-jib.
  • St. Mary Ann de Paredes Born: 31 October 1618 at Quito, Ecuador. Died: 26 May 1645 at Quito, Ecuador. Canonized: 9 July 1950 by Pope Pius XII. Patronage: against bodily ills or sickness; sick people, against the loss of parents, people rejected by religious orders, and Americas.
  • St. Oduvald of Melrose Born: Scottish. Died: 698.
  • St. Paulinus of Todi
  • St. Pere Sans Jordà Born: 3 September 1680 in Ascó, Tarragona, Spain. Died: beheaded on 26 May 1747 in Fuzhou, Fujian, China. Canonized: 1 October 2000 by Pope John Paul II. Also called Pedro Sanz.
  • St. Ponsiano Ngondwe Born: at Buganda, Uganda. Died: beheaded and dismembered on 26 May 1886 at Ttakajjunge, Uganda. Canonized: 18 October 1964 by Pope Paul VI at Rome, Italy. Also called Pontian Ngondwe and Martyrs of Uganda.
  • St. Priscus of Auxerre Died: c.272 near Auxerre, France. Also called Prisco.
  • St. Quadratus Died: Africa.
  • St. Quadratus the Apologist Died: 2nd century. Also called Disciple of the Apostles and Quadratus of Athens.
  • St. Regintrudis of Nonnberg Died: c.750. Also called Regintrude.
  • St. Simitrius of Rome Died: beheaded c.159 on the Via Salaria Nuova in Rome, Italy. Also called Simitrio, Simetrio.
  • St. Zachary of Vienne Died: c.106.

Popes (1)

  • Pope St. Eleuterus Born: at Nicopolis, Epirus, Greece. Died: 24 May 189 in Rome, Italy. Also called Eleuterius, Eleutherius, Eleutheros.

Blessed (9)

  • Blessed Andrea Franchi Born: 1335 in Pistoia, Italy. Died: 26 May 1401 in Pistoia, Italy. Beatified: 21 November 1921 by Pope Benedict XV (cultus confirmation).
  • Blessed Armand Radigue Born: 8 May 1823 in Saint-Patrice-du-Désert, Orne, France. Also called Father Ladislas, Ladislao Radigue, and Armando Pietro Pietro.
  • Blessed Berengar of Saint-Papoul Died: 26 May 1093. Also called Berenger, Berencardus, Berengary.
  • Blessed Francis Patrizzi Born: 1266 in Siena, Italy. Died: 26 May 1328 in Siena, Italy. Beatified: 11 September 1743 by Pope Benedict XIV (cultus confirmation). Patronage: for reconciliation. Also called Francis Patrizi, Francis Patrizi of Siena, and Francesco of Siena.
  • Blessed Jean-Marie Rouchouze Born: 14 December 1810 in Saint-Julien-en-Jarez, Loire, France. Also called Father Marcellin, Marcellino, and John Mary Rouchouze.
  • Blessed Jean-Pierre-Eugène Tardieu Born: 18 November 1814 in Chasseradès, Lozère, France. Also called Father Frézal, Frézal Tardieu, and Giovanni Pietro Eugenio.
  • Blessed Jules Tuffier Born: 16 March 1807 in Malzieu, Lozère, France. Also called Father Polycarpe, Polycarpo, and Giulio Tuffier.
  • Blessed Lambert Péloguin of Vence Born: 1084 at Bauduen, France. Died: 1154 at Vence, France.
  • Blessed Mathieu-Henri Planchat Born: 8 November 1823 in La-Roche-sur-Yon, Vendée, France. Also called Enrico Planchat.

Other (4)

  • Arnaldo Buysson and Companions
  • Maria Angelica Mastroti of Papasidero
  • Maria del Fonte of Caravaggio
  • Pardo
