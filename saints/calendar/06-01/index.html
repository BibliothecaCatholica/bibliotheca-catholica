Saints of 1 June

65 saints, blessed, and feasts kept on 1 June, with short facts.

Marian titles (2)

  • Our Lady, Comforter of the Afflicted
  • Our Lady of Grace

Saints (43)

  • St. Justin Martyr Born: c.100 at Flavia Neapolis, Judaea province, Roman Empire (modern Nablus, Palestine). Died: beheaded in 165 at Rome, Italy. Patronage: apologists, lecturers, orators, speakers, and philosophers. Also called Justin the Philosopher and Justin of Nablus.
  • St. Agapetus of Ruthenia Died: c.1100. Also called Agapitus.
  • St. Atto of Oca Died: 1044.
  • St. Candida of Whitchurch Died: in Dorset, England. Also called Gwen, Hwitn, White, Whyte, Wite, Witt, Witta.
  • St. Caprasius of Lérins Died: 430. Patronage: Aulla, Italy. Also called Caprais, Caprasio.
  • St. Clarus of Aquitaine Died: beheaded in the early 2nd century in Lectoure, France. Patronage: eyes; against eye problem (in Cologne-du-Gers, France). Also called Clarus of Albi, Clarus of Lactoræ, Clarus of Lectoure, and Chiaro, Clair, Clar.
  • St. Claudius of Vienne Also called Claudio.
  • St. Conrad of Trier Born: Swabia, Germany. Died: thrown. Also called Conrad of Treves and Cuno....
  • St. Crescentinus of Saldo Died: beheaded on 1 June 303 at Saldo, Italy. Patronage: Città di Castello, Italy and Urbino, Italy. Also called Crescentinus di Città di Castello.
  • St. Cronan of Lismore Died: 717.
  • St. Damian of Scotland Also called Damianus.
  • St. Dionysius of Ruthenia Died: c.1100.
  • St. Donatus of Lucania Died: Lucania region of southern Italy.
  • St. Egole of the Eegoilse Desert Born: Irish. Also called Egole di Disert Eegoilse.
  • St. Felinus of Perugia Died: c.250 at Perugia, Italy. Patronage: Arona, Italy. Also called Felino of Perugia.
  • St. Fermus of Rome Died: Rome, Italy in the early days of the Church. Also called Fermo.
  • St. Firmus Died: beheaded c.290.
  • St. Fortunatus of Spoleto Also called Fortunatus of Territet and Fortunato....
  • St. Gaudentius of Ossero Born: in Trzic, Istria (in modern Croatia). Died: 31 May 1044 in Ancona, Italy. Patronage: Osor, Croatia and Cres, Croatia. Also called Gaudentius Auxerensis.
  • St. Giovanni Battista Scalabrini Born: 8 July 1839 at Fino Mornasco, Como, Italy. Died: dawn on the feast of the Ascension. Canonized: 9 October 2022 by Pope Francis. Patronage: Scalabrinians. Also called John Baptist Scalabrini, Apostle of the Catechism, and Father of Migrants.
  • St. Giuse Túc Born: c.1843 in Hoàng Xá, Bac Ninh, Vietnam. Also called Joseph Tuc.
  • St. Gratian of Perugia Died: c.250 at Perugia, Italy. Also called Gratianus.
  • St. Hannibal Mary di Francia Born: 5 July 1851 at Messina, Italy. Died: 1 June 1927 at Messina, Italy. Canonized: 16 May 2004 by Pope John Paul II. Also called Annibale Maria di Francia and Hannibal di Francia.
  • St. Iñigo of Oña Born: 11th century at Bilbao, Spain. Died: 1 June 1057 at the monastery at Oña, Spain. Canonized: 1259 by Pope Alexander IV. Also called Eñeco, Eñecone.
  • St. Ischyrion Born: Egyptian. Died: impaled c.250 at Alexandria, Egypt. Also called Ischirion, Ischirione.
  • St. Juventius Died: Rome, Italy.
  • St. Medulph of Thiers Died: 1 June 430. Also called Medulfo, Mion.
  • St. Melosa Died: Thessalonica.
  • St. Pamphilus of Alexandria Born: Berytus, Phoenicia. Died: beheaded in 309 in Alexandria, Egypt.
  • St. Paulus of Autun Born: 3rd century Italy. Died: beheaded c.273 in Autun, France. Also called Paul, Paolo.
  • St. Porphyrius of Alexandria Died: beheaded in 309 in Alexandria, Egypt.
  • St. Proculus of Bologna Died: 542. Also kept on 12 July in some calendars.
  • St. Proculus the Soldier Died: crucified c.304 in Bologna, Italy. Also called Procolo.
  • St. Reverianus of Autun Born: 3rd century Italy. Died: beheaded c.273 in Autun, France. Also called Reverentianus, Reveriano, Reverie, Rivianus.
  • St. Ronan of Cornwall Born: in Cornwall, England. Died: 6th century in Brittany (in modern France). Patronage: Laurenan, France, Locronan, France, Quimper, France, and Saint-Renan. Also called Ronan of Locronan, Ronan of Quimper, Ronan the Silent, and Renan, Ronanus, Ruadan, Rumon, Ruadhan, Ruan.
  • St. Secundus of Amelia Died: drowned in 304 in the River Tiber at Amelia, Italy.
  • St. Seleucus of Alexandria Born: Cappadocia. Died: beheaded in 309 in Alexandria, Egypt.
  • St. Simeon of Syracuse Born: in Syracuse, Sicily. Died: in 1035 in Trier, Germany. Canonized: 1042 by Pope Benedict IX. Also called Simeon of Trier and Symeon, Simeone.
  • St. Telga of Denbighshire Also called Tegla, Thecla.
  • St. Thecla of Antioch
  • St. Thespesius of Cappadocia Died: 230 in Cappadocia (in modern Turkey).
  • St. Wystan of Evesham Died: murdered on 1 June 849 at Wistanstow, England. Patronage: Evesham, England and Repton, England. Also called Wystan of Mercia.
  • St. Zosimus of Antioch

Blessed (12)

  • Blessed Alfonso Navarrete-Benito Born: 21 September 1571 in Logroño, Spain. Died: beheaded on 1 June 1617 in Koguchi, Omura, Nagasaki, Japan. Beatified: 7 May 1867 by Blessed Pope Pius IX. Also called Alfonsus Navarrete, Alphonso Navarrete, and Alphonsus de Mena.
  • Blessed Alphonsus de Mena Born: 3 February 1568 in La Rioja, Logroño, Spain. Died: burned alive on 10 September 1622 in Nishizaka, Nagasaki, Japan. Beatified: 7 May 1867 by Pope Blessed Pius IX. Also called Alonso de Mena Navarette and Alonso de Mena Navarro.
  • Blessed Arnald Arench Born: 14th century France. Died: beaten to death in 1394 in Granada, Spain.
  • Blessed Arnold of Geertruidenberg Also called Arnoldus.
  • Blessed Conrad of Hesse Born: c.1200 in Herlesheim, Upper Hesse, Germany. Died: 3 August 1270. Also called Conrad of Herlesheim, Conrad of Haina, Conrad of Hessen, and Konrad....
  • Blessed Ferdinand Ayala Born: 1575 in Ballesteros de Calatrava, Ciudad Real, Spain. Died: beheaded on 1 June 1617 in Koguchi, Omura, Nagasaki, Japan. Beatified: 7 May 1867 by Pope Blessed Pius IX.
  • Blessed Gaius Xeymon Born: Japanese. Died: 17 August 1627 at Nagasaki, Japan. Beatified: 7 May 1867 by Pope Blessed Pius IX. Also called Caius.
  • Blessed Jean-Baptiste-Ignace-Pierre Vernoy de Montjournal Born: 17 November 1736 in Molins, Allier, France. Died: 1 June 1794 aboard the prison galley Deux-Associés in port at Rochefort, Charente-Maritime, France. Beatified: 1 October 1995 by Pope John Paul II.
  • Blessed John Pelingotto Born: 1240 at Urbino, Italy. Died: 1 June 1304 in Urbino, Italy. Beatified: 13 November 1918 by Pope Benedict XV (cultus confirmed). Also called John Pelino Goto and Giovanni Pelino Goto.
  • Blessed John Storey Born: 1504 in northern England. Died: hanged. Beatified: 29 December 1886 by Pope Leo XIII (cultus confirmed).
  • Blessed Leo Tanaka Born: c.1590 in Omi, Japan. Died: beheaded on 1 June 1617 on a rock near Omura, Nagasaki, Japan. Beatified: 7 May 1867 by Pope Blessed Pius IX. Also called Leo Tanaca and Leone....
  • Blessed Theobald Roggeri Born: late 11th century in Vico, Liguria, Piedmont, Italy. Died: 1150. Beatified: 1841 by Pope Gregory XVI (cultus confirmation). Patronage: against fever, against sterility, church cleaners, cobblers, shoemakers, and porters. Also called Theobald of Vico, Theobald of Alba, and Theobald Roggeris.

Martyr groups (6)

  • Martyrs of Alexandria Died: beheaded in 249 at Alexandria, Egypt.
  • Martyrs of Caesarea Died: 309 at Caesarea, Palestine.
  • Martyrs of Lycopolis Died: Lycopolis, Egypt.
  • Martyrs of Rome Died: Rome, Italy.
  • Martyrs of Saddi Died: at Pieve de' Saddi, Pietralunga, Italy.
  • Martyrs of Thessalonica Died: in Thessalonica, Greece.

Other (2)

  • Hildegard Brujan
  • Notre-Dame du Laus
