Saints of 18 June

42 saints, blessed, and feasts kept on 18 June, with short facts.

Saints (28)

  • St. Abraham of Clermont Born: 5th century Syria. Died: 472.
  • St. Alena of Dilbeek Born: near Brussels, Belgium. Died: martyred c.640. Also called Alena of Forest and Elena.
  • St. Amandus of Bordeaux Born: latter 4th century. Died: c.431 of natural events. Also called Amand, Amantius, Amatius.
  • St. Arcontius of Brioude Also called Arconce, Arcons.
  • St. Calogero of Sicily Born: c.466 in Chalcedon, Thrace. Died: c.561 at Monte Cronios, Sciacca, Sicily. Also called Calogerus.
  • St. Calogerus of Fragalata Died: at Fragalata, Sicily. Patronage: Fragalata, Sicily.
  • St. Calogerus the Anchorite Born: Greece. Died: c.486. Also called Calogero the Anchorite and Calogerus the Anchoret.
  • St. Colman mac Mici
  • St. Cyriacus of Málaga Died: stoned to death in 305 in Málaga, Spain.
  • St. Demetrius of Fragalata Died: at Fragalata, Sicily. Patronage: Fragalata, Sicily.
  • St. Edith of Aylesbury Died: c.650. Also called Edith of Bicester.
  • St. Elizabeth of Schönau Born: 1126 in Bingen, Germany. Died: 18 June 1164 at Bingen, Germany. Canonized: never formally canonized, but popular devotion went on for centuries. Patronage: against temptations. Also called Elizabeth of Sconauge and Elisabeth of....
  • St. Elpidius of Brioude Died: Brioude, France. Also called Elpida, Elpidia, Helpidius.
  • St. Equizio of Telese Also called Equizio the Deacon.
  • St. Erasmo
  • St. Etherius of Nicomedia Died: 304 in Nicomedia (in modern Turkey). Also called Aetherius of Nicomedia.
  • St. Fortunatus the Philosopher Died: c.569.
  • St. Gerland
  • St. Gregory Barbarigo Born: 16 September 1625 at Venice, Italy as Gregorio Giovanni Gasparo Barbarigo. Died: 18 June 1697 at Padua, Italy. Beatified: 6 July 1771 by Pope Clement XIV. Canonized: 26 May 1960 by Blessed Pope John XXIII.
  • St. Gregory of Fragalata Died: at Fragalata, Sicily. Patronage: Fragalata, Sicily.
  • St. Guy of Baume Died: c.940.
  • St. Marcellian Died: nailed to a post and pierced. Also called Marcellianus.
  • St. Marina of Alexandria Died: Alexandria, Egypt. Also called Maria, Marianus.
  • St. Marina of Bithynia Died: c.750.
  • St. Mark Died: nailed to a post and pierced. Also called Marcus.
  • St. Osanna of Northumbria Born: 7th century England. Died: 8th century England.
  • St. Osmanna of Jouarre Died: c.700. Also called Osanna.
  • St. Paula of Málaga Died: stoned to death in 305 in Málaga, Spain.

Blessed (5)

  • Blessed Euphemia of Altenmünster Born: 12th century Bavaria, Germany. Died: 18 June c.1180 in Altomünster abbey in Dachau, Upper Bavaria (in modern Germany). Also called Eufemia.
  • Blessed Jerome of Vallumbrosa Died: 1135.
  • Blessed Marina of Spoleto Born: early 13th century Spoleto, Italy as Vallarina Petruccini. Died: 18 June 1300.
  • Blessed Osanna Andreasi Born: 17 January 1449 at Mantua, Italy. Died: 1505. Beatified: 24 November 1694 by Pope Leo X and Pope Innocent XII (cultus confirmed). Patronage: school girls, Prayers, and General Calendar of the Order of Preachers.
  • Blessed Peter Sanchez Died: 1503 in Valensia, Spain.

Martyr groups (3)

  • Martyrs of Ravenna Died: Ravenna, Italy.
  • Martyrs of Rome Died: Rome, Italy.
  • Martyrs of Tripoli Born: Greek. Died: c.135 at Tripoli, Phoenicia (in modern Lebanon).

Other (6)

  • Baithan of Dunleer
  • Cadwgan of Bangor
  • Furudran of Dunleer
  • Hermann Floreffe
  • Hermits of Karden Born: Aquitaine (in modern France). Canonized: 12 August 1908 by Pope Pius X (cultus confirmation).
  • Hosanna Howden
