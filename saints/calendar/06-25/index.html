Saints of 25 June

31 saints, blessed, and feasts kept on 25 June, with short facts.

Marian titles (1)

  • Our Lady of Grace

Saints (20)

  • St. Adalbert of Egmond Born: in Northumbria, England. Died: c.740. Also called Adelbert, Aedelbert, Aedelbertus.
  • St. Amand of Coly Died: 6th century. Also called Amandus, Amantius, Amatius.
  • St. Cyneburga of Gloucester Also called Cyniburg, Kineburga.
  • St. Domingo Henares de Zafra Cubero Born: 19 December 1765 at Baena, Córdoba, Spain. Died: beheaded on 25 June 1838 in Nam Ðinh, Vietnam. Canonized: 19 June 1988 by Pope John Paul II. Also called Dominic Henares and Domingo Henares.
  • St. Eurosia Died: 714. Canonized: 1902 by Pope Leo XIII (cultus confirmation). Patronage: against bad weather and diocese of Jaca, Spain. Also called Orosia.
  • St. Febronia of Nisibis Born: 3rd century. Died: 304. Also called Febronia of Sebapte.
  • St. Gallicanus of Embrun Died: c.540.
  • St. Gallicanus of Ostia Died: c.362.
  • St. Gohard of Nantes Died: 843.
  • St. Luceias Died: c.260 in Rome, Italy. Also called Lucy.
  • St. Maximus of Turin Born: c.380 at Rhaetia (in modern Switzerland). Died: c.466. Patronage: Turin, Italy.
  • St. Moloc of Mortlach Born: c.530 in northern Ireland. Died: 25 June 592 in Rosemarkie, Scotland. Canonized: 1898 by Pope Leo XIII (cultus confirmation). Patronage: against mental illness and Argyll, Scotland.
  • St. Molonachus of Lismore
  • St. Phanxicô Ðo Van Chieu Born: c.1797 at Trung Le, Liên Thùy, Nam Ðinh, Vietnam. Died: beheaded on 25 June 1838 in Nam Ðinh, Vietnam. Canonized: 19 June 1988 by Pope John Paul II. Also called Francis Do Minh Chieu, Francesco Do Minh Chieu, and Martyrs of Vietnam.
  • St. Prosper of Reggio Born: c.403 at Aquitaine (in modern France). Died: c.460. Patronage: Reggio Emilia, Italy and Romano Canavese, Italy. Also called Prosper of Aquitaine and Prospero, Tiro.
  • St. Selyf of Cornwall Also called Levan, Levin, Selevan, Selyr, Silvanus.
  • St. Solomon I Born: Cornwall, England. Died: 5th century Brittany, France.
  • St. Solomon III of Bretagne Died: 25 June 874. Also called Solomon III of Brittany and Selyf of....
  • St. Tigre of Maurienne Born: Valloires, Mauirenne, Gaul (in modern France). Also called Tecla, Thecla, Thècle, Tigride, Tigris.
  • St. William of Vercelli Born: 1085 at Vercelli, Italy. Died: 25 June 1142 at Guglietto, Italy. Patronage: Irpinia, Italy. Also called William of Monte Vergine.

Blessed (7)

  • Blessed Burchard of Mallersdorf Born: 11th century in the Upper Franconia (in modern Bavaria, Germany). Died: 25 June 1122 in Mallersdorf, Bavaria, Germany. Also called Burkhard.
  • Blessed Dorothy of Montau Born: 6 February 1347 at Gross Montau, Prussia. Died: 25 January 1394 at Marienwerder, Kwidzyn, Prussia (in modern Poland). Beatified: 9 January 1976 by Pope Paul VI (cultus confirmed). Also called Dorota z Matowów, Dorothea of Montau, Dorothea Swartz, Dorothea von Montau, and Dorthea von Montau.
  • Blessed Fulgentius de Lara Died: 1287.
  • Blessed Guy Maramaldi Born: at Naples, Italy. Died: 1391. Beatified: 1612 by Pope Paul V (cultus confirmed).
  • Blessed Henry Zdick Died: 1150. Also called Henry of Olomouc and Henry of Olmütz.
  • Blessed John the Spaniard Born: 1123 at Almanza, Spain. Died: 1160. Beatified: 1864 by Pope Pius IX (cultus confirmed). Also called John of Spain.
  • Blessed Paolo Giustiniani Born: 15 June 1476 in Venice, Italy. Died: 25 June 1528 in the hermitage of San Silvestro de Monte Soratte, Rome, Italy. Patronage: Society of Saint Romuald.

Other (3)
