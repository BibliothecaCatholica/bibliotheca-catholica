Saints of 27 June

35 saints, blessed, and feasts kept on 27 June, with short facts.

Marian titles (1)

  • Our Lady of Perpetual Help Patronage: Haiti, archdiocese of Oklahoma City, Oklahoma, diocese of Buxar, India, diocese of Hallam, England, diocese of Leeds, England, diocese of Middlesbrough, England, diocese of Rapid City, South Dakota, diocese of Salina, Kansas, and others.

Saints (22)

  • St. Cyril of Alexandria Born: 376 at Alexandria, Egypt. Died: 444 at Alexandria, Egypt. Patronage: Alexandria, Egypt.
  • St. Adeodato of Naples Died: 671.
  • St. Aedh McLugack Born: Irish.
  • St. Anectus of Caesarea Died: scourged.
  • St. Arialdus of Milan Died: 1066 at a castle on a small island in Lago Maggiore near Milan, Italy. Canonized: 1067 by Pope Alexader II (decree of martyrdom). Also called Arialdo.
  • St. Arianell Born: Gwent, Wales.
  • St. Brogan
  • St. Crescens of Galatia Died: c.100.
  • St. Crescentius of Mainz Born: 4th century in the area of modern Germany. Died: c.406 in Mainz, Germany.
  • St. Desideratus of Gourdon Died: c.569. Also called Désiré, Desert, Didier.
  • St. Dimman Also called Dioman, Diman.
  • St. Felix of Rome Died: Rome, Italy.
  • St. Ferdinand of Aragon Born: 1030 in Aragon, Spain. Died: 27 June 1082 in Alvignano, Italy. Patronage: Alvignano, Italy and Dragoni, Italy. Also called Ferdinand of Caiazzo and Ferdinando of....
  • St. Gudene of Carthage Died: beheaded in Carthage, North Africa (modern Tunis, Tunisia). Also called Guddene.
  • St. Joanna the Myrrhbearer Also kept on 24 May in some calendars.
  • St. John of Chinon Born: in the British Isles. Died: 6th century near Chinon, France. Also called John of Caion, John of Moutier, and John of Tours.
  • St. Ladislas Born: 1040 in Hungary. Died: 1095 in Neutra, Hungary (in modern Slovakia). Canonized: 1192 by Pope Celestine III. Patronage: Szekszard, Hungary. Also called Ladislaus, Lancelot, Laszlo.
  • St. Poma of Châlons-sur-Marne
  • St. Sampson of Constantinople Died: c.530. Also called Sampson Xenodochius, Sampson the Hospitable, Samson..., and Father of the Poor.
  • St. Spinella of Rome Died: Rome, Italy.
  • St. Tôma Toán Born: c.1764 in Can Phán, Nam Ðinh, Vietnam. Died: starved to death on 27 June 1840 in prison in Nam Ðinh, Vietnam. Canonized: 19 June 1988 by Pope John Paul II. Also called Tommaso Toan and Thomas Toan.
  • St. Zoilus of Cordoba Died: c.301 in Cordoba, Spain. Also called Zoilo.

Blessed (5)

  • Blessed Benvenutus of Gubbio Born: 12th-century Gubbio, Italy. Died: 1232 in Corneto, Italy. Beatified: 1697 by Pope Innocent XII (cultus confirmation). Also called Benvenuto.
  • Blessed Daniel of Schönau Born: 12th century Germany. Died: 1218.
  • Blessed Davanzato of Poggibonsi Born: c.1200 in Poggibonsi, Italy. Died: 7 July 1295. Patronage: Barberino Val d'Elsa, Italy.
  • Blessed Louise-Thérèse de Montaignac de Chauvance Born: 14 May 1820 in Le Havre-de-Grâce, Seine Maritime, France. Died: 27 June 1885 in Moulins, Allier, France. Beatified: 4 November 1990 by Pope John Paul II. Patronage: Oblates of the Heart of Jesus.
  • Blessed Marguerite Bays Born: 8 September 1815 in Siviriez, Fribourg, Switzerland. Died: 3pm on Friday 27 June 1879 in Siviriez, Fribourg, Switzerland. Beatified: 29 October 1995 by Pope John Paul II. Canonized: 15 January 2019.
