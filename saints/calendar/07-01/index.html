Saints of 1 July

55 saints, blessed, and feasts kept on 1 July, with short facts.

Feasts (1)

  • Feast of the Most Precious Blood of Our Lord Jesus Christ

Marian titles (1)

  • Our Lady of Calvary

Saints (33)

  • St. Aaron of Caerleon Died: martyred in 303 in Wales.
  • St. Aaron the Patriarch
  • St. Arnulf of Mainz Died: martyred in 1160 at the cloister of Saint Jacob, Mainz, Germany. Also called Arnold von Selenhofen.
  • St. Atilano Cruz Alvarado Born: 5 October 1901 in Ahuetita de Abajo, Teocaltiche, Jalisco, Mexico. Died: shot at dawn on 1 July 1928 in Las Cruces, Cuquío, Jalisco, Mexico. Canonized: 21 May 2000 by Pope John Paul II.
  • St. Calais of Anisola Born: Auvergne, France. Died: 536. Also called Carilefus, Calevisus.
  • St. Carilephus Born: French. Died: c.541. Also called Carileff, Calais, Carileffo, Carilefus.
  • St. Castus of Sinuessa Died: 305 in Sinuessa, Campania, Italy.
  • St. Cewydd Born: Welsh. Died: 6th century.
  • St. Concordius of Toledo Died: c.745. Also called Concordio.
  • St. Cuimmein of Nendrum Died: c.658. Also called Cuimmein of Mahee Island, Cuimmein of Aendruim, and Cummine of....
  • St. Domitian of Lerins Born: c.347 in Rome, Italy. Died: 440 at the Saint Rambert de Joux Abbey. Also called Domitian of Bebron.
  • St. Eparchius of Perigord Born: 504 in Perigord, France. Died: 581. Patronage: diocese of Angouleme, France. Also called Eparchius of Angouleme.
  • St. Eutychius of Umbria Died: 4th century Umbria, Italy. Also called Eutizio, Euticio.
  • St. Fleuret of Estaing Died: c.621 in Estaing (modern Aveyron), France. Patronage: Estaing (modern Aveyron), France. Also called Fleuret of Auvergne.
  • St. Gall of Clermont Born: c.489 at Clermont, Auvergne (in modern France). Died: c.554. Also called Gal.
  • St. Golvinus of Leon Born: in England. Died: at Rennes, France.
  • St. Gwenyth of Cornwall
  • St. Huailu Zhang Born: c.1843 in Zhuketian, Hengshui, Hebei, China. Died: beheaded on 9 July 1900 in Zhuketian, Hengshui, Hebei, China. Canonized: 1 October 2000 by Pope John Paul II. Also called Hoai-Lou Tchang.
  • St. Julius of Caerleon Died: martyred c.305 at Caerleon, Monmouthshire, England.
  • St. Junipero Serra Born: 24 November 1713 at Petra, Spanish Majorca as Miguel Jose Serra. Died: 28 August 1784. Canonized: 23 September 2015 by Pope Francis. Also called Apostle of California and Miguel José Serra Ferrer.
  • St. Justino Orona Madrigal Born: 14 April 1877 in Cuyucapán, Atoyac, Jalisco, Mexico. Died: 1 July 1928 in Las Cruces, Cuquío, Jalisco, Mexico. Canonized: 21 May 2000 by Pope John Paul II.
  • St. Juthware Born: England. Died: 7th century. Also called Aude Wyry, Aed, Iutwara, Juthwara.
  • St. Leonorious of Brittany Born: Wales. Died: c.570. Also called Leonorius, Lunaire.
  • St. Leontius of Autun Died: c.430 in Autun, France. Also called Léonce.
  • St. Martin of Vienne
  • St. Nectarius of Vienne Died: c.449. Also called Nectaire, Nectario, Nettario.
  • St. Nicasius of Jerusalem Born: c.1135 in Sicily, Italy. Died: beheaded 1187 at Acre, Palestine. Canonized: 1305. Patronage: against scrofula and Caccamo, Italy (declared on 31 May 1625). Also called Nicasius Burgio, Nicasius Camuto de Burgio, Nicasius de Burgo, Nicasius of Sicily, and Nicasius the Martyr.
  • St. Oliver Plunkett Born: 30 September 1629 at Loughenew, County Meath, Ireland. Died: hanged. Canonized: 12 October 1975 by Pope Paul VI at Rome, Italy. Patronage: archdiocese of Armagh, Ireland. Also called Oileabhéar Pluincéad.
  • St. Secundinus of Sinuessa Died: 305 in Sinuessa, Campania, Italy.
  • St. Servan of Culross Born: c.500 in Ireland. Died: c.583. Patronage: Orkney Islands. Also called Apostle of West Fife and Sair, Serbán, Serf, Servanus.
  • St. Theobald of Vicenza Died: Vicenza, Italy. Canonized: by Pope Alexander III.
  • St. Theodoric of Mont d'Or Died: 533. Also called Thierri, Thierry.
  • St. Veep Born: 6th century in northern England. Died: 6th century in Cornwall, England. Patronage: Saint Veep, Cornwall, England.

Blessed (12)

  • Blessed Antonio Rosmini-Serbati Born: 24 March 1797 in Rovereto, Austrian Tyrol (modern Trent, Italy). Died: 1 July 1855 in Stresa, Viterbo, Italy. Beatified: 18 November 2007 by Pope Benedict XVI Works.
  • Blessed Assunta Marchetti Born: 15 August 1871 at Lombrici di Camaiore, Lucca, Italy. Died: 1 July 1948 in São Paulo, Brazil. Beatified: 25 October 2014 by Pope Francis.
  • Blessed Elisabeth de Vans Died: 1250. Also called Elisabeth de Wans.
  • Blessed George Beesley Born: c.1562 at The Hill, Goosnargh parish, Lancaster, England. Died: 2 July 1591 in Fleet Street, London, England. Beatified: 22 November 1987 by Pope John Paul II. Also called George Bisley.
  • Blessed Jan Nepomucen Chrzan Born: 25 April 1885 in Gostyczyna, Wielkopolskie, Poland. Died: 1 July 1942 at the concentration camp in Dachau, Bavaria, Germany. Beatified: 13 June 1999 by Pope John Paul II in Warsaw, Poland.
  • Blessed Jean-Baptiste Duverneuil Born: c.1737 in Limoges, Haute-Vienne, France. Died: 1 July 1794. Beatified: 1 October 1995 by Pope John Paul II. Also called Léonard, Giovan Battista Duverneuil, and John Baptist Duverneuil.
  • Blessed Luis Obdulio Navarro Born: 21 June 1950 in Quiriguá, Los Amates, Izabal, Guatemala. Died: 1 July 1981 in Quiriguá, Los Amates, Izabal, Guatemala. Beatified: 27 October 2018 by Pope Francis.
  • Blessed Montfort Scott Born: c.1550 in Suffolk, England. Died: 2 July 1591 in Fleet Street, London, England. Beatified: 22 November 1987 by Pope John Paul II. Also called Montford.
  • Blessed Nazju Falzon Born: 1 July 1813 at Valletta, Malta. Died: 1 July 1865, Valletta, Malta. Beatified: 9 May 2001 by Pope John Paul II. Also called Ignatius Falzon.
  • Blessed Pierre-Yrieix Labrouhe de Laborderie Born: 24 May 1756 in Saint-Yrieix, Haute-Vienne, France. Died: 1 July 1794. Beatified: 1 October 1995 by Pope John Paul II. Also called Peter Aredio Labrouhe de Laborderie and Pietro Aredio Labrouhe de Laborderie.
  • Blessed Thomas Maxfield Born: The Mere, Enville, Staffordshire, England. Died: hanged. Beatified: 15 December 1929 by Pope Pius XI. Also called Thomas Macclesfield and Martyrs of Douai.
  • Blessed Tullio Maruzzo Born: 23 July 1929 in Lapio di Arcugnano, Vicenza, Italy as Marcello Maruzzo. Died: shot in the evening of 1 July 1981 on the road near Los Amates, Guatemala. Beatified: 27 October 2018 by Pope Francis. Also called Marcello Maruzzo.
