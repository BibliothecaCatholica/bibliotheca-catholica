Saints of 9 July

41 saints, blessed, and feasts kept on 9 July, with short facts.

Marian titles (4)

  • Our Lady of Chiquinquirá Patronage: Colombia (1829) and Venezuelan National Guard. Also called La Chinita.
  • Our Lady of Itatí
  • Our Lady of Peace
  • Our Lady of Victories

Saints (17)

  • St. Agrippinus of Autun Died: 538.
  • St. Alexander of Egypt Died: burned to death in a furnace c.363 in Egypt.
  • St. Audax of Thora Died: beheaded c.250 in Rome, Italy.
  • St. Auremondo of Mairé Born: 6th century in Chaunay. Died: c.625. Also called Aurebondo.
  • St. Brictius of Martola Died: c.312.
  • St. Copra of Egypt Died: beheaded c.363 in Egypt. Also called Copres, Copretes.
  • St. Cyril of Gortyna Died: beheaded in 250.
  • St. Everild of Everingham Born: in Wessex, England. Died: c.700. Also called Averil, Everildis.
  • St. Faustina of Rome Died: Rome, Italy.
  • St. Felician of Sicily Also called Feliciano, Felicianus.
  • St. Floriana of Rome Died: Rome, Italy.
  • St. Hérombert of Minden Died: 800.
  • St. Ioachim Hao Born: c.1782 in Zhazuo, Xiuewen, Guizhou, China. Died: strangled on 9 July 1839 at Guiyang, Guizhou, China. Canonized: 1 October 2000 by Pope John Paul II. Also called Joachim Ho, Joakim Hao Kaizhi, and Yajin.
  • St. Mary Hermina Grivot Born: 28 April 1866 in Beaune, France. Died: beheaded on 9 July 1900 at Taiyuanfu, China. Canonized: 1 October 2000 by Pope John Paul II in Rome. Also called Irma Grivot, Maria Ermellina di Gesù, Marie Hermine de Jésus, and Mary Hermina of Jesus.
  • St. Patermutius of Egypt Died: beheaded c.363 in Egypt.
  • St. Paulina Do Coracao Agonizante de Jesus Born: 16 December 1865 in Vigolo Vattaro, Trent, Italy as Amabile Lucia Visintainer. Died: 9 July 1942 at Ipiranga, Brazil. Canonized: 19 May 2002 by Pope John Paul II at Rome, Italy. Patronage: against diabetes and diabetics.
  • St. Veronica Giuliani Born: 1660 at Mercatello, Duchy of Urbino (part of modern Italy) as Ursula Giuliani. Died: 9 July 1727 at Città di Castello, Italy. Beatified: 17 June 1804 by Pope Pius VII. Canonized: 26 May 1839 by Pope Gregory XVI. Also called Ursula Giuliani and Veronica de Julianis.

Blessed (10)

  • Blessed Adrian Fortescue Born: 1476 in Punsborne, Hertfordshire, England. Died: beheaded on 9 July 1539 on Tower Hill, London, England. Beatified: 13 May 1895 by Pope Leo XIII (cultus confirmation).
  • Blessed Catherine of Jesus Died: 1520. Also called Caterina.
  • Blessed Catherine of Santa Chiara Born: 15th century Baeza, Spain. Died: 1514 in Baeza, Spain. Also called Caterina.
  • Blessed Dionysius the Rhetorician Died: 1606.
  • Blessed Fidelis Jerome Chojnacki Born: 1 November 1906 at Lodz, Poland as Jerome Spurinska. Died: 9 July 1942 at the Dachau concentration camp, Bavaria, Germany. Beatified: 13 June 1999 by Pope John Paul II. Also called Fedele Chijnacki, Jerome Spurinska, and Hieronim Chojnacki.
  • Blessed Jane Scopelli Born: 1428 at Reggio d' Emilia, Italy. Died: 1491. Beatified: 1771 by Pope Pius VI (cultus confirmed). Also called Giovanna Scopelli and Jane of Reggio.
  • Blessed Luigi Caburlotto Born: 7 June 1817 in Venice, Italy. Died: 9 July 1897 in Venice, Italy. Beatified: 16 May 2015 by Pope Francis.
  • Blessed Marguerite-Marie-Anne de Rocher Born: 20 January 1755 in Bollène, Vaucluse, France. Died: 9 July 1794 in Orange, Vaucluse, France. Beatified: 10 May 1925 by Pope Pius XI. Also called Sister Marguerite-Marie-Anne of the Angels and Maria Anna Margherita degli Angeli de Rocher.
  • Blessed Marie-Anne-Madeleine de Guilhermier Born: 29 June 1733 in Bollène, Vaucluse, France. Died: 9 July 1794 in Orange, Vaucluse, France. Beatified: 10 May 1925 by Pope Pius XI. Also called Sister Saint Melania.
  • Blessed Marija Petkovic Born: 10 December 1892 at Blato, Korcula, Dubrovnik-Neretva, Croatia as Maria Petkovic. Died: 9 July 1966 in Rome, Italy. Beatified: 6 June 2003 by Pope John Paul II in Croatia. Also called Maria Petkovic, Marija of Jesus Crucified Petkovic, Marija of Jesus Crucified, and Mary of Jesus Crucified.

Martyr groups (4)

  • Franciscan Martyrs of China Died: beheaded on 9 July 1900 at Taiyuanfu, Shanxi, China. Canonized: 1 October 2000 by Pope John Paul II in Rome.
  • Martyrs of Gorkum Died: hanged on 9 July 1572 in Brielle, Zuid-Holland, Netherlands. Canonized: 29 June 1867 by Pope Pius IX. Also called Gorkum Martyrs and Martyrs of Gorcum.
  • Martyrs of Orange Died: guillotined between 6 July and 26 July 1794 at Orange, Vaucluse, France. Beatified: 10 May 1925 by Pope Pius XI. Also called Women Religious of Orange.
  • Martyrs of the Baths Died: c.304.

Other (6)

  • Brictius of Seez
  • Dominic Serrano
  • Eusanius of Furci
  • Four Holy Polish Brothers Born: Poland. Died: 1008.
  • Pontian of Todi
  • Procula of Gannat
