Saints of 10 July

35 saints, blessed, and feasts kept on 10 July, with short facts.

Marian titles (1)

  • Our Lady of Boulogne

Saints (17)

  • St. Amalburga of Mauberge Born: in Brabant, Belgium. Died: 690. Patronage: against arm pain, against bruises, against fever, farmers, fever victims, and Ghent, Belgium. Also called Amalburga of Temse and Amalberga, Amalia, Amelberg, Amelia.
  • St. Anatolia of Thora Died: in 250 at Tabulana, Italy.
  • St. Antôn Nguyen Huu Quynh Born: c.1768 in My Huong, Quang Bình, Vietnam. Died: strangled to death on 10 July 1840 at Ðong Hoi, Quang Bình, Vietnam. Canonized: 19 June 1988 by Pope John Paul II.
  • St. Apollonius of Sardis Born: Sardis, Lydia (in Asia Minor). Died: crucified at Iconium. Also called Apollonio.
  • St. Bianor of Pisidia Died: beheaded in Pisidia, Asia Minor in the early 4th-century.
  • St. Cuán of Airbhre
  • St. Elilantus Died: c.770.
  • St. Etto Born: Ireland. Died: c.670.
  • St. Knud Born: c.1043. Died: murdered in 1086 in the church of Saint Alban on the island of Fünen, Denmark. Canonized: 1101 by Pope Paschal II. Patronage: Denmark.
  • St. Lantfrid Died: c.770.
  • St. Pascharius of Nantes Died: c.680. Also called Pascual, Pasquier.
  • St. Peter Vincioli Born: Perugia, Italy. Died: 1007. Also called Peter of Perugia.
  • St. Phêrô Nguyen Khac Tu Born: c.1808 in Ninh Bình, Gia Long, Vietnam. Died: strangled to death on 10 July 1840 at Ðong Hoi, Quang Bình, Vietnam. Canonized: 19 June 1988 by Pope John Paul II.
  • St. Rufina of Rome and Saint Secunda of Rome Died: martyred in 257 in Rome, Italy.
  • St. Sylvanus of Pisidia Died: beheaded in Pisidia, Asia Minor in the early 4th-century.
  • St. Victoria Died: stabbed through the heart in 250 by the executioner Liliarcus at Tabulana, Italy. Patronage: against earthquakes, against lightning, against severe weather, and 18 cities.
  • St. Waltram Died: c.770.

Blessed (8)

  • Blessed Arnold of Camerino Born: Italian.
  • Blessed Euménios Died: 1905 at the Koudomia monastery on Crete.
  • Blessed Faustino Villanueva y Villanueva Born: 15 February 1931 in Yesa, Navarra, Spain. Died: 10 July 1980 in Joyabaj, Quiché, Guatemala. Beatified: 23 April 2021 by Pope Francis.
  • Blessed Marie-Gertrude de Ripert d'Alauzier Born: 15 November 1757 in Bollène, Vaucluse, France. Died: 10 July 1794 in Orange, Vaucluse, France. Beatified: 10 May 1925 by Pope Pius XI. Also called Sister Saint Sophia.
  • Blessed Nicholas Spira Born: 1484 in Brussels, Belgium. Died: 10 July 1568.
  • Blessed Pacificus Born: c.1162 in the Marches of Ancona, Italy. Died: c.1234 at the convent of Lens, Pas-de-Calais, France. Also called Pacific, Pacifico.
  • Blessed Parthenios Died: 1905 at the Koudomia monastery on Crete.
  • Blessed Sylvie-Agnès de Romillon Born: 15 March 1750 in Bollène, Vaucluse, France. Died: 10 July 1794 in Orange, Vaucluse, France. Beatified: 10 May 1925 by Pope Pius XI. Also called Sister Agnès of Jesus.

Martyr groups (6)

  • Martyrs of Africa
  • Martyrs of Antioch Died: Antioch.
  • Martyrs of Damascus Died: cut to pieces on 9-10 July 1860 in Damascus, Syria. Beatified: 10 October 1926 by Pope Pius XI.
  • Martyrs of Nicopolis Died: c.329 in Nicopolis, Armenia (modern Koyulhisar, Turkey).
  • Martyrs of Nitria Died: 4th century in Nitria, Egypt. Also called Fathers of Nitria.
  • Martyrs of Tomis Died: in Tomis, Scythia Minor (modern Constanta, Romania).

Other (3)

  • Antonios Petsjerskij
  • Bernard of Quintavalle
  • Seven Holy Brothers Patronage: Abbey of Badia di Cava, Italy.
