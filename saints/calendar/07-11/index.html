Saints of 11 July

49 saints, blessed, and feasts kept on 11 July, with short facts.

Feasts (1)

  • Dedication of the Cathedral of Puy

Saints (28)

  • St. Benedict of Nursia Born: c.480, Narsia, Umbria, Italy. Died: 21 March 547. Canonized: 1220 by Pope Honorius III. Patronage: against erysipelas, against fever, against gall stones, against inflammatory diseases, against kidney disease, against nettle rash, against poison, against temptations, and others.
  • St. Abundius of Ananelos Died: beheaded in 854 at Cordoba, Spain. Also called Abundius of Cordoba and Abbondio....
  • St. Amabilis of Rouen Died: c.634.
  • St. Anna An Jiaoshi Born: c.1874 in Anping, Hebei, China. Died: beheaded on 11 July 1900 in Liugongying, Shenzhou, Hebei, China. Canonized: 1 October 2000 by Pope John Paul II.
  • St. Anna An Xingshi Born: c.1828 in Anping, Hebei, China. Died: beheaded on 11 July 1900 in Liugongying, Shenzhou, Hebei, China. Canonized: 1 October 2000 by Pope John Paul II.
  • St. Basinus of Poitiers Also called Basino.
  • St. Berthevin of Lisieux Born: 10th century in the area of modern Lisieux, France. Died: stabbed. Also called Berthevin of Laval, Berthevin of Parigny, Berthevin of Vicoin, and Berthevin of Val-Guidon.
  • St. Cindeus Died: burned at the stake c.300. Also called Cindée.
  • St. Cowair Patronage: Llangower, Wales. Also called Cywair.
  • St. Cyprian of Brescia Also kept on 21 April in some calendars.
  • St. Cyriacus the Executioner Died: beheaded.
  • St. Drostan of Dier Born: 6th century Scotland. Died: 7th century. Patronage: Dier, Scotland. Also called Drostan of Deer.
  • St. Hidulf of Moyenmoutier Born: Regensburg, Germany. Died: 707.
  • St. Januarius Died: beheaded in 320 at Nicopolis, Lesser Armenia.
  • St. John of Bergamo Died: 681.
  • St. Leontius the Younger Born: c.510. Died: 565. Also called Leontius II and Leoncio, Leonzio.
  • St. Marcian of Lycaonia Died: 243 in Iconium, Lycaonia, Asia Minor. Also called Marcian of Iconium and Marciano.
  • St. Marciana of Caesarea Born: Russucur, Mauritania (modern Dellys, Algeria).
  • St. Maria An Guoshi Born: c.1836 in Anping, Hebei, China. Died: beheaded on 11 July 1900 in Liugongying, Shenzhou, Hebei, China. Canonized: 1 October 2000 by Pope John Paul II. Also called Mali.
  • St. Maria An Linghua Born: c.1871 in Anping, Hebei, China. Died: beheaded on 11 July 1900 in Liugongying, Shenzhou, Hebei, China. Canonized: 1 October 2000 by Pope John Paul II. Also called Mali.
  • St. Olga of Kiev Born: 879 at Pskov, Russia. Died: 11 July 969 in Kiev, Ukraine. Patronage: converts, widows, and archeparchy of Winnipeg, Manitoba. Also called Olga Prekrasa, Olga the Beauty, and Helena, Helga, Olha.
  • St. Pelagia Died: beheaded in 320 at Nicopolis, Lesser Armenia.
  • St. Placid of Dissentis Died: murdered for defending the ecclesiastical rights of the abbey. Canonized: 1905 (cultus confirmed). Also called Placido.
  • St. Sabinus of Brescia Also called Savinus, Savino.
  • St. Sabinus of Poitiers Died: 5th century near Poitiers, France.
  • St. Sidronius Died: c.270 in Rome, Italy.
  • St. Sigisbert of Dissentis Canonized: 1905 (cultus confirmed). Also called Sigebert.
  • St. Thurketyl Born: 887. Died: 975. Also called Turketil.

Popes (1)

  • Pope St. Pius I Born: at Aquileia, Italy. Died: c.155.

Blessed (11)

  • Blessed Antonio Muller
  • Blessed Bertrand of Grand-Selve Died: 11 July 1149 in the monastery of Grandselve, Toulouse, France.
  • Blessed Kjeld of Viborg Died: c.1151 in Viborg, Denmark. Beatified: 1189 by Bishop Absalon and Pope Clement III (cultus confirmation). Patronage: Viborg, Denmark. Also called Ketil, Ketille, Kield.
  • Blessed Marie-Clotilde Blanc Born: 17 January 1742 in Bollène, Vaucluse, France. Died: guillotined on 11 July 1794 in Orange, Vaucluse, France. Beatified: 10 May 1925 by Pope Pius XI. Also called Sister Saint Martin.
  • Blessed Marie-Elisabeth Pélissier Born: 15 April 1741 in Bollène, Vaucluse, France. Died: guillotined on 11 July 1794 in Orange, Vaucluse, France. Beatified: 10 May 1925 by Pope Pius XI. Also called Sister Théotiste of the Blessed Sacrament.
  • Blessed Marie-Marguerite de Barbégie d'Albrède Born: 18 October 1740 in Saint Laurent de Carnols, Gard, France. Died: guillotined on 11 July 1794 in Orange, Vaucluse, France. Beatified: 10 May 1925 by Pope Pius XI. Also called Sister Saint Sophia.
  • Blessed Nectaire of Sainte-Anne Born: Ephesus. Died: beheaded in 1820 in Ephesus.
  • Blessed Rosalie-Clotilde Bes Born: 30 June 1753 in Beaume-de-Transit, Drôme, France. Died: guillotined on 11 July 1794 in Orange, Vaucluse, France. Beatified: 10 May 1925 by Pope Pius XI. Also called Sister Saint Pelagia of Saint John the Baptist.
  • Blessed Thomas Hunt Born: c.1573 in Norfolk, England. Died: hanged. Beatified: 22 November 1987 by Pope John Paul II. Also called Thomas Benstead.
  • Blessed Thomas Sprott Born: c.1571 in Skelsmergh, Cumbria, England. Died: early July 1600 in Lincoln, Lincolnshire, England. Beatified: 22 November 1987 by Pope John Paul II. Also called Thomas Parker.
  • Blessed Valeriu Traian Frentiu Born: 25 April 1875 in Resita, Caras-Severin, Romania. Died: 11 July 1952 in Sighetu Marmatiei, Maramures, Romania. Beatified: 2 June 2019 by Pope Francis.
