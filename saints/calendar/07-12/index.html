Saints of 12 July

40 saints, blessed, and feasts kept on 12 July, with short facts.

Feasts (1)

  • Dedication of the Cathedral of Coutances

Saints (28)

  • St. Leo of Cava Born: c.990 in Lucca, Tuscany, Italy. Died: 1079 at the monastery of the Holy Trinity in La Cava de Tirreni at Salerno, Italy. Canonized: 1579 by Pope Gregory XIII (cultus confirmation). Also called Leo I. Date differs between sources; also kept on 10 November.
  • St. Agnes De Born: c.1781 at Ba Den, Tranh Hóa, Vietnam. Died: 12 July 1841 in prison at Ninh Binh, Vietnam. Canonized: 19 June 1988 by Pope John Paul II. Also called Anê Lê Thi Thành, Bà Ðê, and Martyrs of Vietnam.
  • St. Andreas the Soldier
  • St. Ansbald of Prüm Born: Luxembourg. Died: 886.
  • St. Balay Also called Bachla.
  • St. Clemente Ignacio Delgado Cebrián Born: 23 November 1761 in Villafeliche, Zaragoza, Spain. Died: 12 July 1838 in Nam Ðinh, Vietnam. Canonized: 19 June 1988 by Pope John Paul II. Also called Ignacio Clemente, Ignatius, Klemens Ignatius.
  • St. Epiphana Also called Epiphania.
  • St. Faustus the Soldier
  • St. Felix of Milan Died: c.304 in Milan, Italy.
  • St. Fortunatus of Aquileia Died: beheaded c.66. Patronage: Udine, Italy and Aquileia, Italy.
  • St. Harduin of Fontenelle Born: 749 in Alvimare. Died: 12 July 811 at Fontenelle, France. Also called Arduin.
  • St. Hermagorus of Aquileia Died: beheaded c.66. Patronage: Aquileia, Italy, Hermagor-Pressegger See, Austria, and Udine, Italy. Also called Ermacora, Ermagora, Hermagoras, Hermagoro, Mohor.
  • St. Hilarion of Ancyra Died: 115 at Ancyra, Galatia.
  • St. Jason of Tarsus Also called Mnason, Nason.
  • St. John Gualbert Born: c.985 at Florence, Italy. Died: 1073 at Passignano near Florence, Italy. Canonized: 1193 by Pope Celestine III. Patronage: forest workers, foresters, park services, parks, and Florence, Italy. Also called Giovanni Gualberto.
  • St. John Jones Born: 1559 in Clynog-Fawr, Carnarvonshire, Wales. Died: hanged. Canonized: 25 October 1970 by Pope Paul VI. Also called Godefride Moritius, Godefridus Mauritius, Godfrey Maurice, Griffith Jones, and Gryffith Jones.
  • St. John the Georgian Born: at Georgia near the Black Sea. Died: c.1002 at Mount Athos. Also called John the Iberian, John the Hagiorite, and John Iweron.
  • St. Menas the Soldier
  • St. Menulphus of Quimper Born: Ireland. Died: near Bourges, France. Also called Menou, Menuiphus.
  • St. Nabor of Milan Died: c.304 in Milan, Italy.
  • St. Paternian of Bologna Died: c.470.
  • St. Paulinus of Antioch Born: Antioch, Syria. Died: martyred c.67. Patronage: Lucca, Italy. Also called Paulinus of Lucca and Paulino of....
  • St. Phêrô Khan Born: c.1780 in Hòa Hue, Nghe An, Vietnam. Died: beheaded on 12 July 1842 in Hà Tinh, Vietnam. Canonized: 19 June 1988 by Pope John Paul II.
  • St. Proclus of Ancyra Died: Ancyra, Asia Minor (in modern Turkey). Also called Proclo.
  • St. Proculus of Bologna Died: 542. Also kept on 1 June in some calendars.
  • St. Uguzo of Carvagna Patronage: cheese makers. Also called Lucio, Lucius, Luguzzone, Uguzon, Uguzzone.
  • St. Veronica Patronage: against bleeding or hemorraghes, domestic workers, dying people, laundry workers, linen weavers, maids, parsonage or rectory housekeepers, photographers, and others. Also called Berenice.
  • St. Viventiolus of Lyons Died: 524. Also called Juventiole, Viventiole, Vivenziolo.

Blessed (8)

  • Blessed David Gonson Died: hanged. Beatified: 15 December 1929 by Pope Pius XI. Also called David Gunston.
  • Blessed Jeanne-Marie de Romillon Born: 12 July 1753 in Bollène, Vaucluse, France. Died: guillotined on 12 July 1794 in Orange, Vaucluse, France. Beatified: 10 May 1925 by Pope Pius XI. Also called Sister Saint Bernard.
  • Blessed Lambert of Cîteaux Died: 1163 in Morimond Abbey, Parnoy-en-Bassigny, France.
  • Blessed Louis Martin Born: 22 August 1823 in Bordeaux, Gironde, France. Died: 29 July 1894 in Arnières-sur-Iton, Eure, France. Beatified: 19 October 2008 by Pope Benedict XVI. Canonized: 18 October 2015 by Pope Francis during the Synod on the Family.
  • Blessed Madeleine-Thérèse Talieu Born: 13 September 1746 in Bollène, Vaucluse, France. Died: guillotined on 12 July 1794 in Orange, Vaucluse, France. Beatified: 10 May 1925 by Pope Pius XI. Also called Sister Rose of Saint Xavier.
  • Blessed Marguerite-Eléonore de Justamond Born: 12 January 1746 in Bollène, Vaucluse, France. Died: guillotined on 12 July 1794 in Orange, Vaucluse, France. Beatified: 10 May 1925 by Pope Pius XI. Also called Sister Marie of Saint Henry.
  • Blessed Marie-Azélie Guérin Martin Born: 23 December 1831 in Saint-Denis-sur-Sarthon, Orne, France. Died: 28 August 1877 in Alençon, Orne, France. Beatified: Mission Sunday, 19 October 2008 by Pope Benedict XVI. Canonized: 18 October 2015 by Pope Francis during the Synod on the Family.
  • Blessed Marie Cluse Born: 5 December 1761 in Bouvante, Drôme, France. Died: guillotined on 12 July 1794 in Orange, Vaucluse, France. Beatified: 10 May 1925 by Pope Pius XI. Also called Maria Cluse, Marta of the Good Angel, Marta dell'Angelo Buono Cluse, and Sister Marthe of the Good Angel.

Martyr groups (1)

  • Martyrs of Nagasaki Died: 12 July 1626 in Nagasaki, Japan. Beatified: 7 May 1867 by Pope Blessed Pius IX.

Other (2)

  • Bertha the Queen
  • Colman of Clonpriest
