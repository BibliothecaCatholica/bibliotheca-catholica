Saints of 13 July

49 saints, blessed, and feasts kept on 13 July, with short facts.

Marian titles (2)

  • Our Lady of Grace of Valsorda
  • Our Lady of Soccorso

Saints (17)

  • St. Arno of Würzburg Born: 9th century. Died: 13 July 892 at Chemnitz, Saxony (in modern Germany).
  • St. Clelia Barbieri Born: 13 February 1847 at Bundrie di San Giovanni, Persiceto, Italy. Died: 13 July 1870 at Bologna, Italy. Canonized: 9 April 1989 by Pope John Paul II. Patronage: Little Sisters of the Mother of Sorrows and people ridiculed for their piety. Also called Cloelia Barbieri.
  • St. Dogfan Died: 5th century in Dyfed, Wales. Also called Doewan.
  • St. Emanuele Lê Van Phung Born: c.1796 in Ðau Nuoc, Cù Lao Giêng, Vietnam. Died: beheaded on 13 July 1859 in Châu Ðoc, An Giang, Vietnam. Canonized: 19 June 1988 by Pope John Paul II. Also called Emmanuel Le Van Phung and Martyrs of Vietnam.
  • St. Eugene of Carthage Died: 505 in Albi, Italy. Also called Eugenius.
  • St. Giustina of Arzano Patronage: Arzano, Italy and unmarried girls (in Torre d'Arese, Italy).
  • St. Henry II Born: 6 May 972 at Albach, Hildesheim, Bavaria, Germany. Died: 13 July 1024 at Pfalz Grona. Canonized: 1146 by Pope Blessed Eugene III. Also called Good King Henry and Heinrich, Duke of Bavaria.
  • St. Iosephus Wang Kuiju Born: c.1863 in Nangong, Jizhou, Hebei, China. Died: 13 July 1900 in Nangong, Jizhou, Hebei, China. Canonized: 1 October 2000 by Pope John Paul II. Also called Joseph Wang Guiji, Ruose, and Martyrs of China.
  • St. Mildred of Thanet Died: c.700. Canonized: 1388 by Pope Urban VI. Also called Mildred of Minster and Mildthryth....
  • St. Muritta of Carthage Died: 505.
  • St. Myrope Born: Chios, Greece. Died: c.251 in prison.
  • St. Paulus Liu Jinde Born: c.1821 in Lanziqiao, Hengshui, Hebei, China. Died: 13 July 1900 in Lanziqiao, Hengshui, Hebei, China. Canonized: 1 October 2000 by Pope John Paul II. Also called Baolu, Paul.
  • St. Salutaris of Carthage Died: 505.
  • St. Sarra of Egypt
  • St. Serapion of Alexandria Died: burned alive c.248 in Alexandria, Egypt. Also called Serapione.
  • St. Serapion of Macedonia Died: burned alive c.195.
  • St. Turiaf of Dol Born: in Brittany, France. Died: c.750.

Blessed (15)

  • Blessed Anne-Andrée Minutte Born: 4 February 1740 in Sérignan, Vaucluse, France. Died: guillotined on 13 July 1794 in Orange, Vaucluse, France. Beatified: 10 May 1925 by Pope Pius XI. Also called Sister Saint Alexis.
  • Blessed Barthélemy Jarrige de La Morelie de Biars Born: 18 March 1753 in Moutier, Haute-Vienne, France. Died: 13 July 1794 aboard the prison ship Deux-Associés. Beatified: 1 October 1995 by Pope John Paul II.
  • Blessed Berthold of Scheide Born: 12th century near Lübeck, Schleswig-Holstein (in modern Germany). Died: c.1214. Also called Berthold of Scheda.
  • Blessed Carlos Manuel Cecilio Rodriguez Santiago Born: 22 November 1918 at Caguas, Puerto Rico. Died: 13 July 1963. Beatified: 29 April 2001 by Pope John Paul II.
  • Blessed Élisabeth Verchière Born: 2 January 1769 in Bollène, Vaucluse, France. Died: guillotined on 13 July 1794 in Orange, Vaucluse, France. Beatified: 10 May 1925 by Pope Pius XI. Also called Madeleine of the Mother of God.
  • Blessed Ferdinando Maria Baccilieri Born: 14 May 1821 in Campodoso (modern Modena), Italy. Died: 13 July 1893. Beatified: 3 October 1999 by Pope John Paul II.
  • Blessed James of Voragine Born: c.1226 at Varazze (modern Voragine). Died: 13 July 1298 in Genoa, Italy. Beatified: 11 May 1816 by Pope Pius VII (cultus confirmation). Also called James of Varazze, James of Viraggio, James of Genoa, and Giacomo, Jacob, Jacobus, Jacopo.
  • Blessed Jean of France Died: 1401.
  • Blessed Louis-Armand-Joseph Adam Born: 19 December 1741 in Rouen, Seine-Maritime, France. Died: 13 July 1794 aboard the prison ship Deux-Associés. Beatified: 1 October 1995 by Pope John Paul II.
  • Blessed Mariano de Jesus Eues Hoyos Born: 14 October 1845 at Yarumal. Died: 13 July 1926 at Angostura, Antioquia, Colombia. Beatified: 9 April 2000 by Pope John Paul II. Patronage: against cancer. Also called Padre Marianito.
  • Blessed Marie-Anastasie de Roquard Born: 5 October 1749 in Bollène, Vaucluse, France. Died: guillotined on 13 July 1794 in Orange, Vaucluse, France. Beatified: 10 May 1925 by Pope Pius XI. Also called Sister Saint Gervase.
  • Blessed Marie-Anne Depeyre Born: 2 October 1756 in Tulette, Drôme, France. Died: guillotined on 13 July 1794 in Orange, Vaucluse, France. Beatified: 10 May 1925 by Pope Pius XI. Also called Saint Saint Frances.
  • Blessed Marie-Anne Lambert Born: 17 August 1742 in Pierrelatte, Drôme, France. Died: guillotined on 13 July 1794 in Orange, Vaucluse, France. Beatified: 10 May 1925 by Pope Pius XI. Also called Sister Saint Francis.
  • Blessed Thérèse-Henriette Faurie Born: 13 February 1770 in Sérignan, Vaucluse, France. Died: guillotined on 13 July 1794 in Orange, Vaucluse, France. Beatified: 10 May 1925 by Pope Pius XI. Also called Marie of the Annunciation.
  • Blessed Thomas Tunstal Born: Norwich, Norforlk, England. Died: hanged on 13 July 1616 in Tyburn, London, England. Beatified: 15 December 1929 by Pope Pius XI. Also called Thomas Helmes and Thomas Dyer.
