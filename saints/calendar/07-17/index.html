Saints of 17 July

30 saints, blessed, and feasts kept on 17 July, with short facts.

Saints (19)

  • St. Alexius of Rome Died: early 5th century. Patronage: Alexians, beggars, belt makers, nurses, pilgrims, and travellers. Also called Alexis of Rome, Alexis the Beggar, and The Man of God.
  • St. Andrew Zorard Born: c.980 in Opatowiec, Poland. Died: c.1010. Canonized: 1085 by Pope Saint Gregory VII. Patronage: Abbey of Saint Andrew, Cleveland, Ohio, Hungary, diocese of Nitra, Slovakia, and diocese of Tarnów, Poland.
  • St. Clement of Ohrid Born: 9th century in the Thesaloniki district of modern Bulgaria. Died: 17 July 916 in Ohrid, Macedonia. Patronage: Macedonia and Ohrid, Macedonia. Also called Clement of Okbrida and Kliment Ohridski.
  • St. Colman of Stockerau Born: in the British Isles. Died: hanged in October 1012 at Stockerau, Austria. Patronage: against gout, against hanging, against plague, hanged men, horned cattle, horses, Austria, and Melk, Austria. Also called Colman of Melk and Coloman, Colomannus, Koloman, Kálmán.
  • St. Cynllo Died: 5th century. Also called Cynlio.
  • St. Ennodius of Pavia Born: c.473 in Cisalpine Gaul (an area of modern northern Italy). Died: 17 July 521. Also called Ennodio of Pavia, Magnus Felix Ennodius, and Magno Felice Ennodio.
  • St. Frédégand of Kerkelodor Born: Ireland. Died: c.740 at Deurne. Also called Fregaut, Fridigand, Frégaud.
  • St. Generosus
  • St. Gorazd
  • St. Hedwig, Queen of Poland Born: 18 February 1374 in Buda. Died: 17 July 1399. Beatified: 31 May 1979 by Pope John Paul II (cultus confirmation). Canonized: 8 June 1997 by Pope John Paul II. Patronage: queens. Also called Hedwig of Anjou, Hedwig Andegawenska, and Eduviges, Jadwiga, Jadvyga, Hedvig, Hedvigis.
  • St. Hyacinth of Amastris Died: Amastris, Paphlagonia (in modern Turkey).
  • Sts. Justina and Rufina of Seville Died: mauled by lions in 287. Patronage: potters and Seville, Spain.
  • St. Kenelm Died: killed in battle in 821 at Clent Hills near Birmingham, England. Also called Cynehelm, Chenelmo.
  • St. Marcellina Born: c.330 at Trier, Gaul (in modern Germany). Died: c.398.
  • St. Nerses Lambronazi Born: 1153. Died: 1198.
  • St. Petrus Liu Zeyu Born: c.1843 in Zhujiaxie, Shenzhou, Hebei, China. Died: beheaded on 17 July 1900 in Zhujiaxie, Shenzhou, Hebei, China. Canonized: 1 October 2000 by Pope John Paul II. Also called Peter Liu Ziyu and Baiduo.
  • St. Theodosius of Auxerre Died: 516.
  • St. Theodota of Constantinople Died: 735 in Constantinople (modern Istanbul, Turkey).
  • St. Turninus Born: Ireland. Died: 8th century.

Popes (1)

  • Pope St. Leo IV Born: Rome, Italy. Died: 855 at Rome, Italy.

Blessed (5)

  • Blessed Arnold of Himmerod Also called Arnoldus.
  • Blessed Benigno of Vallumbrosa Born: c.1136 in Montevarchi, Arezzo, Italy. Died: 17 July c.1236 in Vallumbrosa, Italy. Also called Benigno Benizzi, Benigno Visdomini, and Benignus, Bénigne.
  • Blessed Biagio of the Incarnation Died: 1612.
  • Blessed Pavol Gojdic Born: 17 July 1888 at Ruské Peklany, PreSov, Slovak Republic as Peter Gojdic. Died: 17 July 1960 in the prison hospital at Leopoldov, Hlohovec, Slovak Republic. Beatified: 4 November 2001 by Pope John Paul II. Also called Pavel Peter Gojdic and Peter Gojdic.
  • Blessed Sebastian of the Holy Spirit Died: 1721 in Lima, Peru.

Martyr groups (2)

  • Carmelite Martyrs of Compiègne Beatified: 27 May 1906 by Pope Pius X. Also called Sixteen Blessed Teresian Martyrs of Compiègne.
  • Martyrs of Scillium Died: beheaded on 17 July 180 in Scillium, Numidia (in North Africa). Also called Scillitan Martyrs and Martyrs of Scilla.

Other (3)

  • Costanza of Aragon
  • The Humility of Mary
  • Livier
