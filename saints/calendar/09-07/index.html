Sancti · September

Saints of 7 September

48 saints, blessed, and feasts kept on 7 September, with short facts.

Saints (27)

  • St. Alcmund of Hexham Died: 781. Also called Alchmund.
  • St. Augustalis Died: c.450. Also called Augustalus, Autal.
  • St. Balin Also called Balanus, Balloin. Also kept on 3 September in some calendars.
  • St. Carissima of Albi Born: Albi, France.
  • St. Chiaffredo of Saluzzo Died: near Crissolo, Italy c.270. Patronage: Crissolo, Italy and Saluzzo, Italy, city of.
  • St. Cloud Born: 522 in Gaul (modern France). Died: 560 in France. Patronage: nail makers and Saint Cloud, Minnesota, diocese of. Also called Clodoald, Clodoaldus, Claud, Clodoaldo.
  • St. Desiderio of Benevento Died: Benevento, Campania, Italy.
  • St. Dinooth Also called Dinothus, Dunawd, Dunod.
  • St. Eupsychius of Caesarea Died: c.130 at Caesarea, Cappadocia.
  • St. Eustace of Beauvais Born: Beauvais, France.
  • St. Evortius of Orléans Died: c.340. Also called Euvert, Evurtius.
  • St. Faciolus Died: c.950.
  • St. Festo of Benevento Died: Benevento, Campania, Italy.
  • St. Goscelinus of Toul Died: 962. Also called Gauzlino.
  • St. Gratus of Aosta Died: c.470 in Aosta, Italy. Patronage: against animal attacks, insectophobics; against fear of insects, against fire, against hail, against lightning, against rain, against storms, vineyards, and others.
  • St. Grimonia of Picardy Born: 4th century Irish. Died: beheaded at Picardy, France. Also called Germana.
  • St. Hiduard Died: c.750. Also called Hilduard, Hilward, Garibald, Hilduardo.
  • St. John of Lodi Born: at Lodi Vecchio, Lombardy, Italy. Died: 1106 at Gubbio, Italy.
  • St. John of Nicomedia Died: burned alive in 303 at Nicomedia.
  • St. Madalberta Died: 706.
  • St. Marko Krizevcanin Born: c.1589 in Krizevci, Koprivnicko-Krizevacka, Croatia. Died: 7 September 1619 in Kosice, Kosický kraj, Hungary (now in Slovakia). Canonized: 2 July 1995 by Pope John Paul II.
  • St. Melichar Grodecký Born: c.1584 in Ceský Tesín, Karviná, Czech Republic. Died: 7 September 1619 in Kosice, Kosický kraj, Hungary (now in Slovakia). Canonized: 2 July 1995 by Pope John Paul II. Also called Melchior Grodziecki.
  • St. Memorius of Troyes Died: beheaded in 451 outside Troyes, France. Also called Mesmin, Nemorius, Memorio, Nemorio.
  • St. Pamphilus of Capua Born: Greece. Died: c.400.
  • St. Regina Died: throat cut c.286 at Autun, (in modern France). Patronage: poor people, shepherdesses, and torture victims. Also called Regnia, Reine.
  • St. Sozonte Died: burned at the stake in Pompeiopoli, Cilicia (modern Soli, Turkey).
  • St. Tilbert of Hexham Died: 789. Also called Gilbert of Hexham.

Blessed (14)

  • Blessed Alexander of Milan Born: Milan, Italy. Died: 7 September 1505 in Chieri, Italy.
  • Blessed Berengario Bertrandi Died: 14th-century France.
  • Blessed Claude-Barnabé Laurent de Mascloux Born: 11 June 1735 in Dorat, Haute-Vienne, France. Died: 7 September 1794 aboard the prison ship Deux-Associés. Beatified: 1 October 1995 by Pope John Paul II.
  • Blessed Eugenia Picco Born: 8 November 1867 at Cresenzago, Milan, Italy. Died: 7 September 1921 at Parma, Italy. Beatified: 7 October 2001 by Pope John Paul II. Also called Anna Eugenia Picco and Maria Angela Picco.
  • Blessed Félix Gómez-Pinto Piñero Born: 18 May 1870 in La Torre de Esteban Hambrán, Toledo, Spain. Died: shot. Beatified: 28 October 2007 by Pope Benedict XVI.
  • Blessed François d'Oudinot de la Boissière Born: 3 September 1746 in Saint-Germain, Haute-Vienne, France. Died: 7 September 1794 aboard the prison ship Deux-Associés. Beatified: 1 October 1995 by Pope John Paul II.
  • Blessed Giovanni Battista Mazzucconi Born: 1 March 1826 in Rancio di Lecco, Italy. Died: 7 September 1855 in Woodlark Island, Milne Bay, Papua New Guinea. Beatified: 19 February 1984 by Pope John Paul II. Also called John Mazzucconi, Johannes Baptiste Mazzucconi, and John Baptist Mazzucconi.
  • Blessed Ignatius Klopotowski Born: 20 July 1866 in Korzeniówka, Poland. Died: 7 September 1931. Beatified: 19 June 2005 by Pope Benedict XVI. Also called Ignacy Klopotowski.
  • Blessed John Duckett Born: 1603 at Sedbergh parish, Underwinder, Yorkshire, England. Died: hanged. Beatified: 15 December 1929 by Pope Pius XI.
  • Blessed John Maki Born: in Nagasaki, Japanese. Died: burned alive on 7 September 1627 at Nagasaki, Japan. Beatified: 7 May 1867 by Pope Blessed Pius IX. Also called Ioannes Maki Jizaemon.
  • Blessed Ludovicus Maki Soetsu Born: Nagasaki, Japan. Died: burned alive on 7 September 1627 in Nagasaki, Japan. Beatified: 7 May 1867 by Pope Pius IX. Also called Louis Maki.
  • Blessed Maria of Bourbon Died: c.1445.
  • Blessed Ralph Corby Born: 25 March 1598 in Maynooth, Ireland. Died: hanged. Beatified: 15 December 1929 by Pope Pius XI. Also called Ralph Corbington.
  • Blessed Thomas Tsuji Born: c.1571 in Sonogi, Nagasaki, Japan. Died: burned at the stake on 7 September 1627 at Nagasaki, Japan. Beatified: 7 July 1867 by Pope Pius IX. Also called Thomas Tsugi, Thomas Tsughi, and Thomas Tzugi.

Martyr groups (2)

  • Martyred in the Spanish Civil War Also kept on 6 April, 20 May, 22 May, 25 July, and 92 other days in some calendars.
  • Martyrs of Noli Born: Noli, Italy. Died: Corsica, France.

Other (5)

  • Albino of Chalons
  • Chalcedony
  • Leonardo
  • Stefano di Chatillon
  • Ventura of Città di Castello
