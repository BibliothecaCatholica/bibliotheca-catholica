Saints of 11 September

38 saints, blessed, and feasts kept on 11 September, with short facts.

Marian titles (1)

  • Our Lady of Coromoto Also called Nuestra Señora de Coromoto, Virgin of Coromoto, and Virgen de Coromoto.

Saints (19)

  • St. Adelphus of Remiremont Died: c.670.
  • St. Almirus Born: Auvergne, France. Died: c.560 at Gréez-sur-Roc, France. Also called Almer, Almire.
  • St. Deiniol of Bangor Died: c.565. Also called Daniel.
  • St. Elijah Speleota Born: 863 in Reggio Calabria, Italy. Died: 11 September 960 at the Aulon monastery in Calabria, Italy. Also called Elijah Bozzetta, Elijah Espeleota, and Elia, Elias.
  • St. Emilian of Vercelli Died: 520 in Vercelli, Italy. Also called Aemilian of Vercelli.
  • St. Essuperanzio of Zurich Died: 3rd century near Zurich, Switzerland.
  • St. Felix of Zurich Died: 3rd century near Zurich, Switzerland.
  • St. Gusmeo of Gravedona sul Lario Died: church re-built in 1533 and re-named for Santi Gusmeo and Matthew. Patronage: Gravedona sul Lario, Italy.
  • St. Hyacinth of Rome Died: burned alive c.257 at Rome, Italy.
  • St. Jean-Gabriel Perboyre Born: 6 January 1802 at Le Puech. Died: lashed to a cross on a hill named the "red mountain". Canonized: 2 June 1996 by Pope John Paul II. Also called John Gabriel Perboyre.
  • St. Leudinus of Toul Born: c.625 in Bassigny, Austrasia (in modern France). Died: c.673. Also called Leudinus Bodo, Leudin, Bodon.
  • St. Matthew of Gravedona sul Lario Died: church re-built in 1533 and re-named for Santi Gusmeo and Matthew. Patronage: Gravedona sul Lario, Italy.
  • St. Paphnutius of Thebes Also called Paphnutius of Egypt, Paphnutius the Confessor, and Pafnucius....
  • St. Patiens of Lyon Died: c.491.
  • St. Protus of Rome Died: burned alive c.257 at Rome, Italy.
  • St. Regula of Zurich Died: martyred in the 3rd century near Zurich, Switzerland.
  • St. Sacerdos of Lyon Born: 487. Died: 11 September 552 at Paris, France. Also called Sardot, Serdon.
  • St. Sperandea Died: 1276. Patronage: Cingoli, Italy. Also called Sperandia.
  • St. Theodora the Penitent Born: Alexandria, Egypt. Died: 491.

Blessed (16)

  • Blessed Baldassarre Velasquez Died: with arrows in 1588.
  • Blessed Bonaventure of Barcelona Died: 11 September 1684 at the friary of Saint Bonaventure on the Palantine Hill in Rome, Italy. Beatified: 10 June 1906 by Pope Pius X. Patronage: Riudoms, Spain. Also called Bonaventure of Riudoms, Bonaventura Gran, Fra Bonaventure of Barcelona, and Miguel Baptista Gran Peris.
  • Blessed Bonincontri of San Miniato Born: latter 12th century. Died: 1230. Also called Bonincontro.
  • Blessed Charles Spinola Born: c.1565 at Madrid, Spain. Died: slowly burned to death on 10 September 1622 at Nagasaki, Japan. Beatified: 7 May 1867 by Pope Pius IX. Also called Carlo Spinola.
  • Blessed Dominic Dillon Born: Irish. Died: 11 September 1649 in Drogheda, Louth, Ireland. Beatified: 27 September 1992 by Pope John Paul II in Rome, Italy.
  • Blessed Francesco Giovanni Bonifacio Born: 7 September 1912 in Piran, Istarska, Italy (now in Croatia). Died: 11 September 1946 at Villa Gardossi, Krasica, Istarska, Italy (now in Croatia). Beatified: 4 October 2008 by Pope Benedict XVI.
  • Blessed Franciscus Takeya Born: 1610 in Nagasaki, Japan. Died: beheaded on 11 September 1622 in Nishizaka, Nagasaki, Japan. Beatified: 7 May 1867 by Pope Blessed Pius IX.
  • Blessed François Mayaudon Born: 2 May 1739 in Terrasson, Dordogne, France. Died: 11 September 1794 aboard the prison ship Deux-Associés. Beatified: 1 October 1995 by Pope John Paul II.
  • Blessed Gaspar Koteda Born: 1601 in Nagasaki, Japan. Died: beheaded on 11 September 1622 in Nishizaka, Nagasaki, Japan. Beatified: 7 May 1867 by Pope Blessed Pius IX.
  • Blessed Joan Roig i Diggle Born: 12 May 1917 in Barcelona, Spain. Beatified: 7 November 2020 by Pope Francis.
  • Blessed John Bathe Born: Irish. Died: 11 September 1649 in Drogheda, Louth, Ireland. Beatified: 27 September 1992 by Pope John Paul II in Rome, Italy.
  • Blessed José María Segura Panadés Born: 13 October 1896 in Ontinyent, Valencia, Spain. Died: shot on 11 September 1936 in Genovés, Valencia, Spain. Beatified: 11 March 2001 by Pope John Paul II.
  • Blessed Peter Taaffe Born: Irish. Died: martyred on 11 September 1649 in Drogheda, Louth, Ireland. Beatified: 27 September 1992 by Pope John Paul II in Rome, Italy.
  • Blessed Petrus Kawano Born: 1615 in Nagasaki, Japan. Died: beheaded on 11 September 1622 in Nishizaka, Nagasaki, Japan. Beatified: 7 May 1867 by Pope Blessed Pius IX.
  • Blessed Richard Overton Born: Irish. Died: martyred on 11 September 1649 in Drogheda, Louth, Ireland. Beatified: 27 September 1992 by Pope John Paul II in Rome, Italy.
  • Blessed Thomas Bathe Born: Irish. Died: martyred on 11 September 1649 in Drogheda, Louth, Ireland. Beatified: 27 September 1992 by Pope John Paul II in Rome, Italy.

Martyr groups (1)

  • Martyred in the Spanish Civil War Also kept on 6 April, 20 May, 22 May, 25 July, and 92 other days in some calendars.

Other (1)

  • Louis IV, Landgrave of Thuringia
