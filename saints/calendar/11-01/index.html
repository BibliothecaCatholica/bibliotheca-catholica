Sancti · November

Saints of 1 November

48 saints, blessed, and feasts kept on 1 November, with short facts.

Feasts (1)

  • Feast of All Saints Patronage: Arzignano, Italy.

Marian titles (1)

  • Our Lady of Heede

Saints (37)

  • St. Austremonius Born: 3rd century. Died: early 4th century. Canonized: Pre-Congregation. Patronage: archdiocese of Clermont, France. Also called Apostle of Auvergne and Austromoine, Stramonius, Stremonius.
  • St. Benignus of Dijon Born: at Smyrna. Died: clubbed to death. Patronage: Dijon, France, archdiocese of and Dijon, France, city of. Also called Benigna, Benigne, Benigny.
  • St. Cadfan Born: Brittany (in modern France). Patronage: Llangadfan, Wales. Also called Catman, Catamanu, Catamanus, Gadfan, Gideon.
  • St. Caesarius of Africa Born: African. Patronage: Terracina, Italy. Also called Caesarius of Terracina and Cesario, Cesareo.
  • St. Caesarius of Damascus Died: in Damascus, Syria.
  • St. Ceitho Patronage: Llangeitho, Wales.
  • St. Cledwyn of Wales Died: 5th century. Patronage: Llangedwyn, Wales. Also called Clydwyn of Wales.
  • St. Cyrenia of Tarsus Died: burned to death in 306 at Tarsus, Asia Minor.
  • St. Dacius of Damascus Died: in Damascus, Syria. Also called Decius of Damascus.
  • St. Dingad
  • St. Floribert of Ghent Died: c.660. Canonized: 20 April 1049 by Pope Leo IX. Also called Florbert, Floribertus, Florbertus.
  • St. Gal of Clermont
  • St. Genesius of Lyon Died: 679. Also called Genès, Genes, Genestus.
  • St. Germanus of Montfort Born: c.906 at Montfort, Brittany (in modern France). Died: 1000.
  • St. Harold the King Died: 980 in Denmark.
  • St. James of Persia Born: Persian. Died: c.344 in Persia.
  • St. Jerome Hermosilla Born: 30 September 1800 at Santo Domingo de la Calzada, Old Castile, Spain. Died: tortured and beheaded on 1 November 1861 Hai Duong, Vietnam. Canonized: 19 June 1988 by Pope John Paul II. Also called Jerónimo Hermosilla and Jerónimo Hermosilla Aransãez.
  • St. John of Persia Died: c.344.
  • St. Julian of Africa Born: African. Patronage: Terracina, Italy.
  • St. Juliana of Tarsus Died: burned to death in 306 at Tarsus, Asia Minor.
  • St. Lucinus of Angers Born: c.540 in France. Died: c.618. Also called Lesin, Lezin, Licinius.
  • St. Ludre Also called Lusor, Lusore. Also kept on 16 November in some calendars.
  • St. Magnus of Milan Died: 525. Patronage: Legnano, Italy. Also called Magno.
  • St. Marcel of Paris Born: c.396 on Île de la Cité, Paris, France. Died: November 436. Also called Marcellus.
  • St. Mary the Slave Died: c.300.
  • St. Mathurin Born: Larchant, France. Died: Rome, Italy. Patronage: against mental illness. Also called Maturinus.
  • St. Meigan Also called Megan.
  • St. Nichole
  • St. Nuño de Santa Maria Álvares Pereira Born: 24 June 1360 at Cernache do Bonjardim, Sertã, Castelo Branco, Portgual. Died: 1 November 1431 (Easter Sunday) at the Carmelite monastery at Lisbon, Portugal. Canonized: 26 April 2009 by Benedict XVI. Also called Nuño of Saint Mary, Nonius Alvares Pereira, and Nuno Álvares Pereira.
  • St. Pabiali of Wales Patronage: Partypallai, Wales.
  • St. Pere Josep Almató Ribera Auras Born: 1 November 1830 in San Feliú Saserra, Barcelona, Spain. Died: tortured and beheaded on 1 November 1861 Hai Duong, Vietnam. Canonized: 19 June 1988 by Pope John Paul II. Also called Pedro Ribera.
  • St. Peter Absalon
  • St. Rómulo of Bourges Also called Romolo.
  • St. Salaun of Leseven Died: 1358. Also called Salomon.
  • St. Severinus of Tivoli Died: 609.
  • St. Valentin Faustino Berri Ochoa Born: 14 February 1827 at Elorrio. Died: tortured and beheaded on 1 November 1861 Hai Duong, Vietnam. Canonized: 19 June 1988 by Pope John Paul II. Also called Balentin Berrio-Otxoa de Arizti, Valentin de Berriochoa, and Valentine Berrio-Ochoa.
  • St. Vigor of Bayeux Born: Artois, France. Died: 537. Also called Vigeur, Vigile, Vigorus.

Blessed (7)

  • Blessed Clemens Kyuemon Born: c.1574 in Japan. Died: burned to death on 1 November 1622 in Shimabara, Nagasaki, Japan. Beatified: 7 May 1867 by Pope Blessed Pius IX.
  • Blessed Dionysius Fugixima Born: at Aitzu, Arima, Japan. Died: burned alive on 1 November 1622 at Shimabara, Nagasaki, Japan. Beatified: 7 May 1867 by Pope Blessed Pius IX. Also called Denis Fugiscima, Dionysius Fugishima, and Dionisius Fujishima Jubyoe.
  • Blessed Peter Paul Navarro Born: 1560 at Laino Borgo, Cosenza, Italy. Died: burned alive on 1 November 1622 at Ximabara, Nagasaki, Japan. Beatified: 7 May 1867 by Pope Blessed Pius IX. Also called Paul Navarro and Pietro Paolo Navarro.
  • Blessed Petrus Onizuka Sadayu Born: Arima, Japan. Died: burned alive on 1 November 1622 at Shimabara, Nagasaki, Japan. Beatified: 7 May 1867 by Pope Blessed Pius IX. Also called Peter Onizuko.
  • Blessed Ranieri Rasini Born: c.1250 in Sansepolcro, Umbria, Italy. Died: 1 November 1304 in Sansepolcro, Umbria, Italy. Patronage: women in labour. Also called Ranieri of Aretino, Ranieri of Arezzo, Ranieri of Borgo, Ranieri of Sansepolcro, and Raniero, Ranier.
  • Blessed Rupert Mayer Born: 23 January 1876 in Stuttgart, Germany. Died: 1 November 1945 in the church of Saint Michael in Munich, Bavaria, Germany. Beatified: 3 May 1987 by Pope John Paul II in Munich, Germany. Also called The Apostle of Munich.
  • Blessed Teodor Jurij Romzha Born: 14 April 1911 at Velykyj Bychkiv, Transcarpathia, Ukraine. Died: poisoned in his hospital bed on 1 November 1947 at Mukachiv, Ukraine. Beatified: 27 June 2001 by Pope John Paul II at Ukraine.

Other (2)

  • Deborah the Prophetess Also called Deborah the Judge, Deborah the Matriarch, and Debbora.
  • Rachel the Matriarch Born: 17th-18th century BC. Died: 17th-18th century BC in childbirth.
