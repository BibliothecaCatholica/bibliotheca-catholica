Saints of 3 November

40 saints, blessed, and feasts kept on 3 November, with short facts.

Saints (32)

  • St. Acepsimas Died: 5th century in Syria.
  • St. Acheric of Vosges Died: c.860.
  • St. Caesarius Died: 250 at Caesarea, Cappadocia.
  • St. Clydog Also called Clodock, Clitaucus.
  • St. Cristiolus Born: Wales. Died: 7th century.
  • St. Domnus of Vienne Died: 657.
  • St. Elerius
  • St. Englatius Died: 966. Also called Englat, Tanglen.
  • St. Florus of Lodève Died: 389. Patronage: Saint-Flour, France, diocese of. Also called Flour of Lodève.
  • St. Gaudiosus of Tarazona Died: c.585.
  • St. Germanus Died: 250 at Caesarea, Cappadocia.
  • St. Guénhaël Born: in Brittany (in modern France). Died: c.550 at Landevennec, Brittany (in modern France). Patronage: Corbeil, France, Landevennec, France, and Vannes, France.
  • St. Hermengaudius of Urgell Died: 1035. Patronage: Urgell, Spain, diocese of. Also called Armengol, Ermengol.
  • St. Hilary of Viterbo Died: beheaded at Viterbo, Italy.
  • St. Hubert of Liege Born: c.656 at Maastricht, Netherlands. Died: 30 May 727 at Fura (modern Tervueren), Brabant, Belgium. Patronage: against dog bite, against hydrophobia, against mad dogs, against rabies, archers, dogs, forest workers, furriers, and others. Also called Apostle of the Ardennes and Hubertus of Liege.
  • St. Joannicus of Mount Olympus Born: c.754 at Bithynia (in modern Turkey). Died: 4 November 846 at Antidium. Also called Joannicius the Great, Joannicus of Olympus, Joannicus of Uludag, and Ioannikios, Ioannikos, Ioninicus.
  • St. Libertine of Agrigento Also called Libertino, Libertinus.
  • St. Malachy Omore Born: 1094 at Armagh, Ireland. Died: 2 November 1148 at Clairvaux Abbey, France. Canonized: 6 July 1190 by Pope Clement III. Patronage: Armagh, Ireland, archdiocese of, Down and Connor, Ireland, diocese of, and Ireland.
  • St. Martin de Porres Born: 9 December 1579 at Lima, Peru. Died: 3 November 1639 in Lima, Peru. Canonized: 6 May 1962 by Pope John XXIII. Also called Martín de Porres Velázquez, Martin of Charity, and Martin the Charitable.
  • St. Odrada of Alem
  • St. Papulus Died: c.300. Also called Papoul, Papulius.
  • St. Pierre-François Néron Born: 21 September 1818 in Bornay, Jura. Died: beheaded on 3 November 1860 in Son Tây, Ha Tay, Vietnam. Canonized: 19 June 1988 by Pope John Paul II. Patronage: young people in the diocese of Jura, France.
  • St. Pirmin Born: c.670 at South Aragon (part of modern Spain). Died: 3 November 753. Patronage: against poisoning, against snake bite, and Monsheim, Germany. Also called Pirminius.
  • St. Quartus Died: 1st century.
  • St. Rumwold of Buckingham Born: 662 at King Sutton, Northants, England. Died: 662 at King Sutton, Northants, England. Patronage: fishermen of Folkestone, England, Brackley, England, and Buckingham, England. Also called Rumwold of Brackley and Rumbald, Rumbold, Rumwald.
  • St. Silvia of Rome Born: c.515 in either Rome or Sicily (records vary). Died: c.592 in Rome, Italy. Patronage: pregnant women. Also called Sylvia of Rome.
  • St. Theophilus Died: 250 at Caesarea, Cappadocia.
  • St. Valentine of Viterbo Died: beheaded at Viterbo, Italy.
  • St. Valentinian Died: c.500.
  • St. Vitalis Died: 250 at Caesarea, Cappadocia.
  • St. William of Vosges Died: c.860.
  • St. Wulganus Born: British Isles (exact location varies by source). Died: c.704 at Arras, France. Patronage: Lens, France. Also called Vulgan, Vulganius, Wulgan.

Blessed (6)

  • Blessed Alphais of Cudot Born: at Cudot. Died: 1211. Beatified: 1874 by Pope Blessed Pius IX (cultus confirmed). Patronage: against sickness; sick people and disabled, handicapped and physically challenged people. Also called Alpais, Alpaida, Alpaidis.
  • Blessed Berardo dei Marsi Born: 1079 in Colli di Monte Bove, Italy. Died: 3 November 1130 in San Benedetto dei Marsi, Italy. Beatified: 10 May 1802 by Pope Pius VII (cultus confirmation). Patronage: diocese of Avezzano, Italy.
  • Blessed Berchtold of Engelberg Died: 3 November 1197. Also called Berchtold von Engelberg and Berthold.
  • Blessed Ida of Toggenburg Born: 1156. Died: 1226. Beatified: 1724 by Pope Benedict XIII (cultus confirmation). Also called Ida of Fischingen and Gutta, Idda, Iddah, Ita, Itha, Itta, Judith, Ydda.
  • Blessed Lorenzo Moreno Nicolás Born: 24 March 1899 in Lorca, Murcia, Spain. Died: 3 November 1936 in Lorca, Murcia, Spain. Beatified: 13 October 2013 by Pope Francis.
  • Blessed Simon Ballachi Born: c.1240 at Santarcangelo di Romagna, Italy. Died: 5 November 1319 in Rimini, Italy. Beatified: 1817.

Martyr groups (2)

  • Innumerable Martyrs of Saragossa Died: 304.
  • Martyred in the Spanish Civil War Also kept on 6 April, 20 May, 22 May, 25 July, and 92 other days in some calendars.
