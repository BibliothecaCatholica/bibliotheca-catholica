Saints of 7 November

35 saints, blessed, and feasts kept on 7 November, with short facts.

Saints (29)

  • St. Achillas Died: 313.
  • St. Amarand Died: c.700.
  • St. Amaranthus Died: at Vieux, France. Also called Amaranto.
  • St. Anthony of Ancyra Died: latter 4th century in Ancyra, Galatia.
  • St. Auctus of Amphipolis Died: Amphipolis, Macedonia.
  • St. Baud of Tours Also called Baldo.
  • St. Blinlivet Also called Blevileguetus.
  • St. Carina of Ancyra Died: latter 4th century in Ancyra, Galatia. Also called Cassina.
  • St. Congar Born: Wales. Also called Cungaro.
  • St. Engelbert of Cologne Born: c.1185 at Berg in modern Germany. Died: stabbed to death on the evening of 7 November 1225 near Schwelm, Germany. Canonized: no formal canonization. Also called Engelbert of Berg.
  • St. Ernest of Mecca Born: Steißlingen, Germany. Died: 1148 in Mecca. Also called Ernest of Zwiefalten.
  • St. Florentius of Strasbourg Born: Ireland. Died: c.693. Patronage: against gall stones and against ruptures. Also called Florent.
  • St. Gébétrude of Remiremont Died: c.680. Beatified: 1051 by Pope Saint Leo IX (cultus confirmation). Also called Gertrude of Remiremont.
  • St. Herculanus of Perugia Died: beheaded 549 by Ostro-Gothic soldiers. Patronage: Perugia, Italy. Also called Ercolano, Herculan.
  • St. Hesychius of Mytilene Born: Armenia. Died: c.300 at Mytilene, Greece.
  • St. Hieron of Mytilene Born: Armenia. Died: c.300 at Mytilene, Greece. Also called Gerone, Ierone.
  • St. Jacinto Castañeda Puchasóns Born: 13 November 1743 in Xàtiva, Valencia, Spain. Died: beheaded on 7 November 1773 in Ðong Mo, Ha Tay, Vietnam. Canonized: 19 June 1988 by Pope John Paul II. Also called Hyacint, Hyacinth.
  • St. Melasippus of Ancyra Died: latter 4th century in Ancyra, Galatia. Also called Melasippo.
  • St. Nicander of Mytilene Born: Armenia. Died: c.300 at Mytilene, Greece.
  • St. Peter Ou Born: 1768 at Longping in Guizhou Province, China. Died: strangled to death on 7 November 1814 at Tsen-y-Fou, Su-Tchuen province, China. Beatified: 27 May 1900 by Pope Leo XIII. Canonized: 1 October 2000 by Pope John Paul II. Also called Peter Wu Gousheng, Wu Gousheng, and Wu Guosheng Petrus.
  • St. Prosdocimus of Padua Died: c.100. Patronage: Asolo, Italy, Cittadella, Italy, and Padua, Italy. Also called Prosdecimus, Prosdocimo, Prosdozimus.
  • St. Prosdocimus of Rieti Born: 1st century. Died: Rieti, Italy.
  • St. Raverranus of Séez Died: 682.
  • St. Taurio of Amphipolis Died: Amphipolis, Macedonia. Also called Taurion.
  • St. Thessalonica of Amphipolis Died: Amphipolis, Macedonia.
  • St. Tremorus of Brittany Died: 6th century at a monastery at Carhaix, Brittany (in modern France). Patronage: Carhaix, France. Also called Trémeur.
  • St. Vincent Liêm Born: c.1732 in Trà Lu, Nam Ðinh, Vietnam. Died: beheaded on 7 November 1773 in Ðong Mo, Ha Tay, Vietnam. Canonized: 19 June 1988 by Pope John Paul II. Also called Vincent Liêm Quang Lê, Vinh-son Le Quang Liem, and Vinh-son Liêm Quang Lê.
  • St. Vincenzo Grossi Born: 9 March 1845 in Pizzighettone, Cremona, Italy. Died: 7 November 1917 in Vicobellignano, Cremona, Italy. Canonized: 18 October 2015 by Pope Francis at Rome, Italy.
  • St. Willibrord of Echternach Born: 658 at Northumbria, England. Died: 7 November 739. Patronage: against convulsions; convulsives, against epilepsy; epileptics, Belgium, Luxembourg, Netherlands, archdiocese of Utrecht, Netherlands, Heusden, Belgium, and Waalre, Netherlands.

Blessed (3)

  • Blessed Anthony Baldinucci Born: 19 June 1665 in Florence, Italy. Died: 6 November 1717. Beatified: 23 April 1893 by Pope Leo XIII.
  • Blessed Lazarus the Stylite Died: 1054 on Mount Galision near Ephesus, Asia Minor.
  • Blessed Lucia of Settefonti Died: 12th century Italy. Beatified: 1779 by Pope Pius VI (cultus confirmation).

Martyr groups (1)

  • Martyred in the Spanish Civil War Also kept on 6 April, 20 May, 22 May, 25 July, and 92 other days in some calendars.

Other (2)

  • All Dominican Saints
  • Eleanor of Portugal
