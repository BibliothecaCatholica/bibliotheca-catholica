Saints of 26 November

35 saints, blessed, and feasts kept on 26 November, with short facts.

Feasts (1)

  • Feast of Christ the King Patronage: Gaspé, Québec, diocese of, Keta-Akatsi, Ghana, diocese of, Lexington, Kentucky, diocese of, Lubbock, Texas, diocese of, Marbel, Philippines, diocese of, Moosonee, Québec, diocese of, Reyjavik, Iceland, diocese of, Aymavilles, Italy, and others.

Saints (20)

  • St. Leonard of Port Maurice Born: 20 December 1676 at Porto Maurizio, Italy on the Riviera di Ponente as Paul Jerome Casanova. Died: 11:00pm 26 November 1751 at the monastery of Saint Bonaventura, Rome, Italy. Canonized: 29 June 1867 by Pope Pius XI. Patronage: Imperia, Italy and parish missions.
  • St. Alypius Stylites Born: early 4th-century in Adrianople, Paphlagonia, Asia Minor (modern Edirne, Turkey). Died: c.390 at Adrianopolis, Paphlagonia, Asia Minor (modern Edirne, Turkey). Also called Alypius of Adrianople, Alypius of Adrianoplis, Alypius of Hadrianople, and Alypius of Hadrianopolis.
  • St. Amator of Autun
  • St. Ðaminh Nguyen Van Xuyên Born: c.1786 in Hung Lap, Nam Ðinh, Vietnam. Died: beheaded on 26 November 1839 in Bay Mau, Hanoi, Vietnam. Canonized: 19 June 1988 by Pope John Paul II. Also called Dominic Nguyen Van Xuyen.
  • St. Basolus of Verzy Born: c.555 in Limoges, France. Died: 620. Also called Basolus of Limoges and Basle of....
  • St. Bellinus of Padua Born: late 11th century in Padua, Italy. Died: stabbed by assissins 1151 on a forest road. Canonized: by Pope Eugene IV. Patronage: Adria, Italy, city of, Adria, Italy, diocese of, against dog bites, and against rabies. Also called Bellino.
  • St. Bertger of Herzfeld Died: c.830 in Herzfeld, Germany.
  • St. Conrad of Constance Died: 975. Canonized: 1123 by Pope Callistus II. Patronage: Constance, Germany, diocese of and Freiburg im Breisgau, Germany, archdiocese of. Also called Konrad of Konstanz.
  • St. Egelwine of Athelney Born: Athelney, Somersetshire, England. Also called Aylwine, Egelwin, Ethelwin, Ethelwine.
  • St. Humilis of Bisignano Born: 26 August 1582 at Bisignano, Cosanza, Italy. Died: 26 November 1637 at Bisignano, Cosanza, Italy. Canonized: 19 May 2002 by Pope John Paul II. Also called Luca Antonio Pirozzo and Umile of Bisignano.
  • St. Ida of Cologne Died: 1060.
  • St. James the Hermit Born: near Cyrus, Syria. Died: 457. Also called James the Lonely, James Hypeterius, and James the Palestinian. Also kept on 28 January in some calendars.
  • St. Magnance of Ste-Magnance Died: c.450. Also called Magnance of Auxerre and Magnentia, Magnantia, Magnence.
  • St. Marcellus of Nicomedia Died: thrown.
  • St. Martin of Arades Died: 726. Patronage: against gout and against paralysis. Also called Martin of Corbie.
  • St. Nicon of Sparta Born: Armenia. Died: 998. Also called Nicon Metanoiete ( = repent).
  • St. Sabaudus of Trier Died: c.614. Also called Sebaldus, Sebaud.
  • St. Sylvester Gozzolini Born: 1177 in Osimo, Marche, Italy. Died: 26 November 1267 at Monte Fano, Fabriano, Italy. Canonized: 1598 by Pope Clement VIII (added to the Martyrology).
  • St. Tôma Ðinh Viet Du Born: c.1783 in Phú Nhai, Nam Ðinh, Vietnam. Died: beheaded on 26 November 1839 in Bay Mau, Hanoi, Vietnam. Canonized: 19 June 1988 by Pope John Paul II.
  • St. Vacz

Popes (1)

  • Pope St. Siricius Born: c.334 at Rome, Italy. Died: 26 November 399. Canonized: by Pope Benedict XIV.

Blessed (7)

  • Blessed Albert of Haigerloch Born: 1239 in Haigerloch, Hohenzollern (Germany). Died: 26 November 1311 at Oberaltaich, Bavaria, Germany. Also called Albert of Oberaltaich and Adalbert of....
  • Blessed Delphine of Glandèves Died: c.1359. Also called Delfina.
  • Blessed Gaetana Sterni Born: 26 June 1827 at Cassola, Vicenza, Italy. Died: 26 November 1889. Beatified: 4 November 2001 by Pope John Paul II. Also called Cajetana Sterni.
  • Blessed Giacomo Alberione Born: 4 April 1884 in San Lorenzo di Fossano, Cuneo, Italy. Died: 6:26pm on 26 November 1971 in the Generalate House, Rome, Italy. Beatified: 27 April 2003 by Pope John Paul II. Also called Santiago Alberione.
  • Blessed Hugh Taylor Born: c.1559 at Durham, England. Died: hanged. Beatified: 22 November 1987 by Pope John Paul II.
  • Blessed Marmaduke Bowes Born: Ingram Grange, Yorkshire, England. Died: hanged on 26 November 1585 in York, Yorkshire, England. Beatified: 22 November 1987 by Pope John Paul II.
  • Blessed Pontius of Faucigny Born: c.1100 in Faucigny, Savoy (in modern France). Died: 26 November 1179 in Sixt, Savoy (in modern France). Beatified: 15 December 1896 by Pope Leo XIII (cultus confirmation). Also called Ponzio.
