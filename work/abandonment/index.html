Abandonment; or, Absolute Surrender to Divine Providence

Jean-Pierre de Caussade. Spiritual counsels associated with Jean-Pierre de Caussade on trusting divine providence and responding faithfully to the duties of the present moment.

By Jean-Pierre de Caussade. 1 edition, with a text you can read on this site.

about 23,000 words · about 1 hour 40 minutes of reading

About this edition

Project Gutenberg #52057: Ella McMahon’s translation, edited by H. Ramière (New York: Benziger Brothers, 1887). Thirty-three chapters in three books, divided as the hosted text heads them; the appendix of prayers stays in the reader. No words were changed.

Chapters

  1. I. The Sanctity of the Righteous of the Old Law, and of Joseph and of Mary herself, consisted in Fidelity to the Order of God
  2. II. The Duties of each Moment are the Shadows which veil the Divine Action
  3. III. How much Easier Sanctity becomes when studied from this Point of View
  4. IV. Perfection does not consist in knowing the Order of God, but in submitting to it
  5. V. Reading and other Exercises only sanctify us in so far as they are the Channels of the Divine Action
  6. VI. The Mind and other Human Means are Useful only in as far as they are the Instruments of the Divine Action
  7. VII. There is no Enduring Peace but in Submission to the Divine Action
  8. VIII. The Perfection of Souls and the Excellence of Different States are in Proportion to their Conformity to the Order of God
  9. IX. All the Riches of Grace are the Fruit of Purity of Heart and Perfect Self-abandonment
  10. I. The Divine Action is everywhere and always Present, though only Visible to the Eye of Faith
  11. II. The Divine Action is all the more Visible to the Eye of Faith when hidden under Appearances most Repugnant to the Senses
  12. III. The Divine Action offers us at each Moment Infinite Blessings, which we receive in proportion to our Faith and Love
  13. IV. God reveals Himself to us as Mysteriously, as Adorably, and with as much Reality in the most Ordinary Events as in the great Events of History and the Holy Scriptures
  14. V. The Divine Action continues in our Hearts the Revelation begun in Holy Scripture; but the Characters in which it is written will be Visible only at the Last Day
  15. VI. Divine Love is communicated to us through the Veil of Creatures, as Jesus communicates Himself to us through the Veil of the Eucharistic Species
  16. VII. The Divine Action, the Will of God, is as unworthily treated and disregarded in its Daily Manifestation by many Christians as was Jesus in the Flesh by the Jews
  17. VIII. The Revelation of the Present Moment is the more Profitable that it is addressed Directly to us
  18. IX. The Revelation of the Present Moment is an Inexhaustible Source of Sanctity
  19. X. The Present Moment is the Manifestation of the Name of God and the Coming of His Kingdom
  20. XI. The Divine Will imparts the Highest Sanctity to Souls; they have but to abandon Themselves to its Divine Action
  21. XII. The Divine Action alone can sanctify us, for it forms us after the Divine Model of our Perfection
  22. I. God Himself guides Souls who wholly abandon themselves to Him
  23. II. The more God seems to withdraw Light from the Soul abandoned to His Direction, the more Safely He guides Her
  24. III. The Afflictions with which God visits the Soul are but Loving Artifices at which she will One Day rejoice
  25. IV. The more God seems to take from a Soul wholly abandoned to Him, the more Generous He is to her
  26. V. The less Capable the Faithful Soul is of defending Herself, the more Powerfully does God defend Her
  27. VI. The Soul abandoned to the Will of God, so far from resisting her Enemies, finds in them Useful Auxiliaries
  28. VII. The Soul who abandons Herself to God has no Need to justify Herself by Words or Actions: the Divine Action justifies Her
  29. VIII. God gives Life to the Soul abandoned to Him by Means which apparently lead only to Death
  30. IX. Love holds the Place of All Things to Souls who walk in the Way of Abandonment
  31. X. The Faithful Soul finds in Submission to the Will of God more Force and Strength than the Proudest of those who resist Him
  32. XI. The Soul abandoned to God learns to recognize His Will even in the Proud who resist Him. All Creatures, whether Good or Evil, reveal Him to her
  33. XII. God assures to Faithful Souls a Glorious Victory over the Powers of Earth and Hell
