Saint Catherine of Siena as Seen in Her Letters

Catherine of Siena. A selection of Catherine's letters to her family, disciples, rulers, and popes, arranged with introductions that trace her life and her work for the Church. Catherine is a Doctor of the Church.

By Catherine of Siena. 1 edition, with a text you can read on this site.

about 98,000 words · about 7 hours 10 minutes of reading

About this edition

Project Gutenberg #7403: Vida D. Scudder’s translation and selection (1905). Sixty letters in the book’s sections, divided as the hosted text heads them, each with the editor’s note that introduces it; the introduction and tables stay in the reader. No words were changed.
