Virgin Saints and Martyrs

Sabine Baring-Gould. Lives of women saints from the early martyrs Thekla, Blandina, and Agnes to Teresa of Ávila and later foundresses, by the Anglican hagiographer.

By Sabine Baring-Gould Non-Catholic Christian · Anglican. 1 edition, with a text you can read on this site.

About this edition

Project Gutenberg #53841: Gutenberg’s title page names Thomas Y. Crowell & Co., New York, 1901; illustrations by F. Anger. Eighteen lives, divided as the hosted text heads them; each plate caption stays in the reader. No words were changed.
